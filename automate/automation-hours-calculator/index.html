Questions

How do I work out which tasks to automate first?

List every task you repeat, how many minutes it takes and how often it happens, then convert each to hours per month. The task with the most hours is usually the best first candidate, as long as it follows the same steps every time.

How are daily, weekly and monthly tasks converted to hours per month?

Daily tasks are multiplied by your working days per month (22 by default), weekly tasks by 52 ÷ 12 = 4.33, and monthly tasks by 1. Then minutes × times per month × people ÷ 60 gives hours per month.

What makes a task a good fit for automation?

It happens often, follows the same steps each time, moves information between apps (forms, email, spreadsheets, CRM) and has a clear trigger. Tasks that need judgement every time are better kept human or only partly automated.

Does the calculator save my tasks?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Your list is kept in the page address so you can copy a link to it; nothing is uploaded.

How accurate is it?

It is exact arithmetic on your estimates. Most people underestimate how long small tasks take, so timing a task once or twice gives a better number than guessing.
