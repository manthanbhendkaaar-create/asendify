Is it for you?

Who it's for, and who it's not for

Automation Blueprint is for

  • Small business owners, founders and agencies who know what they want automated but not how to build it.
  • People who want a plan to hand to a developer or automation freelancer before paying for the build.
  • No-code builders who want the right platform, tools, steps and error handling laid out for one workflow.

It's not for

  • Anyone who wants the automation built and running for them: this product is the blueprint, not the build.
  • People who need exact tool prices: the costs are estimates, and providers change their plans.
  • Projects that need a live consultation or ongoing support: it is fully automatic, with no calls.

How much does it cost?

£261 per blueprint: £180 plus a 45% service fee of £81. It is a one-time payment through PayPal, not a subscription.

How long does it take?

Usually a few minutes after payment. The page updates by itself and we email you the private link.

Who writes the blueprint?

An AI model writes it to a strict structure, and our system checks it automatically (for example that the diagram is complete and every step connects) before you see it.

Mainstream tools such as n8n, Make, Zapier, Google Sheets, Airtable, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Gmail, Outlook, Slack, Microsoft Teams, WhatsApp Business API, Twilio, Stripe, Typeform, Calendly, Notion, Shopify, Xero, QuickBooks, OpenAI and Anthropic. No. This product is the blueprint. You can build it yourself by following the steps, or hand it to any developer or automation freelancer.

No. They are estimates for the volume you describe. Tool prices and plans change, so check each provider's pricing page before you commit.
