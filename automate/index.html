Questions

What exactly do I get?

A private web page (also emailed to you) with a workflow diagram you can download as PNG or SVG, a Mermaid text copy, the recommended platform and tools for each step with reasons, numbered build instructions, the data passed between steps, error handling, what you need before you start, how to test it, the difficulty, the time to build and an estimated monthly tool cost range.

How much does it cost?

£217.50 per blueprint: £150 plus a 45% service fee of £67.50. It is a one-time payment through PayPal, not a subscription.

How long does it take?

Usually a few minutes after payment. The page updates by itself and we email you the private link.

Who writes the blueprint?

An AI model writes it to a strict structure, and our system checks it automatically (for example that the diagram is complete and every step connects) before you see it.

Which tools can it recommend?

Mainstream tools such as n8n, Make, Zapier, Google Sheets, Airtable, HubSpot, Pipedrive, Gmail, Outlook, Slack, Microsoft Teams, WhatsApp Business API, Twilio, Stripe, Typeform, Calendly, Notion, Shopify, Xero, QuickBooks, OpenAI and Anthropic. Tell us what you already use and it builds around those.

Do you build the automation for me?

No. This product is the blueprint. You can build it yourself by following the steps, or hand it to any developer or automation freelancer.

Are the cost estimates exact?

No. They are estimates for the volume you describe. Tool prices and plans change, so check each provider's pricing page before you commit.

Is my request private?

Your blueprint lives at a private link with a long random code; only people you share it with can open it. See our policies for details, including payments and refunds.
