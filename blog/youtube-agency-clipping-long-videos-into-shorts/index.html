Key takeaways

  • Clipping means cutting short, self-contained moments from long videos for Shorts and other short-form feeds.
  • YouTube Shorts can be up to three minutes long, in a square or vertical aspect ratio, for videos uploaded from 15 October 2024.
  • Shorts longer than one minute with an active copyright claim are blocked globally until the claim is resolved, so clip only content you have rights to.
  • The best clips are moments that make sense with no context; long videos' retention spikes are a good place to look.
  • One team doing both formats can pick clips using the long video's data, but you still need clear terms on ownership and volume.

These rules come from YouTube's help page on three-minute Shorts. Wider formats such as 16:9 stay long-form even if they are short.

How to choose which moments to clip

The long video's own data is the best guide. YouTube's key moments for audience retention page explains that spikes mean viewers watching, rewatching or sharing a part, and that "top moments" are where almost no one dropped off. Those are natural clip candidates: they already held attention with a long-form audience.

Good clip checklist:

  1. Stands alone. Makes sense without the rest of the video.
  2. Hooks immediately. The first line or image grabs attention.
  3. Has a payoff within the clip, not after it.
  4. Is rights-clear. Your own footage, licensed music, guests who agreed.
  5. Fits vertical framing. Faces and text stay in frame after cropping.

Questions to ask an agency about clipping

  • How many clips per long video? Get a number, and ask how they choose them.
  • Who picks the moments? Ideally someone who reads your retention graph.
  • Captions and formatting? Burned-in captions and vertical reframing should be included.
  • Access? Through YouTube's channel permissions, for example Editor (Limited), which can manage content without seeing revenue data, never your password.
  • Ownership? GOV.UK's guidance on ownership of copyright works says the creator of commissioned work owns the copyright unless agreed otherwise in writing. Put ownership of all edits and clips in the contract.
  • Guests and music? Who checks permissions and music licences before posting?

Doing it yourself first

If the agency you want is full, you can start alone:

  1. Wait two days after a long upload, then open its retention graph.
  2. Note the two biggest spikes and any top moments.
  3. Cut 30-90 second clips around them, starting on the strongest line.
  4. Reframe vertically and add captions.
  5. Check every clip for music and guest permissions before posting.

Common mistakes

  • Clipping the intro. The first minute of a long video rarely works as a Short.
  • No hook in the first seconds. Short-form viewers scroll fast.
  • Posting clips with claimed music. Over a minute, an active claim blocks the Short worldwide.
  • Too many clips from one moment. Variety tests more ideas.
  • Never linking back. Point viewers to the full video where it makes sense.
