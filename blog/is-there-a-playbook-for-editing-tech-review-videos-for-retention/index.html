What a tech review needs from a retention method

A review has a predictable shape: first look, specs, tests, comparison, verdict. Retention problems show up in the same places: slow openings, spec lists, repeated test footage and an unclear end. A retention method should help with the opening, the pacing of the spec section and the structure of the verdict.
