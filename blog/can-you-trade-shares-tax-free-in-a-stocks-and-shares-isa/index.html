Can you trade shares tax-free in a Stocks and Shares ISA?

By Manthan Bhendkar · 6 min read · Published 20 November 2026 · Last updated 20 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

Yes. You can buy and sell shares, funds and bonds inside a Stocks and Shares ISA without paying Capital Gains Tax on profits or tax on dividends. The limit is on what you pay in: up to £20,000 a year across all your ISAs in 2026 to 2027. You cannot move shares you already own into an ISA (except from employee share schemes), and leveraged products such as CFDs and spread bets are not on the list of ISA investments.

This page is education only, not financial or tax advice. Investments can fall as well as rise.

Key takeaways

  • The ISA allowance for 2026 to 2027 is £20,000, across cash, stocks and shares, innovative finance and Lifetime ISAs.
  • A Stocks and Shares ISA can hold company shares, unit trusts and investment funds, corporate and government bonds, and long-term asset funds.
  • HMRC: "You do not pay tax on dividends from shares in an ISA."
  • Outside an ISA, the Capital Gains Tax allowance is £3,000 and the dividend allowance £500; dividend tax rates for 2026 to 2027 are 10.75%, 35.75% and 39.35%.
  • Crypto exchange traded notes cannot be held in a Stocks and Shares ISA unless held there before 6 April 2026.

How the ISA rules work

GOV.UK's guide to Individual Savings Accounts sets the basics: you must be 18 or over and UK resident, you cannot hold an ISA jointly, and the most you can pay into ISAs in the 2026 to 2027 tax year is £20,000. The tax year runs from 6 April to 5 April.

GOV.UK's page on how ISAs work lists what a Stocks and Shares ISA can hold:

The allowance counts money paid in, not trading activity. Once inside, you can buy and sell as often as your provider allows, and gains stay in the ISA.

GOV.UK's Capital Gains Tax allowances page gives the annual tax-free allowance as £3,000, and the tax on dividends page gives the £500 dividend allowance and the 2026 to 2027 rates. Because gains in an ISA are not taxed, losses inside an ISA generally cannot be used to reduce taxable gains elsewhere.

What about active trading?

An ISA is built for buying and selling listed investments, so frequent share trading is possible with many providers, but:

  • No leverage. CFDs and spread bets are separate products outside the ISA list; our page on whether you can lose more than you deposit covers those.
  • Costs still apply. Dealing fees, spreads and FX charges reduce returns inside an ISA as much as outside; see what slippage is.
  • The allowance does not refill when you sell. Selling inside the ISA and reinvesting is fine; withdrawing and paying back in may use new allowance, depending on whether the ISA is "flexible".
  • Tax-free does not mean profitable. Research on retail traders, summarised in our page on whether people who trade more earn lower returns, shows frequent trading often hurts results.

Sam would need to have paid the money in within his £20,000 allowance, and could not have moved existing shares in. If he loses money instead, the ISA offers no tax relief on the loss, which is one more reason to keep a written trading plan; see how to build a trading plan.

Before you open one

  • Check who can open it: 18 or over, UK resident, and no joint ISAs.
  • Count every ISA you pay into this tax year, including cash and Lifetime ISAs, against the single £20,000 limit.
  • Compare provider costs for the way you trade: frequent dealers pay far more in fees than buy-and-hold investors.
  • Keep your own records of every trade, so you can judge your results honestly even though there is no tax to report.

Do I pay tax on share profits in an ISA?

No. Gains and dividends inside an ISA are tax-free.

How much can I put in a Stocks and Shares ISA?

Up to £20,000 in 2026 to 2027, across all your ISAs.

Can I day trade in an ISA?

You can buy and sell listed shares and funds, subject to your provider's rules, but you cannot use leveraged products such as CFDs or spread bets.

Can I move my existing shares into an ISA?

Not directly, except shares from an employee share scheme. Shares bought outside an ISA cannot be transferred in.

Can I hold crypto in an ISA?

Crypto exchange traded notes cannot be held in a Stocks and Shares ISA unless they were already there before 6 April 2026.
