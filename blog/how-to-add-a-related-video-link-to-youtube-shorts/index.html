How do you link a YouTube Short to a long video, and does it help?

By Manthan Bhendkar · 5 min read · Published 22 November 2026 · Last updated 22 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

To link a YouTube Short to a long video, open YouTube Studio, go to Content, select the Short, choose a Related video from your channel and save. Viewers then see a clickable link below your channel handle in the Shorts player. You need advanced feature access, and the linked video must be public or unlisted. It helps most when the Short is a genuine taster of the long video, so the viewer who taps already wants what comes next.

Key takeaways

  • YouTube Help says "Adding a related video is only available with advanced feature access", and the video "has to be public or unlisted."
  • You can link videos, Shorts or live streams from your own channel, and viewers see "a clickable link below your channel handle."
  • If you make a Short from a long video inside YouTube Studio, the source video is added as the related video automatically, unless the Short is private, unlisted or made for kids (YouTube Help).
  • A related video link turns a Short into a doorway; the Short still has to make people want to walk through it.
  • Check results with YouTube Studio's traffic source reports rather than guessing.

Step by step

StepWhat to do
1Sign in to YouTube Studio
2Select Content in the left menu
3Open the Short you want to edit
4Under Related video, pick a video from your channel
5Save

If you cannot see the option, check that your channel has advanced features enabled. YouTube usually asks for phone verification or similar steps to unlock them.

When a related video link helps

The link works when the Short and the long video share one promise.

  • Teaser Shorts. A Short showing the result of a test, linking to the full test.
  • Cut-downs. The strongest 30 seconds of a long video, linking to the rest.
  • Questions. A Short that asks a question the long video answers.
  • Series. Part one of a story in the Short, with the full story in the long video.

It works less well when the Short is a joke or trend clip that has nothing to do with the linked video. The viewer who enjoyed a meme is not necessarily looking for your 20-minute tutorial.

How to edit a Short so people tap the link

End on an open question, not a full answer

If the Short answers everything, there is no reason to tap. Show the problem and the start of the solution, then stop. The long video delivers the rest.

Keep the promise identical

If the Short says "this setting doubled my battery life", the linked video should open on that setting, not on a five-minute intro. Viewers who arrive from a Short and do not see the promised thing fast tend to leave. Our page on why viewers leave in the first 30 seconds covers the landing side.

Point to the link in the edit

A short on-screen arrow or a spoken line ("full test linked below my name") can help, as long as it does not interrupt the content.

Do not rely on the loop

A Short that loops perfectly keeps people in the Shorts feed. If your goal is the long video, a clear end with a reason to tap may serve you better than a seamless loop.

Example

A tech creator has a 22-minute laptop review and wants more people to find it.

  1. Using YouTube Studio's Create clip or Short tool, they cut a 40-second Short from the battery test section.
  2. Because the Short was made from the long video, YouTube adds the review as the related video automatically.
  3. They re-edit the Short to end on the question "but here's what happened at hour nine", without the answer.
  4. They check the long video's opening: it starts with a 90-second intro. They trim it to 15 seconds so Shorts viewers see the laptop quickly.
  5. After two weeks, they check traffic sources in YouTube Studio to see how many views came from the Shorts link, and compare those viewers' retention with other sources.

CardsPop-up during a long videoPointing to a related video mid-way
Pinned commentTop of the commentsExtra context, not a tap target for most viewers

The related video link is the only one of these built for the Shorts player, which is why it is worth setting on every Short that has a natural long-form follow-up.

Tips and limits

  • One link per Short. Choose the video the Short leads to most naturally.
  • Your own channel only. The help page steps refer to videos from your channel.
  • Made for kids Shorts. Shorts made from long videos do not get the automatic link if they are made for kids.
  • Unlisted can work. You can link an unlisted video, for example bonus material for people who come from the Short.
  • Update old Shorts. If an old Short is still getting views, link it to your newest relevant video.

Why can't I add a related video to my Short?

YouTube says the feature needs advanced feature access, and the linked video must be public or unlisted and follow Community Guidelines.

Can I link another creator's video?

YouTube's steps refer to selecting a video from your channel, so plan on linking your own content.

Where does the link appear?

Below your channel handle in the Shorts player.

Can I link a live stream?

Yes. YouTube says you can showcase videos, Shorts and live streams.

Does a related video link hurt Shorts performance?

YouTube has not said it does. Judge by your own analytics, comparing Shorts with and without links.
