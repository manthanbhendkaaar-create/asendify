What size should a YouTube profile picture be?

By the Asendify editorial team, led by Manthan Bhendkar · 5 min read · Published 21 November 2026 · Last updated 21 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

Upload your YouTube profile picture as a square image, ideally 800 x 800 pixels, as a JPG, PNG, BMP or non-animated GIF under 15 MB. YouTube renders it at 98 x 98 pixels and crops it to a circle, so keep the face or logo centred and simple enough to recognise at thumbnail size. Change it in YouTube Studio under Customization > Profile; it may take a few minutes to update everywhere.

Key takeaways

  • YouTube Help: use a "JPG, GIF, BMP, or PNG file (no animated GIFs)" that does "not exceed 15 MB", rendering at 98 x 98 px.
  • Adobe recommends 800 x 800 px (1:1); the image "displays as a circle throughout the site."
  • Your profile picture appears next to every video, comment and post, often tiny, so clarity beats detail.
  • Keep text out of it: at 98 px or smaller, words become unreadable.
  • Changes publish from YouTube Studio > Customization > Profile and can take a few minutes to show.

Design tips for a tiny circle

DoAvoid
A clear face, close-cropped, or a simple logo markFull-body shots or group photos
Strong contrast with a plain backgroundBusy backgrounds that blur at small sizes
Keep the subject in the centre so the circle crop doesn't cut itImportant details in the corners
Use the same image across platforms for recognitionChanging it often
Check it at small size before publishingText, slogans or long names

A quick test: shrink your image to the size of a fingernail on your phone screen. If you can still tell what it is, it works.

Photo or logo?

Which works better depends on what your channel is about. If you are the face of the videos, talking to camera, a clear photo of your face helps viewers connect the person in the thumbnail with the person in the comments. If the channel is about a topic rather than a presenter, such as gameplay, animation, recipes filmed from above or a business, a simple logo is usually clearer and easier to keep consistent. Whichever you choose, use the same image on your other social profiles so people who find you elsewhere recognise you instantly on YouTube.

Why it matters

Your profile picture sits beside your channel name on every video in search and recommendations, every comment you leave and every post. It's a small but constant part of your packaging, alongside your thumbnails, handle and channel name. YouTube's channel profile page also notes that changing your channel name removes a verification badge and can take a few days to appear, which is another reason to settle your branding early and keep it consistent.

How to change it, step by step

  1. Sign in to YouTube Studio.
  2. Choose Customization, then Profile.
  3. Under Picture, click Change.
  4. Choose an image (or one of YouTube's illustrations), adjust the crop, click Done.
  5. Click Publish.

Yes. Asendify's free YouTube Channel Roast scores your latest public uploads out of 100 and gives three fixes, with no sign-up. YouTube Studio analytics are a fair free alternative.

What is the best YouTube profile picture size?

800 x 800 px square is a safe choice; YouTube displays it at 98 x 98 px as a circle.

Can my profile picture be a GIF?

You can upload a GIF, but not an animated one.

What's the maximum file size?

15 MB, according to YouTube Help.

Why is my profile picture blurry?

It may have been uploaded too small or not square. Use a sharp 800 x 800 px image.

YouTube says it may take a few minutes to update across the site.
