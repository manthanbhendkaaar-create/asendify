What size should a YouTube banner be?

By Manthan Bhendkar · 5 min read · Published 20 November 2026 · Last updated 20 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

A YouTube banner should be at least 2,048 x 1,152 pixels in a 16:9 ratio, and YouTube recommends 2,560 x 1,440 pixels for the best results on TVs. Keep the file under 6 MB, and put all text and logos inside the central safe area of 1,235 x 338 pixels, because the rest gets cropped on phones and desktops. Profile pictures display at 98 x 98 pixels, so they need to be simple.

Key takeaways

  • Minimum banner upload: 2,048 x 1,152 px at 16:9; recommended: 2,560 x 1,440 px; maximum file size 6 MB.
  • Safe area for text and logos at the minimum size: 1,235 x 338 px. YouTube warns other parts "will get cropped on certain views and devices."
  • Profile pictures render at 98 x 98 px, can be JPG, GIF (not animated), BMP or PNG, and up to 15 MB.
  • Video watermarks: at least 150 x 150 px, square, under 1 MB.
  • Don't add borders, shadows or frames to the banner file.

YouTube's official image sizes

YouTube's help page on managing your channel branding gives the specifications:

YouTube also says: "Do not include any additional file embellishments (e.g. shadows, borders or frames)."

Why the safe area matters

The banner appears at very different shapes depending on the device. On a TV you may see most of the 16:9 image; on a phone you see a thin horizontal strip. YouTube warns that parts of the banner "will get cropped on certain views and devices." The 1,235 x 338 px safe area in the middle of a 2,048 x 1,152 px image is the part visible everywhere.

Area of the bannerWhat to put there
Central safe area (1,235 x 338 px at minimum size)Channel name, one-line promise, upload schedule, key face or logo
Wider strip either sideBackground colour or texture that can be cropped
Top and bottom of the full 16:9 imageBackground only; seen mainly on TVs

If you design at 2,560 x 1,440 px, scale the safe area by the same factor, about 1.25 times, to roughly 1,544 x 423 px in the centre.

What to put on your banner

Your banner sits right under your channel name, so it should help a new visitor decide in a second whether to subscribe:

  • Who it's for and what they get, in under eight words.
  • Upload schedule, if you keep one.
  • A face or logo that matches your profile picture and thumbnails.
  • High contrast so the text survives on small screens.

Your profile picture works the same way at a much smaller size: at 98 x 98 pixels, fine detail and small text disappear. A clear face or a bold simple logo works best.

Banner, trailer and the rest of the channel page

The banner is one part of your channel's first impression. YouTube's help page on customising your channel layout explains that you can also set a channel trailer, which "offers a preview of your channel so that viewers can learn more and subscribe", plus a featured video for returning subscribers and up to 12 sections on your Home tab. The manage your channel page covers your channel name, handle, description and links.

Worked example: fixing a cropped banner

Example: Jamal runs a cycling channel. His 2,048 x 1,152 px banner has his channel name in the top-left corner and "New videos every Sunday" in the bottom-right.

ProblemFix
On phones, both text blocks are cropped outMove channel name and schedule into the central 1,235 x 338 px area
Thin white border added in his design appRemove it, as YouTube advises against borders
Profile picture is a wide logo with small textReplace with a bold square icon readable at 98 x 98 px
File exported at 9 MBRe-export under 6 MB

He redesigns at 2,560 x 1,440 px, keeps the text within the scaled safe area, and checks the result on his phone, a laptop and the TV app before publishing. The banner now shows "Cycling for beginners · New videos every Sunday" on every screen.

What is the best size for a YouTube banner?

2,560 x 1,440 pixels, YouTube's recommended size, with a minimum of 2,048 x 1,152 and a maximum file size of 6 MB.

What is the YouTube banner safe area?

1,235 x 338 pixels in the centre of a 2,048 x 1,152 banner. Text and logos outside it may be cropped.

What size is a YouTube profile picture?

It renders at 98 x 98 pixels. Upload a JPG, PNG, BMP or non-animated GIF up to 15 MB.

Why is my YouTube banner blurry?

Usually because it was uploaded below the minimum size or compressed too much. Use at least 2,048 x 1,152 px and keep text in the safe area.

What size is a YouTube watermark?

At least 150 x 150 pixels, square, and under 1 MB.
