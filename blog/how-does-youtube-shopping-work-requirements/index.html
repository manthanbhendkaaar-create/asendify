How does YouTube Shopping work, and what are the requirements?

By Manthan Bhendkar · 5 min read · Published 21 November 2026 · Last updated 21 November 2026

YouTube Shopping lets eligible creators connect their own store to show products under their videos, or tag products from other brands, in long videos, Shorts and live streams. To promote products, YouTube lists 500 subscribers, 3 valid public uploads in the last 90 days, and either 3,000 qualified watch hours in the last 12 months or 3 million qualified Shorts views in the last 90 days, plus other conditions. The affiliate programme for other brands' products is available in the UK and US among other countries.

Key takeaways

  • YouTube Help says YouTube Shopping lets eligible creators connect a store to feature their own products, tag products from other brands and check Shopping analytics.
  • Products can appear in your channel's store, in the video description, in a product shelf below your content and through the Shopping button on your content.
  • YouTube's monetisation overview lists the Shopping threshold: 500 subscribers with 3 valid public uploads in the last 90 days and 3,000 watch hours in 12 months or 3 million Shorts views in 90 days; music channels and Made for Kids channels are excluded.
  • The YouTube Shopping affiliate programme is available to eligible creators in countries including the United Kingdom and United States; it requires YPP membership and the YPP subscriber threshold, and pays commission when fans buy on the retailer's site.
  • Tagged products from brands you are paid by still need proper disclosure, under YouTube's paid promotion rules.

Who is not eligible

According to YouTube's overview, the Shopping features are not available to channels that are music channels, Official Artist Channels or associated with music partners for the own-products route, or to channels set as Made for Kids or with a significant number of videos set as made for kids. Countries also matter: products are shown based on the countries of sale the store sets in Google Merchant Center.

Shopping and retention

Shopping works best when the product fits the story. A mixer tagged during a bake feels useful; a sudden list of unrelated products interrupts the video and can cause viewers to leave. Plan product moments into the script, show the product in use, and keep the main promise of the video at the centre.

Shopping vs other ways to earn on YouTube

Shopping is one of several income streams. It suits some channels far better than others.

Many creators combine two or three. The common thread is retention: every income stream works better when more viewers stay to the moment the product, sponsor or call to action appears.

Common problems

  • Products not showing: check the store is connected and countries of sale are set in Google Merchant Center.
  • Not eligible: review the subscriber, upload and watch-time thresholds, and whether the channel is set as Made for Kids.
  • Affiliate programme not visible: YouTube says filling in its interest form does not guarantee an invitation.

Do I need to be in the Partner Program for YouTube Shopping?

The affiliate programme requires YPP membership. The own-products route has its own threshold of 500 subscribers plus uploads and watch time or Shorts views.

Is the YouTube Shopping affiliate programme available in the UK?

Yes. YouTube lists the United Kingdom among eligible countries.

Can I tag products in Shorts?

Yes. YouTube says tagged products can appear in videos, Shorts and live streams.

Do I have to disclose tagged products?

If you are paid or earn commission, disclose it clearly, following YouTube's paid promotion rules and UK guidance.
