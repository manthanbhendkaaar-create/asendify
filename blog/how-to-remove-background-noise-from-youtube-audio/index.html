How do you remove background noise from audio in a YouTube video?

By Manthan Bhendkar · 6 min read · Published 20 November 2026 · Last updated 20 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

To remove background noise from a YouTube video, capture a few seconds of the noise on its own, use a noise reduction effect that learns from that sample, and apply it gently, because heavy settings create watery artifacts. Free Audacity does this well; Premiere Pro and DaVinci Resolve Studio add AI tools that separate speech from noise. Recording in a quieter spot with the mic closer always beats fixing it later.

Key takeaways

  • Noise reduction works best on steady noise (hum, hiss, fans, air conditioning) and needs a sample of noise on its own. Audacity's manual says "A longer Noise Profile is better."
  • Audacity's defaults are 6 dB reduction, sensitivity 6 and frequency smoothing 6; too aggressive and "random artifacts may occur, which can be even more objectionable than the original noise."
  • A noise gate is different: it turns down sound below a threshold, which cleans the gaps between sentences but not noise under your voice.
  • Blackmagic announced AI voice isolation in DaVinci Resolve Studio 18.1 to "isolate dialog from background sounds"; Adobe's Premiere Pro guide describes AI that removes noise from dialogue clips.
  • Fix clicks and hum first: Audacity advises doing notch filtering or click removal before noise reduction.

Method 1: Noise reduction in Audacity (free)

Audacity's manual page on Noise Reduction gives a two-step process:

  1. Get a noise profile. Select a section that contains only the noise, open Effect > Noise Reduction and click Get Noise Profile. The manual says the profile needs at least 2,048 samples (about 0.05 seconds at 44,100 Hz), but "A longer Noise Profile is better", and that duplicating a tiny sample to make it longer "will not help."
  2. Reduce the noise. Select the whole clip, set the controls, press Preview to listen, then apply.
SettingDefaultWhat it doesTip
Noise reduction (dB)6How much the identified noise is turned downRaise slowly; listen for a "watery" voice
Sensitivity6.00 (scale 0 to 24)How much of the audio counts as noiseUse the lowest value that removes the noise well
Frequency smoothing (bands)6Smooths the reduction across frequencies0 turns it off

The manual is honest about limits: "Reducing noise usually results in some distortion. This is normal", and artifacts can happen "if the noise floor is higher than was estimated." It also says to do notch filtering or click removal before noise reduction. The profile isn't saved when you close Audacity, so finish the job in one session or capture it again.

Method 2: Add a noise gate for the gaps

Audacity's Noise Gate is a dynamics effect that reduces "the level of sound below a specified threshold level". It is useful to "cut the level of residual noise between sections of a recording." Key controls are the gate threshold, level reduction (at -100 dB "the gate stops the sound completely when closed"), attack, hold and decay. Longer decay times close the gate more gradually, which sounds more natural.

A gate won't remove noise underneath your speech, so use it after noise reduction, and don't set level reduction so deep that the silence between sentences sounds unnaturally dead.

AI tools can over-process too: a voice can start to sound thin or synthetic. Blend or reduce the effect if your editor lets you, and always compare before and after on headphones. Our page on whether free DaVinci Resolve is good enough for YouTube covers what the free version includes.

Prevention beats repair

Every tool above removes some of your voice along with the noise. The cleanest fix happens before you press record:

  1. Turn off fans, air conditioning and the fridge if you can, and silence phones.
  2. Move the microphone closer to your mouth; your voice gets louder relative to the room.
  3. Record in a room with soft furnishings, not a bare kitchen.
  4. Record 10 seconds of silence at the start of every take; it's your noise profile.
  5. Monitor on headphones while recording.

Worked example: cleaning a desk-fan talking-head video

Example: Lena records a 9-minute tutorial with a lapel mic. Playback reveals a steady fan hiss and a fridge hum, plus a car passing once.

StepTool and settingResult
1Exported audio to Audacity; selected 8 seconds of silence from the start of the takeGood noise profile, much longer than the minimum

Total time: about 15 minutes. The bigger lesson for her next video was step zero: switch the fan off. Since clear audio also matters for captions, see whether to add captions to YouTube videos.

FAQ

Is there a self-paced course that teaches retention editing to creators who edit their own videos?

Yes. Asendify's Retention Editing Accelerator is a self-paced course with assignments on your own footage. Free software manuals are a fair alternative for audio basics.

What is the best free way to remove background noise?

Audacity's Noise Reduction effect, using a noise profile from a few seconds of noise on its own and gentle settings.

Why does my voice sound robotic after noise reduction?

The settings are too strong. Lower the reduction and sensitivity; Audacity warns that artifacts can be worse than the original noise.

Can I remove noise in DaVinci Resolve for free?

Blackmagic announced AI voice isolation as a DaVinci Resolve Studio feature, which is the paid version. Check the current feature list for what the free version includes.

Should I use a noise gate or noise reduction?

Noise reduction treats noise throughout the clip; a gate only quietens the gaps. Many editors use reduction first, then a gentle gate.

Can noise be removed completely?

Rarely without affecting the voice. Audacity's manual says some distortion is normal, so prevention while recording matters most.
