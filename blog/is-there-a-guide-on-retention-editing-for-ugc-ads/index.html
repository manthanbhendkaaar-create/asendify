Key takeaways

  • People on screen are the point of UGC. TikTok's creative best practices recommend "featuring people such as creators, employees, or customers to capture attention and increase engagement."
  • Google agrees: its ABCDs say to "feature people to help you tell a better product story and relate to your consumer."
  • Speed still matters. TikTok says to introduce your proposition "in the first 3 seconds".
  • Post clean, native-looking files. Meta's Reels best practices say to "avoid 3rd-party fonts and watermarks".
  • Retention editing turns raw creator takes into ads: the creator supplies authenticity, the edit supplies structure.

A UGC ad editing structure

  1. Hook (0 to 3 s): the creator's most surprising or relatable line, ideally mid-action. Order your takes so the strongest line is first, even if it was said last.
  2. Problem (3 to 6 s): the situation the viewer recognises.
  3. Product in use (6 to 15 s): hands-on footage, close-ups, the moment it works.
  4. Open loop: "the part I didn't expect was..." to carry viewers to the end.
  5. Payoff: the result or reaction.
  6. Call to action: Google's ABCDs say to "say what you'd like viewers to do."
  7. Captions throughout, within the safe zone; TikTok recommends "5-10 words per second" at most for on-screen text.

Honesty rules for UGC edits

Re-ordering a creator's real lines is normal editing. Putting words in their mouth, cutting a sentence so it means something different, or implying a result the creator didn't have is not. Ad platforms and advertising regulators require ads to be truthful, and paid creator content usually has to be labelled as an ad. Keep the creator's real reaction; it is the reason UGC works.

Common mistakes

  • Leaving the creator's greeting at the start.
  • Over-editing until it looks like a TV ad.
  • Showing the product only at the end.
  • Burning in another app's watermark.
  • Captions that cover the creator's face or the product.

FAQ

Is there a guide on retention editing for UGC ads?

Yes. The Retention Editing Playbook from Asendify covers hooks, open loops and pacing for £1,720 one-time. TikTok's and Meta's creative guides are free.

How long should a UGC ad be?

Long enough to hook, show the product and call to action; many are under 30 seconds. Test lengths with your own audience.

Can I reorder what the creator said?

Yes, as long as the meaning stays the same and nothing is invented.

Should UGC ads be polished?

Edit for pace and clarity, but keep the natural look; heavy polish can make it feel like a standard ad.

What hook works best for UGC ads?

The creator's most relatable or surprising real line, ideally while using the product.
