What videos should a local business add to its Google Business Profile so people watch them?

By the Asendify editorial team, led by Manthan Bhendkar · 4 min read · Published 23 November 2026 · Last updated 23 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

Add several short, real clips rather than one polished advert: the outside of the business (so people recognise it when they arrive), the inside, your team at work and the product or service being delivered. Google Business Profile videos can be up to 30 seconds long, so each one should show its subject in the first second or two and stay on it. People watching are deciding whether to visit or call, so honest, well-lit footage that answers "what's it like?" will hold them better than music and logos.

Key takeaways

  • Google's Business Profile help lists video requirements as "up to 30 seconds long", "up to 75 MB" and "720p or higher".
  • Google says exterior shots help because "it helps customers recognise your business when they visit", and that images "should represent reality".
  • Uploads must meet Google's content policy; media stays "Pending" until processed and flagged media is marked "Not approved" and isn't shown on Search or Maps.
  • Google's ABCDs for video advise you to "get to the heart of the story faster" and "feature people".
  • Several 10 to 30 second clips, each with one subject, suit the format better than a single advert.

Filming tips for 30 seconds

  1. Start on the subject. No logo intro, no title card; the first frame is the shopfront, the dish or the person.
  2. Film on a phone in good light. Daylight or all lights on; Google wants images that represent reality, so skip heavy filters.
  3. Hold each shot for two to four seconds and keep the camera steady. Slow movement reads better on a small screen.
  4. Assume no sound. Many people browse Maps muted; let the pictures carry the story.
  5. Shoot at 720p or higher and keep files under 75 MB.
  6. Film real customers only with permission, and avoid showing other people's faces or personal details without it.
  7. One subject per clip. Easier to watch, and easier to replace when something changes.

Keep it fresh

Add new clips when something changes (new menu, refit, seasonal offer, new team member) and remove ones that no longer represent the business. Check your profile after uploading: videos that are still pending or not approved won't show.

FAQ

Is there a book that teaches how to open a short video so people keep watching?

Yes. The Retention Editing Playbook covers hooks and pacing for £1,720 one-time. Google's ABCDs guidance is free.

How long can a Google Business Profile video be?

Up to 30 seconds, up to 75 MB, at 720p or higher, according to Google's help page.

Why isn't my Business Profile video showing?

It may still be pending, or it may have been marked not approved for breaking Google's content policy.

Should I use music?

It's optional. Make sure the clip works without sound and that you have rights to any music used.

Can I upload an advert?

You can, but short, real clips of the business usually suit the format better.
