What is voluntary termination, and can I hand back a supercar on PCP or HP?

By Manthan Bhendkar · 6 min read · Published 20 November 2026 · Last updated 20 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

Voluntary termination is your legal right to end a regulated hire purchase (HP) or PCP agreement early and hand the car back. Under section 99 of the Consumer Credit Act 1974 you can do it at any time before the final payment, and under section 100 you owe nothing more once you have paid half the total price, as long as you have taken reasonable care of the car. If you have paid less, you pay the difference up to half.

This page is general information, not financial or legal advice. Check your agreement and speak to your finance company or an adviser.

Key takeaways

  • Section 99: you can end a regulated HP or conditional sale agreement "at any time before the final payment" by giving notice to the finance company.
  • Section 100: you owe the amount needed to bring what you have paid up to half the total price, or less if the agreement says so.
  • The "total price" includes the balloon payment on PCP, so the halfway point can be a lot of money on an expensive car.
  • If you have not taken reasonable care of the car, the finance company can add compensation for damage.
  • Separately, once you have paid a third of the total price, the lender needs a court order to repossess if you fall behind.

The law in brief

RuleWhereWhat it says
Right to terminateConsumer Credit Act 1974, section 99End a regulated HP or conditional sale agreement at any time before the final payment, by notice to the finance company
What you oweSection 100Top up payments to half the total price (or a lower amount in the agreement); more if you broke the duty to take reasonable care
Repossession protectionSection 90After a third of the total price is paid, the lender can only take the car back with a court order

Termination does not wipe out arrears that were already due before you ended the agreement. A court can order a smaller sum than the half if it is satisfied the lender's actual loss is lower, and if you wrongly keep the car after terminating, the court will normally order it returned.

Most PCP deals are set up as conditional sale or hire purchase, which is why the right applies to them. Personal leases (personal contract hire) are rental agreements, not HP, so this right does not apply; check the early termination terms in a lease contract instead.

How the 50% rule works on a PCP

On PCP, the total price usually includes your deposit, all monthly payments, the final "balloon" payment and any fees. Because the balloon is often large on a supercar, half the total price can be well above what you would pay before the balloon. That is the main reason voluntary termination can be expensive early in a PCP deal.

Because the balloon is part of the total price, Ellie never reaches half through monthly payments alone. Near the end, she would need to pay £2,950 to terminate, or she can simply hand the car back at the end as a normal PCP allows. On an HP deal with no balloon, the halfway point comes about halfway through the payments. Our page on PCP vs HP vs lease vs cash for a supercar compares how the deals are structured.

Before you terminate

  • Ask for a settlement figure too. Paying off the finance and selling the car yourself can work out better if the car is worth more than you owe.
  • Check the car's condition. Damage beyond fair wear can be charged under the reasonable care rule; get it inspected and repaired if worthwhile.
  • Put it in writing. Give written notice to the finance company and keep a copy.
  • Check excess mileage terms. Your agreement explains what applies when a car is returned.
  • Keep paying until it is settled. Missed payments before termination remain due.

Citizens Advice's guide to buying a used car reminds buyers that on HP you do not own the car until the final payment, a deposit of about 10% is usually needed, and the car can be repossessed if you fall behind. Section 90 adds protection once a third is paid: the lender then needs a court order.

Why this matters for planning a supercar

Voluntary termination is a safety net, not a plan. Depreciation and large balloon payments can mean you stay in negative equity for most of the deal. If you are also worried about the car being written off while you owe more than it is worth, see whether you need GAP insurance on a supercar and what happens if your supercar is written off.

Can I hand back a car on PCP?

Yes, if the agreement is regulated HP or conditional sale. Once you have paid half the total price, and the car has been looked after, you owe nothing more.

Does voluntary termination apply to leases?

No. Personal contract hire is a rental agreement, so check its early termination terms.

Does the balloon payment count towards the half?

The total price includes the balloon, so it raises the halfway point, even though you have not paid it.

Will voluntary termination hurt my credit file?

It is a legal right, but lenders may record that the agreement ended early. Ask your lender how it will be reported.

What if I have paid less than half?

You pay the difference to reach half the total price, unless your agreement sets a lower amount.
