How long should a video on X (Twitter) be to keep people watching?

By the Asendify editorial team, led by Manthan Bhendkar · 5 min read · Published 23 November 2026 · Last updated 23 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

Keep most videos on X short: under 60 seconds, which is also the length at which X loops videos automatically, and 6 to 15 seconds for product clips, which is what X's business team recommends. Free accounts can upload up to 140 seconds; Premium subscribers can post much longer. Whatever the length, put the point in the first frame and add captions, because videos autoplay in the timeline and many people never turn the sound on.

Key takeaways

  • X's help centre says non-Premium accounts can upload videos up to 140 seconds (512MB), and videos of 60 seconds or less loop automatically.
  • Premium subscribers can upload up to 4 hours at 1080p on the web and iOS (10 minutes on Android), up to 16GB.
  • X's business guidance suggests 6 to 15 seconds for videos showing products and people, captions for mobile, and a logo fixed in a top corner.
  • Videos autoplay in timelines by default unless the viewer has turned autoplay off, so the first frame works like a thumbnail.
  • Long videos can work on X when people come for them, such as a full interview, but a short clip in the timeline should sell the long one.

What X's own pages say

X's help article How to share and watch videos on X sets the basic limits: up to 140 seconds and 512MB without Premium, and automatic looping for videos of 60 seconds or less. It also explains that native videos autoplay in timelines and Explore, and that each viewer can set autoplay to Wi-Fi only or never on each device. Captions show automatically in the timeline on iOS and Android where a video has them.

The Premium longer videos page says subscribers can upload videos up to 4 hours long at 1080p on x.com and iOS, with 2 to 4 hour videos at 720p, and a 10-minute limit on Android.

X's video resources for business advise keeping videos "between 6-15 seconds long" when showing products and people, adding captions for mobile, and placing your logo in the upper-left or upper-right corner.

Why the 60-second line matters

A video of 60 seconds or less replays on its own. That means a viewer who reaches the end and keeps looking sees the start again, so a clip that ends where it begins can feel continuous. It also means a weak ending is followed straight away by your opening, which is a second chance to catch someone who drifted.

Edit for a timeline where everything autoplays

  1. Make the first frame readable without sound. A face mid-sentence with a caption, or the product in action.
  2. Caption everything. Burn in captions or upload a caption file so the clip makes sense on mute.
  3. Write the post as the hook. The text above the video is read first; use it to say why the clip is worth 30 seconds.
  4. Trim the start. Cut the second of silence before the first word.
  5. End early. Stop once the point lands; let the loop or the reply thread carry the rest.
  6. For long videos, post a clip first. A 30-second highlight with "full conversation below" gives people a reason to commit.

FAQ

Is there a book that teaches openings for short videos?

Yes. The Retention Editing Playbook (Asendify) covers hooks and pacing for £1,720 one-time. X's business video resources are a free alternative.

What is the maximum video length on X without Premium?

140 seconds, with a maximum file size of 512MB, according to X's help centre.

Do videos on X loop?

Yes. X says videos of 60 seconds or less loop automatically.

Do X videos play with sound?

X's help page explains autoplay but does not state the default sound setting. Assume many people watch muted and caption your videos.

Is a long video ever worth posting on X?

Yes, if your audience comes to you for it, such as full interviews. Post a short clip first so people know what they are committing to.
