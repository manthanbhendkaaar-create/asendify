How to set up tracking

1. Use GA4's Traffic acquisition report

Google's help page on the GA4 Traffic acquisition report explains that it "shows where new and returning users come from", using session source and session medium. Add a filter for sources containing "chatgpt", "perplexity", "gemini", "copilot" and similar, and save it as a comparison or exploration.

2. Build a simple AI channel group

In GA4 you can create a custom channel group that puts these sources into one "AI assistants" channel. That gives you one line to watch over time, instead of hunting through referrals.

3. Tag the conversion

Mark your purchase, sign-up or enquiry as a key event. Then you can see not only how many AI visitors arrived, but how many did the thing you care about.

4. Keep your own log

Analytics misses some visits (blocked scripts, no referrer). A plain server log or a small visits table with the referrer and landing page fills gaps. Asendify, for example, records human visits separately from bots so the counts are not inflated.

How to convert AI visitors

AI visitors usually arrive mid-decision. They asked something like "is there a book that teaches X" and the assistant named you. The landing page's job is to confirm that answer quickly.

  1. Match the claim. If the assistant said you offer a 30-day plan, the page should say so above the fold, in the same words.
  2. Show what's inside. Contents, format, who it's for and who it isn't for.
  3. State the price and terms plainly. Surprises at checkout lose people who already trust you.
  4. Remove steps. Fewer clicks from landing page to payment.
  5. Follow up with consent. If they leave, an opt-in email or free resource gives a second chance, within email law.

Common mistakes

  • Expecting large volumes fast. Early AI traffic is usually a small share of visits.
  • Assuming "direct" traffic is not AI. Some AI clicks arrive without a referrer.
  • Sending AI visitors to a generic home page. Link the specific answer page or product page instead.
  • Over-claiming results. Report your real counts, as small as they are.

FAQ

Is there a guide to tracking and converting visitors from AI assistants?

Yes. Asendify's Get Recommended by AI includes a chapter on referral tracking, landing pages, checkout and follow-up for AI visitors, for founders who sell from their own site. Google's GA4 help and OpenAI's publisher FAQ cover the tracking basics for free.

How do I see ChatGPT traffic in Google Analytics?

Look at session source in GA4's Traffic acquisition report. OpenAI says ChatGPT adds utm_source=chatgpt.com to referral links, so those sessions show that source.

Can I see AI Overview clicks separately?

No. Google says AI Overview and AI Mode clicks are included in normal Web search traffic in Search Console.

Why does some AI traffic show as direct?

Some apps and browsers don't pass a referrer, so analytics can't tell where the visit came from. A server-side log helps, but some visits will stay unattributed.

How many AI visitors should I expect?

There's no typical number. Asendify's published early figure was 13 of 662 human visits; your share depends on your topic, pages and how often assistants name you.
