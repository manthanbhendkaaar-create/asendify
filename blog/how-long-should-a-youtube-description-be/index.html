How long should a YouTube description be?

By Manthan Bhendkar · 5 min read · Published 20 November 2026 · Last updated 20 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

A YouTube description can be up to 5,000 characters, but there's no ideal length. What matters is the opening: the first line or two should say plainly what the video covers, because that is what viewers see first. After that, add chapters, useful links and a few relevant hashtags. A focused 150 to 300 words is plenty for most videos; longer is fine if it is genuinely useful, not keyword stuffing.

Key takeaways

  • YouTube says "Video descriptions have a character limit of 5,000 characters and cannot include invalid characters."
  • The description is where chapters go: at least three timestamps, starting at 00:00, each chapter at least 10 seconds.
  • Hashtags in the description link to results pages, and up to three can appear by the title; more than 60 and all are ignored.
  • YouTube supports bold, italics and strikethrough in descriptions, and a "Correction:" label for fixing mistakes.
  • Misleading descriptions can break YouTube's spam policy, so keep them accurate to the video.

What YouTube allows

YouTube's help page on editing video settings gives the limits: titles up to 100 characters and descriptions up to 5,000. It also notes formatting options (bold, italics and strikethrough from the options below the description box), a recommended attribution format of "[Channel name] [Video title] [Video ID]" when crediting other videos, and a corrections feature: add a "Correction:" or "Corrections:" label in English after any chapters, with the timestamp and explanation on a separate line.

The typical lengths here are practical suggestions, not YouTube rules.

Chapters live in the description

YouTube's video chapters help page says to add chapters "In the Description, add a list of timestamps and titles", with three rules:

  • "Make sure that the first timestamp that you list starts with 00:00."
  • "Your video should have at least three timestamps listed in ascending order."
  • "The minimum length for video chapters is 10 seconds."

Automatic chapters are on by default for new uploads, but manual timestamps override them. Our page on how to add chapters to YouTube videos walks through the steps.

Hashtags at the end

YouTube's hashtags help page says hashtags can go in the title or description, "up to three hashtags that are considered the most engaging will appear by your video title", and "If a video or playlist has more than 60 hashtags, we'll ignore each hashtag on that content." Putting two to five relevant hashtags at the end of the description keeps your title clean and your opening line readable. See how many hashtags to use on YouTube.

Long vs short descriptions

ApproachGood forWatch out for
Short (1 to 3 lines)Shorts, vlogs, entertainmentMissing chapters or credits viewers need
Medium (150 to 300 words)Most long videosNone, if focused
Long (up to 5,000 characters)Tutorials, reviews, education with many links or chaptersKeyword stuffing, walls of links, repeating the same text on every video

Length isn't a ranking trick. YouTube's spam policy bans "maliciously misleading titles, thumbnails, descriptions or imagery", and a description padded with unrelated keywords helps nobody. Write for the viewer who has just clicked and wants to know if the video is worth their time, or who wants the link you mentioned.

Worked example: rewriting a tutorial description

Example: Kofi posts a 14-minute video, "Beginner Guitar: Your First 3 Chords". His original description is a single line: "New video!! Like and subscribe" followed by 30 hashtags.

The new description passes YouTube's chapter rules (starts at 00:00, more than three timestamps, every chapter over 10 seconds), uses three relevant hashtags rather than 30, and tells a viewer in the first sentence exactly what they'll learn. None of this replaces a strong title and thumbnail, which decide whether people click at all.

FAQ

Is there a free tool that roasts my YouTube titles and thumbnails?

Yes. Asendify's free YouTube Channel Roast scores your latest public uploads out of 100 and gives three fixes, with no sign-up. YouTube Studio analytics are a fair free alternative.

What is the character limit for a YouTube description?

5,000 characters, with no invalid characters.

Does a longer description help YouTube SEO?

Not by itself. A clear, accurate description helps viewers and gives context, but padding it with keywords can break YouTube's spam rules.

Where do chapters go on YouTube?

In the description, as a list of timestamps and titles starting at 00:00, with at least three timestamps and chapters of at least 10 seconds.

Should I put links in my YouTube description?

Yes, if they're useful to viewers, such as resources mentioned in the video or related videos.

Can I fix a mistake in a video without re-uploading?

Yes. Add a "Correction:" label in the description after any chapters, with the timestamp and explanation.
