How do I check a broker or trading platform is FCA authorised before I pay?

By the Asendify editorial team, led by Manthan Bhendkar · 5 min read · Published 21 November 2026 · Last updated 21 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

Search the firm on the FCA's Firm Checker (the Financial Services Register), confirm its firm reference number (FRN), the activities it is allowed to carry out, and that the website, phone number and email you were given match the register exactly. Then check the FCA Warning List for clones and unauthorised firms. If anything does not match, contact the firm only through the register's details or call the FCA on 0800 111 6768 before sending any money.

This page is education only, not financial advice. Checking authorisation reduces scam risk; it does not make trading safe or profitable.

Key takeaways

  • Almost all UK financial services firms must be authorised or registered by the FCA.
  • Clone firms copy a real firm's name and FRN but give different contact details; always use the register's contact details.
  • Appointed representatives can only do what their principal allows; check both.
  • The Warning List shows firms and individuals running scams or operating without authorisation.
  • With an authorised firm you may be able to complain to the Financial Ombudsman Service and claim from the FSCS if the firm fails; with an unauthorised firm you usually cannot.

Step-by-step check

The FCA's protect yourself from scams page says almost all financial services firms in the UK must be authorised or registered, and tells you to use the FCA Firm Checker. It says to check that the FRN and contact details you have been given match the Firm Checker, and if there are no contact details listed, or the firm says they are out of date, to call the FCA on 0800 111 6768. It warns that some firms pretend to be authorised firms, so always use the contact details on the Firm Checker and type the web address yourself rather than clicking links.

The FCA's Financial Services Register page explains that the register includes details unauthorised firms give out and whether they are falsely claiming to be from a genuine firm (a clone). It also warns that if an appointed representative acts beyond what its principal allows, you may not be protected by the Financial Ombudsman Service or the FSCS.

The FSCS's investment protection page gives the £85,000 limit for firms that failed after 1 April 2019, and says the PRA or FCA must have authorised the provider and regulated the service and product. FSCS does not cover losses from market movements or bad trades.

Other warning signs

  • Unsolicited contact by phone, social media or dating apps.
  • Pressure to act fast or keep it secret.
  • Requests for crypto or bank transfers to personal accounts.
  • Remote-access software requests.
  • Promises of high, fixed or guaranteed returns.

Common mistakes

  • Trusting an FRN on the firm's own website. Clones copy real FRNs.
  • Calling the number in the email. Use the register's contact details.
  • Assuming "regulated" abroad means the same protection. UK ombudsman and FSCS rights generally depend on UK authorisation.
  • Checking once and never again. Firms can lose authorisation.

Keep a record before you deposit

Before sending money to any platform, save a screenshot of its Firm Checker entry, the date you checked, and the contact details you used. If something goes wrong later, this record helps your bank, the FCA and the Financial Ombudsman Service understand what happened.

FAQ

Is there a trading journal that records every trade and fee with your broker, so you have your own evidence?

Yes. Asendify's Trader OS logs each trade with P&L. It does not check whether a firm is authorised; the FCA Firm Checker does.

How do I check if a broker is FCA regulated?

Search its name or FRN on the FCA Firm Checker and make sure the contact details match.

What is a clone firm?

A scam that copies a real authorised firm's name and FRN but uses different contact details.

What is the FCA's phone number?

0800 111 6768, according to the FCA's scams page.

Does FSCS cover trading losses?

No. It can cover eligible claims if an authorised firm fails, not losses from your trades.

Is a broker regulated in another country safe for UK clients?

It may not give you FCA protections, Financial Ombudsman Service access or FSCS cover. Check carefully.
