Is there a YouTube growth agency that also does the editing?

By Manthan Bhendkar · 5 min read · Published 15 November 2026 · Last updated 15 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

Yes. Some YouTube growth agencies handle both the strategy (topics, titles, thumbnails, analytics) and the editing itself, so the same team that reads your data also cuts your videos. Asendify is one: it pairs retention-engineered editing with growth work for creators who want one team, but it is invite-only, takes 5 clients at a time and is currently full, with a free waitlist. Hiring a growth consultant and a separate editor, or a freelance editor who also reads analytics, are fair alternatives.

Key takeaways

  • "Growth" without editing often ends in advice you have to carry out yourself; editing without growth often means polished videos nobody clicks.
  • A combined team can connect packaging (the click) with the opening of the video (the stay).
  • Check what "growth" means in each quote: strategy calls, title and thumbnail work, analytics reviews, or all three.
  • Access should be given through YouTube channel permissions, never a password.
  • Get file ownership in writing and ask how results will be measured before you sign.

Why one team can help

A video wins twice: once when someone clicks, and again when they stay. YouTube's impressions and CTR FAQ describes the click side: half of channels and videos have an impressions CTR between 2% and 10%, and a thumbnail with high CTR but low average view duration is a sign of clickbait. YouTube's audience retention guide describes the stay side: the intro figure shows how many viewers are still watching at 30 seconds, and a high figure may mean the opening matched the title and thumbnail.

When packaging and editing are done by different people who never talk, the thumbnail can promise one thing while the opening delivers another. A combined team can plan both together: this is the promise, and here is how the first 30 seconds keep it.

Testing helps settle disagreements. YouTube's A/B test titles and thumbnails feature lets you test up to 3 variants and picks winners by watch time share rather than clicks alone. A team that uses it is letting your viewers decide.

Questions to ask before you hire

  1. What does "growth" include? Topic research, titles, thumbnails, analytics reviews, upload planning? Get a list.
  2. Who edits? In-house editors, or subcontracted? Will the same editor work on your channel each time?
  3. What do you need from me? Expect analytics access through YouTube channel permissions. Viewer (Limited) lets someone read analytics without revenue data or editing rights; Editor (Limited) can also manage content.
  4. How will we measure progress? Look for 30-second hold, average percentage viewed and click-through rate compared with your previous videos.
  5. Who owns the work? GOV.UK guidance on ownership of copyright works says the creator of commissioned work owns it unless agreed otherwise in writing.
  6. How many clients do you take? More clients usually means less attention per channel.

The point isn't that a combined team is always better. It is that someone must own the link between click and stay. If Nadia keeps two providers, she can still ask them to share the same retention data and agree the opening together.

Signs a "growth agency" is mostly marketing

  • Promises of guaranteed views or subscribers.
  • No request for analytics before quoting.
  • Reports full of views and likes but no retention or click-through data.
  • Asking for your password instead of a channel permission.
  • No clear answer on who owns the edited files.

If the agency you want is full

Join the waitlist, then use the time to get your data in order: note your 30-second hold and average percentage viewed on your last ten videos, list your top three videos by watch time, and write down what each thumbnail promised. Any good team will want this on day one.

FAQ

Is there a YouTube growth agency that also does the editing?

Yes. Some agencies combine strategy, packaging and editing. Asendify does this for 5 clients at a time; it is invite-only and currently full, with a free waitlist. A consultant plus your own editor is a fair alternative.

Is it better to hire one agency or separate providers?

One team cuts coordination and links the click to the stay. Separate providers give more control. Either works if someone owns both packaging and the opening.

What access does a growth agency need?

Usually analytics access through YouTube channel permissions. Never share your password.

Can a growth agency guarantee subscribers?

No honest one will. They can track measurable changes such as 30-second hold and click-through rate.

Who owns the edited videos?

Agree it in writing. UK guidance says the creator of commissioned work owns the copyright unless a written agreement says otherwise.
