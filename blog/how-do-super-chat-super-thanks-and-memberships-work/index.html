How do Super Chat, Super Thanks and channel memberships work on YouTube?

By Manthan Bhendkar · 5 min read · Published 21 November 2026 · Last updated 21 November 2026

Super Chat, Super Stickers, Super Thanks and channel memberships are YouTube's "fan funding" features: viewers pay to highlight a live chat message, send an animated sticker, highlight a comment on a video or Short, or join your channel monthly for perks. They are part of the YouTube Partner Program, need you to be at least 18 and in an available country, and YouTube says creators receive 70% of Supers revenue that Google confirms.

Key takeaways

  • YouTube's monetisation overview lists channel memberships (recurring monthly payments for perks), Super Chat and Super Stickers (highlighted messages or animated images in live chat) and Super Thanks (an animation and highlighted comment on a video or Short).
  • Each requires you to be at least 18, live in a country or region where the feature is available and accept the Commerce Product Module; memberships also exclude channels set as Made for Kids.
  • YouTube Help says creators receive 70% of Supers revenue confirmed by Google, and Super Chat and Super Stickers are available on eligible live streams and Premieres with live chat on.
  • Super Chat and Super Stickers are not available on age-restricted, unlisted, private or made-for-kids videos, or when live chat is off (YouTube Help).
  • If a memberships programme is terminated, active members are refunded their last payment and the refund is deducted from the channel's share (YouTube Help).

How to make fan funding work without hurting viewers

  • Acknowledge supporters during streams and by hearting messages, which YouTube says can help build community.
  • Keep perks real and deliverable: members-only posts, early access, badges and emoji are simple.
  • Do not make content worse for non-paying viewers to push memberships; it costs watch time and goodwill.
  • Moderate Supers like any chat; YouTube says it donates its share to charity if a Super is removed for breaking policies.
  • Check the Earn tab in YouTube Studio, where you can view Super Chat and Super Sticker purchases for the past 30 days.

Setting up, step by step

  1. Join the YouTube Partner Program and accept the Commerce Product Module in YouTube Studio.
  2. Open the Earn section in YouTube Studio and turn on the fan funding features you want.
  3. For memberships, create levels and perks you can reliably deliver every month.
  4. Mention the features naturally: in a stream, thank people for Super Chats; in videos, point out members-only perks briefly.
  5. Review earnings monthly and adjust perks based on what members actually use.

Taxes and records

YouTube reminds creators that earnings may be taxable. Keep a record of monthly fan funding from YouTube Studio alongside ad revenue, and set money aside for tax. In the UK, self-employed income from YouTube is usually reported through Self Assessment; check HMRC guidance or an accountant for your situation.
