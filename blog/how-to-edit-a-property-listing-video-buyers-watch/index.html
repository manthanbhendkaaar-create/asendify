How do I edit a property listing video that buyers watch to the end (UK)?

Key takeaways

  • Rightmove embeds YouTube and Vimeo videos on the property details page; other links open in a pop-up.
  • OnTheMarket accepts YouTube or Vimeo links, or .mp4/.mov uploads up to 150MB.
  • Rightmove reminds agents that under GDPR "you become the data controller": hide personal documents and photos.
  • National Trading Standards guidance lists material information listings must include, such as price, council tax and tenure.
  • Retention comes from a strong opening, a logical route, steady pace and honest framing.

Where the video goes and how it's shown

On Rightmove, agents add a video in Rightmove Plus under Media, Additional media. According to Rightmove's guide to sharing a virtual tour or video, YouTube and Vimeo videos are embedded on the property details page, while other secure links open in a separate pop-up. If listings come through a data feed, videos must be added through your software instead.

OnTheMarket's help page on adding a video tour says you can upload an .mp4 or .mov file or paste a YouTube or Vimeo link, with a maximum file size of 150MB.

Why viewers stop watching a property video

Buyers watch property videos to decide whether a viewing is worth their time. They leave when:

  • The video opens with a long drone shot of the street or a slow logo.
  • The route jumps around so they can't picture the layout.
  • Rooms are shown without saying which room they are or how big.
  • Music is loud and nothing is explained.
  • It feels like it's hiding something, such as a room skipped or an odd angle.

An edit plan for a 90-second tour

TimeSectionWhat to show
0:00 to 0:08HookThe best feature: the garden, the kitchen, the view
0:08 to 0:15FrontFront of house, caption with area and property type
0:15 to 1:10Walk-throughGround floor then upstairs, in a logical order; caption each room
1:10 to 1:25Outside and extrasGarden, parking, storage
1:25 to 1:30CloseAgent name and how to book a viewing

Editing habits:

  1. Open on the best feature. Don't save the best room for last; viewers may never get there.
  2. Follow a real route. Move from room to room as a buyer would. Doorway-to-doorway transitions help people build a mental floor plan.
  3. Caption every room. Name and, where known, measurements. Captions also help people watching muted.
  4. Keep the camera steady and slow. Fast whip pans make people feel seasick and hide detail.
  5. Show the whole room. Wide, level shots that don't distort scale are fairer than ultra-wide lenses that make rooms look bigger.
  6. Remove personal information. Rightmove reminds agents to check that no personal data, documents or sensitive information is visible.

Honesty matters: material information

National Trading Standards published full material information guidance in November 2023 to help agents meet their obligations under consumer protection rules, which already bar omitting material information from listings. Part A covers council tax, price or rent and tenure; Part B covers things such as property type, rooms, utilities, broadband, mobile coverage and parking; Part C covers issues such as flood risk or restrictive covenants where they apply.

The guidance is written for listings rather than videos specifically. But a video sits on the listing, so it shouldn't contradict the facts in it, and editing that hides a known problem (a skipped room, a framed-out pylon) can undermine trust. This is general information, not legal advice; check with your redress scheme or a solicitor if unsure.

FAQ

Is there a book that teaches video hooks and pacing for tours?

Yes. The Retention Editing Playbook covers hooks and pacing for £1,000 one-time. The portals' own guides are free for the upload side.

How do I add a video to a Rightmove listing?

In Rightmove Plus, edit the listing, open the Media tab and add the URL under Additional media; YouTube and Vimeo are embedded.

What video files does OnTheMarket accept?

.mp4 or .mov uploads up to 150MB, or a YouTube or Vimeo link.

How long should a property video be?

Portals don't set a length; a short tour that covers every room in a logical order respects buyers' time.

Can I leave a room out of the video?

You can, but the video shouldn't mislead, and listings must not omit material information under NTS guidance. Showing every room builds trust.
