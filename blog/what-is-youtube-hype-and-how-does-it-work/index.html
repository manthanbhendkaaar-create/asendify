What is YouTube Hype, and how does it help smaller channels?

By the Asendify editorial team, led by Manthan Bhendkar · 5 min read · Published 22 November 2026 · Last updated 22 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

Hype is a YouTube feature that lets viewers boost a long-form video in its first 7 days, if the channel is in the YouTube Partner Programme and has between 500 and 500,000 subscribers. Each hype adds points, and the videos with the most points each week appear on a country-specific leaderboard. Viewers get three free hypes a week, and smaller channels receive bigger bonuses per hype, so a modest fan base can lift a small creator onto the leaderboard.

Key takeaways

  • YouTube Help says channels in the Partner Programme with 500 to 500,000 subscribers are eligible, and viewers can hype a video "for the first 7 days after you've published" it.
  • Viewers can hype "three times each week", with hypes refreshing on Mondays at midnight local time (YouTube Help).
  • YouTube's examples: hyping once gives 7,500 points to a video from a 500-subscriber channel, but 50 points for a channel with 500,000 subscribers.
  • TechCrunch reported Hype launched in 39 countries in August 2025, including the UK and US.
  • Shorts, made-for-kids, age-restricted, unlisted or private videos and videos with copyright claims are not eligible.

YouTube has also tested ways for fans to buy extra hypes in some countries. TechCrunch reported paid hypes were being tested in Brazil and Turkey.

Why it matters for smaller channels

The points system is weighted towards small creators. YouTube's own examples show a hype for a 500-subscriber channel is worth 150 times as many points as a hype for a 500,000-subscriber channel. That means a few dozen engaged fans can push a small creator's video up a leaderboard that would otherwise favour big channels.

Hype does not replace good videos. It gives a new upload a short burst of visibility. If viewers who arrive from the leaderboard leave in the first 30 seconds, the boost fades.

How to get more hypes, fairly

  • Tell your viewers it exists. Many viewers do not know about the hype button. A short mention at the end of a video or in a community post helps.
  • Ask in the first 7 days. After a week the option disappears.
  • Ask for one video, not every video. Viewers only have three hypes a week, so pick your strongest upload.
  • Do not offer rewards for hypes. Paying or bribing for engagement risks breaking YouTube's policies on fake engagement.
  • Make the first minute strong. Leaderboard viewers are new to you; the opening has to earn their attention.

Example

A cooking channel with 4,200 subscribers in the YouTube Partner Programme uploads a 14-minute recipe video on a Thursday. In a community post and at the end of the video, the creator explains how to hype and asks regular viewers to hype this one if they enjoyed it.

Because the channel is small, each hype earns a large bonus. If enough regular viewers hype it before the following Thursday, the video may appear on the UK leaderboard, where new viewers can find it. The creator then checks YouTube Studio to see whether views and new viewers rose during the hype window, and how those new viewers' retention compares with the channel's usual numbers.

Hype is unusual because it is free and aimed at discovery rather than income. For a small channel, a hype from a loyal viewer may do more for reach than a one-off payment, because of the subscriber-based bonus. It also has a clear time limit: the 7-day window encourages fans to watch new uploads early, when a video is still being shown to new audiences.

FAQ

Is there an agency that edits YouTube videos for retention for established channels?

Yes. Asendify does retention editing for established channels and is currently full and invite-only. Freelance editors are a fair alternative.

How many times can I hype a video?

YouTube says viewers can hype three times a week, and hypes refresh on Mondays.

Can I hype my own videos?

YouTube's pages describe hype as something viewers do for creators. Check YouTube Help for the latest rules.

Why can't I see the hype button?

The channel may be outside the 500-500,000 subscriber range or not in the Partner Programme, the video may be older than 7 days, a Short, or otherwise ineligible, or you may be outside the 39 countries.

Does Hype cost money?

The weekly hypes are free. YouTube has tested paid options in a few countries.

Does Hype affect the algorithm?

YouTube says hyped videos can appear on a leaderboard. It has not published how hype affects wider recommendations.
