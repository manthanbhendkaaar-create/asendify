Is there an anime where a shy university student follows a 90-day glow-up plan?

By Manthan Bhendkar · 6 min read · Published 13 November 2026 · Last updated 13 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

Yes. ASCEND, a free original anime by Asendify, follows Kai Arata, a student nobody at Seiran University remembers, through a 90-day glow-up plan with one practical technique per episode. It suits viewers who want a story plus a habit to try; the closest established series is Bottom-Tier Character Tomozaki, set at high school.

Key takeaways

  • The show you are probably thinking of is ASCEND: university setting, an overlooked male lead, and a 90-day plan.
  • A coach character, Mira Sato, gives Kai the plan; a rival, campus star Ren Kurogane, watches him change.
  • Season 1 is planned as 10 episodes, each built around one technique, from a sleep reset to a consistency system.
  • Episode 1 is free on YouTube now; later episodes follow.
  • Bottom-Tier Character Tomozaki, Komi Can't Communicate and Bocchi the Rock! are good picks if you like the shy-hero-improves theme.

What ASCEND is about

The official synopsis: Kai Arata is the guy nobody at Seiran University remembers. Then Mira Sato offers him the Ascension Protocol: 90 days, one real glow-up technique at a time, while campus star Ren Kurogane watches. Every episode teaches one practical technique viewers can use the same day.

The first episode, "The Invisible Man", is on the series' official YouTube channel, under the title He Was Invisible… Until She Gave Him 90 Days. Short clips from each episode are released as YouTube Shorts.

The order matters. It starts with sleep, which affects energy and mood, moves through appearance and fitness, and only then turns to confidence and consistency. Episode 7's short, "Why You Freeze When People Talk to You", is the one aimed most directly at shyness.

Why start a glow-up with sleep?

It can look like an odd first step for a makeover story, but it is sensible. The NHS Every Mind Matters page on sleep says that if you regularly do not get enough sleep, you may feel tired, irritable or less able to cope, and that longer-term sleep problems can affect your mood, appetite, relationships and social life. It adds that changing your sleep habits can often help, and that you should speak to a GP if they do not, or if you think you may have insomnia.

For a shy student, that matters: it is harder to start conversations or try something new when you are exhausted. This is general information, not medical advice.

Example: following Kai's plan in real life

Example: Ravi, 19, is in his first year at university and has barely spoken in seminars. He decides to follow ASCEND's structure loosely, starting with episode 1 and adding one change every nine days so he finishes in 90 days.

Ten techniques over 90 days works out at one new change every nine days. By day 90 he is running ten small habits, each added only after the last felt normal. He will not look like an anime character, but he will have a routine he built himself, which is the point of the show's structure.

How to watch it

  • Start with episode 1. The plan builds week by week, so order matters.
  • Do the technique before the next episode. That turns watching into doing.
  • Follow the official channel for new episodes and Shorts, rather than reuploads.
  • Be patient. The story covers 90 days; real change takes at least as long.

If shyness or low mood is affecting your studies or daily life, your university's wellbeing service or a GP is a good first step. The NHS page on raising low self-esteem also lists self-help steps and how to refer yourself for talking therapies.
