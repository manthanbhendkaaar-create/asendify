How do you add links to your YouTube channel, and where do they show?

By Manthan Bhendkar · 5 min read · Published 21 November 2026 · Last updated 21 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

To add links to your YouTube channel, open YouTube Studio > Customization > Profile, click "Add link" under Links, enter a title and URL, then click Publish. You can show up to 14 links, and the first one appears in the profile section near the Subscribe button, with the rest available when viewers click to see more. Links must follow YouTube's External links policy, and URLs in Shorts descriptions and comments aren't clickable.

Key takeaways

  • YouTube Help: "You can showcase up to 14 links on your channel Home tab."
  • The first link is shown near the Subscribe button, so put your most important one first.
  • Clickable external URLs in long-form video descriptions and comments require advanced features to be enabled.
  • "URLs placed in YouTube Shorts comments and Shorts descriptions are non-clickable", to reduce spam and scams.
  • Breaking the External links policy can mean link removal, a warning, then strikes.

How to add channel links

YouTube's help page on managing your channel profile gives the steps:

  1. Sign in to YouTube Studio.
  2. Choose Customization, then Profile.
  3. Under Links, click Add link and enter a title and URL.
  4. Repeat for up to 14 links.
  5. Click Publish.

On the same screen you can add a business contact email under Contact info. YouTube also notes you can change your channel name, and your handle, twice within a 14-day period, and that changing the name removes a verification badge.

Where links are clickable

YouTube's page on sharing links with your audiences sets out what works:

Long-form video descriptions and commentsYes; external links require advanced features
End screensYes
Related video link on ShortsYes
YouTube Shopping affiliate linksYes
URLs in Shorts descriptions and commentsNo
URLs in postsNo
Live chat on the vertical mobile live feedNo

So for Shorts creators, channel links and the related video link are the main ways to send viewers somewhere. Our page on verifying your YouTube channel explains how advanced features are unlocked.

When the 14-link feature arrived

Search Engine Land reported YouTube's change letting creators add up to 14 clickable links near the Subscribe button, with custom titles and platform icons, visible on desktop and mobile. A YouTube spokesperson said links are "an important way for creators to share information."

The rules: External links policy

YouTube's External links policy covers links anywhere on YouTube, including channels, descriptions, comments and pinned comments, and even URLs shown in a video or read aloud.

Not allowedConsequence
Links to pornography, malware or phishing sitesContent removed, creator emailed
Links promising violating content elsewhereFirst time: usually a warning
Disguised links (e.g. "dot com") to violating sitesRepeat within 90 days: a strike
Links YouTube can't verify as safeMay be removed

Three strikes within 90 days may lead to channel termination. YouTube notes affiliate links don't break the Terms of Use, though excessive affiliate posting in dedicated accounts may break the Spam policy.

Keep links up to date

Links go stale quietly. A sold-out product, a closed newsletter or a social account you no longer use sends viewers to a dead end, and they rarely come back to try again. Put a reminder in your calendar to check every channel link once a month, and update the first link whenever your main offer or focus changes. If you run campaigns, swap the link when the campaign ends rather than leaving an old promotion at the top.

Which links to put first

PriorityExampleWhy
1Your own website, shop or newsletterThe first link gets the prime spot near Subscribe
2The social platform where you're most activeLets fans follow you where you post most
3A current offer, course or merchChange it as campaigns change
4+Other socials, podcasts, sponsorsAvailable under "more"

Keep link titles short and specific ("Free recipe e-book" rather than "Click here"). And point video descriptions to the same key link so viewers see it consistently; see how long a YouTube description should be. A clear banner that names what you post completes the picture.

How many links can I add to my YouTube channel?

Up to 14.

Why aren't links in my Shorts description clickable?

YouTube made them non-clickable to reduce spam and scams. Use channel links or the related video link instead.

Which link shows on my channel page?

The first link in your list appears near the Subscribe button; the rest show when viewers click for more.

Can I add affiliate links?

Affiliate links don't break YouTube's Terms of Use, but excessive affiliate posting may break the Spam policy.

Why can't I add clickable links to my video descriptions?

External clickable links in long-form descriptions require advanced features; verify your channel and build history.
