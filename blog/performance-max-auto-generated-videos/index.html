Will Google make my Performance Max video ads for me, and should I upload my own?

By Manthan Bhendkar · 5 min read · Published 22 November 2026 · Last updated 22 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

Yes. If a Performance Max asset group has no video you uploaded, Google AI may automatically generate video ads from your text, images, brand guidelines and product feed. Uploading your own video turns those auto-generated videos off, and Google recommends one horizontal, one vertical and one square video of 10 seconds or more, so most advertisers who care about how they look should upload their own.

Key takeaways

  • Google: "Auto-generated video ads are created automatically by Google AI when you don't have active, user uploaded videos."
  • They're built from your text and image assets, brand guidelines (colour and font) and Merchant Center product images.
  • "The system automatically disables auto-generated video ads if you upload your own videos."
  • Google recommends 1 video of each orientation (16:9, 1:1, 9:16), 10 seconds or more, with at least one vertical video of 10 to 60 seconds for Shorts.
  • Video enhancements (Google re-formatting your own videos) are separate, and you can opt out of them in campaign settings.

How auto-generated videos work

Google's help page About auto-generated video ads explains that when eligible, "Google AI automatically generates additional video ads in multiple orientations." It picks the template, colours, font and music it judges best for your brand. You can find them in the asset group details, labelled "Automatically created" in the Source column.

You can't edit these videos, and you can't manage the Google-owned channel that hosts them. If you change or delete the original assets, the video is deleted and a new one is generated from your latest assets.

An auto-generated video is a slideshow-style edit of your images and text. It can't show your product in motion, a person explaining it, or a hook you've tested. If video matters to your results, a simple video you made yourself is usually a better test than an automatic one.

A simple set of three videos to upload

You don't need a studio. A phone, good light and a clear script are enough to replace auto-generated videos.

VideoShapeLengthContent
Main16:920 to 30 sProduct in action, benefit, proof, call to action
Shorts cut9:1615 to 30 sSame story, reframed vertical, captions centred
Feed cut1:115 to 20 sThe hook and one proof

Editing rules for all three:

  1. Open on the product or a person, not a logo. The first seconds decide whether anyone keeps watching.
  2. Make it work muted. Captions and on-screen text carry the message.
  3. Reframe, don't just crop. Google may resize horizontal videos to square or vertical; upload proper versions of each shape so your framing is the one shown.
  4. One message per video. Performance Max mixes assets; a focused video fits more combinations.

Worked example

Example: a garden furniture retailer runs Performance Max with a Merchant Center feed and no videos.

  1. The asset group shows two videos labelled "Automatically created", built from product photos and a headline.
  2. One features a parasol, though the landing page is for a rattan sofa set.
  3. The retailer films a 25-second horizontal video of the sofa set being assembled and used, a 20-second vertical cut and a 15-second square cut.
  4. Once uploaded, the system disables the auto-generated videos for that asset group.
  5. After about three weeks, the retailer checks Ad strength and swaps the weakest cut for a new opening.

Details are illustrative.

How to check what Google is showing

Open the asset group and look at the Source column: anything marked "Automatically created" was made by Google. Watch each one start to finish on a phone. Ask three questions: does it show the right product, does it look like your brand, and would you stop scrolling for it? If any answer is no, that's your cue to upload your own videos. After uploading, check again that the automatic ones have stopped serving, and keep an eye on Ad strength over the following weeks.

FAQ

Is there a book that teaches how to make video ads people watch?

Yes. The Retention Editing Playbook covers hooks and pacing for £1,000 one-time. Google's help pages are free for the technical setup.

How do I stop Performance Max making videos for me?

Upload your own video to the asset group; Google says the system then disables auto-generated video ads.

Can I edit an auto-generated video?

No. Google says you can't edit them or manage the channel that hosts them.

What video lengths does Performance Max want?

Google recommends videos of 10 seconds or more, with at least one vertical video of 10 to 60 seconds for Shorts.

What are video enhancements?

New versions of your own videos, such as vertical or square formats, made by Google AI. You can opt out in campaign settings.
