Do YouTube playlists help your channel, and how should you use them?

By Manthan Bhendkar · 6 min read · Published 20 November 2026 · Last updated 20 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

Playlists help a YouTube channel mainly as packaging: they group related videos so a viewer can watch the next one easily, they can fill sections on your channel's home tab, and a public playlist can be linked from an end screen. YouTube doesn't publish a rule that playlists boost rankings, so treat them as a way to make your back catalogue easier to binge, not a growth hack. Setting them up takes minutes.

Key takeaways

  • YouTube's channel layout lets you add "up to 12 custom sections" to the home tab, and sections can highlight "single, created and multiple playlists."
  • End screens can include a playlist element: "Display a public YouTube playlist."
  • Playlists can be public or private; YouTube says "If it's private, only you can view the playlist."
  • There is a daily limit on how many public playlists a channel can create, shared across YouTube, YouTube Music and the API.
  • Titles, descriptions, privacy and video order can be edited later, including in the YouTube Studio app.

What playlists actually do for a channel

Think about a new viewer who enjoys one of your videos. Without playlists, they have to hunt through your Videos tab to find the next relevant one. With a clear playlist ("Beginner sourdough, start here"), the next step is obvious. That is the honest case for playlists: they reduce friction between one video and the next.

How to create a playlist

YouTube's help page on creating and managing playlists gives the desktop steps:

  1. Open the watch page of a video you want in the playlist.
  2. Click More, then Save, then Create new playlist.
  3. Enter a name and choose a privacy setting.
  4. Click Create.

The same steps work for a Short. YouTube notes: "There's a limit to how many public playlists a channel can create each day", and that the limit can be increased by gaining access to advanced features. If you plan to build many playlists at once, spread the work over a few days.

How to edit and organise playlists

YouTube's page on editing playlists explains that you can change the title and description by selecting the playlist and clicking Edit, reorder videos by dragging the handle near each thumbnail, filter by All, Shorts or Videos, and remove a video from the More menu. Reordering isn't available for playlists that contain only Shorts. YouTube Studio's mobile app also lets you edit the title, description, privacy and video order.

Practical organising tips:

  • Name playlists the way viewers search. "Beginner guitar chords" beats "Season 2".
  • Put your strongest video first. The first video is what most people start with.
  • Write a short description saying who the playlist is for and in what order to watch; our page on how long a YouTube description should be covers video descriptions too.
  • Add chapters to long videos inside the playlist so viewers can jump to the part they need; see how to add chapters to YouTube videos.
  • Keep playlists focused. One topic or series per playlist; a "Everything" playlist adds little.

Put playlists on your channel home tab

YouTube's help page on customising your channel layout says the home tab can hold "up to 12 custom sections", with four pre-populated sections by default, and that you can "Choose to highlight single, created and multiple playlists." It also lets you set a channel trailer for people who haven't subscribed and a featured video for returning subscribers. Our page on whether a YouTube channel trailer matters covers the trailer.

A simple layout for a small channel: trailer at the top, then a "Start here" playlist, then two or three topic playlists, then recent uploads.

Use playlists in end screens

YouTube's help page on end screens lists a Playlist element, which can "Display a public YouTube playlist". End screens can be added to "the last 5–20 seconds of a video", the video must be at least 25 seconds long, and you can add up to four elements on a standard 16:9 video. Pointing the end screen of part one to the full series playlist is a natural fit.

Do playlists help YouTube SEO?

YouTube's help pages don't say playlists improve rankings. They help viewers find and watch related videos, and a clear playlist title can describe the topic.

How many playlists should a small channel have?

Enough to group your main topics, often three to five to start. Focused playlists are more useful than many tiny ones.

Can I add Shorts to a playlist?

Yes. You can save Shorts to playlists, filter by Shorts, but you can't reorder a playlist that contains only Shorts.

Why can't I create more public playlists today?

YouTube limits how many public playlists a channel can create each day; the limit can rise when you get access to advanced features.

Should playlists be public or private?

Public for anything you want viewers to find or link from an end screen; private for drafts and personal collections, which only you can view.
