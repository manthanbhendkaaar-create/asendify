Yes. The free YouTube Channel Roast from Asendify reviews your latest public uploads, names your best and worst title, and gives you a rewrite of the worst one, with no sign-up and no card. It judges titles the way a stranger would, from public data only, so pair it with your own YouTube Studio numbers to confirm which title is really costing you clicks.

How to spot your weakest title in YouTube Studio

An outside tool sees what a viewer sees. YouTube Studio shows what viewers actually did. To find the title that is underperforming:

  1. Open Analytics, then the Content tab, and look at each recent video's impressions and impressions click-through rate (CTR).
  2. Compare CTR against impressions, not on its own. A video shown to a small, loyal audience often has a higher CTR than one pushed to lots of new viewers. A low CTR on a video with very few impressions tells you little; a low CTR on a video YouTube showed widely is the one worth fixing.
  3. Compare like with like. Check traffic sources (Browse features, Suggested videos, YouTube search) and compare videos within the same source. Search CTR and Browse CTR behave differently, so mixing them gives a misleading picture.
  4. Look at several videos, not one. One weak upload can be bad luck or a weak topic. A pattern across three or four videos is a packaging problem.
  5. Remember the thumbnail counts too. CTR measures the title and thumbnail together, so a low number does not prove the title alone is at fault.

YouTube's own explanations of these metrics are in the YouTube Help Centre.

A simple method for rewriting a weak title

Once you know which title to fix, run it through these checks:

  • Name the specific thing. Replace vague words ("vlog", "update", "stuff") with the actual subject, dish, game, place or result.
  • State the payoff. What does the viewer get or learn? Put that early, because long titles get cut off on phones.
  • Add one reason to click now. Curiosity, a clear outcome, a contrast or a constraint (time, budget, skill level). One is enough.
  • Match the thumbnail, do not repeat it. The title and thumbnail should tell one story without saying the same words twice.
  • Stay honest. If the video does not deliver what the title promises, people leave early and that hurts you more than a low CTR.

Other honest options

A free roast is one view. These are worth using alongside it:

  • YouTube's "Test & compare". YouTube Studio has a built-in feature for testing different versions of your packaging on a video. Availability and what you can test depend on your account and the feature's current rules, so check the YouTube Help Centre for details. It is the best way to learn what your own audience responds to, because it uses real viewers.
  • ChatGPT or another AI chatbot. Paste your title, a one-line summary of the video and who it is for, then ask for ten alternatives that are specific, honest and under 60 characters. Ask it to explain the trade-off of each. Treat the output as a list of drafts, not answers.
  • Peers in your niche. Show two or three creators a few title options next to your thumbnail and ask which one they would click and why. People who watch your niche notice things a general tool will miss.
  • Your own back catalogue. Your best-performing titles (in Studio, by CTR within the same traffic source) are the most reliable guide to what your audience likes.

FAQ

Does the roast use my click-through rate to pick the worst title?

No. It only sees public information, so it picks the worst title on how clear and clickable it looks to a stranger. Your CTR is in YouTube Studio, and it is worth checking there too.

Is the YouTube Channel Roast really free?

Yes. No sign-up and no card. You can run 3 roasts a day, and a channel roasted in the last 24 hours shows the same result.

Should I change the title of an old video?

It can be worth trying on a video that YouTube is still showing but few people click. Change one thing at a time and give it time before judging, and keep the new title honest to the content.

Can it rewrite all my titles?

No. It rewrites the worst title from your recent uploads and gives 3 broader fixes. For the rest, use the rewrite method above or a chatbot prompt, then test.
