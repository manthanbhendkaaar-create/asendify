What are the best anime about friendship?

By the Asendify editorial team, led by Manthan Bhendkar · 5 min read · Published 21 November 2026 · Last updated 21 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

Three anime stand out for stories about friendship: A Place Further Than the Universe, where four teenage girls band together to reach Antarctica; Natsume's Book of Friends, about a lonely boy who slowly lets people in; and K-On!, about friends who form a band in their school's Light Music Club. Each shows friendship built through shared goals and small moments, not instant bonds. ASCEND, a free original anime by Asendify, adds one practical technique per episode.

This page is about anime and general life lessons, not mental health advice. If loneliness is affecting your daily life, talk to a GP or call Samaritans free on 116 123.

Key takeaways

  • MyAnimeList lists A Place Further Than the Universe (Sora yori mo Tooi Basho) as 13 episodes from January to March 2018; Mari Tamaki meets Shirase Kobuchizawa, who is saving to travel to Antarctica despite ridicule.
  • Natsume's Book of Friends (13 episodes, 2008) follows Takashi Natsume, who sees youkai, was passed between foster homes and closed himself off.
  • K-On! (13 episodes, 2009) follows Yui Hirasawa, who joins the Light Music Club thinking it is about simple instruments.
  • The NHS notes young people aged 16 to 34 are among the most at-risk groups for loneliness, and suggests connecting with others or volunteering.
  • The common thread: friendships grow from doing things together, again and again.

Keeping friendships going

Making friends is only half of it; these shows also show friendships surviving stress and distance. A few habits help in real life.

HabitExample
Regular check-insA short message every couple of weeks, not only when you need something
Remember the detailsAsk how the job interview or exam went
Make plans concrete"Saturday at 11?" rather than "we should meet up sometime"
Repair quicklyApologise and talk after a disagreement, as the characters eventually do
Celebrate their winsTurn up for their gig, race or graduation

None of these takes much time. Together they are what turns a friendly acquaintance into someone who would show up for you, which is the kind of friendship all three anime are really about.

Friendship and loneliness

Friendship anime can be comforting when you feel alone, and the NHS says feeling lonely is common, especially for young adults. But a show cannot replace real connection. If loneliness lasts or comes with low mood, it is worth talking to someone you trust or your GP. Small steps, like one message to an old friend this week, are a good start.

Which friendship anime is best for beginners to anime?

K-On! is light and easy to follow. A Place Further Than the Universe is a strong choice if you want a complete, emotional story in 13 episodes.

Is Natsume's Book of Friends scary?

It features spirits, but its tone is mostly gentle and reflective rather than horror.

Are there friendship anime for adults?

Yes. Many, including those above, appeal to adults because they deal with loneliness, ambition and growing up.

Can anime help with loneliness?

It can be comforting, but it is not a replacement for real connection. The NHS suggests connecting with others or volunteering.
