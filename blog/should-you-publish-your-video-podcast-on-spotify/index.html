Should I publish my video podcast on Spotify too, and should the edit be different from YouTube?

By the Asendify editorial team, led by Manthan Bhendkar · 4 min read · Published 22 November 2026 · Last updated 22 November 2026

If your show is hosted with Spotify for Creators, publishing the video there as well as on YouTube is usually worth it, because it costs you one extra upload and Spotify shows you how much of your audience actually watches. The edit should be a little different, though: on Spotify the same episode is heard as audio by many people, so it has to work without the picture. On YouTube the picture carries more weight, so cut it for viewers.

Key takeaways

  • Spotify says video is "available to shows hosted with Spotify but not to shows hosted somewhere else" through the standard upload flow; other hosts have a separate guide.
  • "Videos and video episodes are only available on Spotify." Other apps get the episode as audio via your RSS feed.
  • Spotify's recommended specs: 16:9, 1080p or higher, MP4, under 4 hours (up to 12 hours compatible). Don't add your own black bars.
  • Spotify's video analytics show total watch time, average watch time and Watch time %, which compares time spent watching against listening.
  • Because many people listen rather than watch, the Spotify edit must make sense as audio; YouTube's edit can lean more on visuals.

How video podcasts work on Spotify

From Spotify's publishing videos guide:

  • You must verify your account before your first video episode, and uploads happen on Spotify for Creators web.
  • The audio from your video is available through your RSS feed, so Apple Podcasts and other apps still get the episode as audio.
  • Requirements include one video track and one audio track, a 12-hour maximum, H.264 High Profile, 16:9 and AAC-LC audio at 192 Kbps or higher in stereo.

From Spotify's video specs page: 16:9 is recommended, other ratios are letterboxed or pillarboxed in some apps, and you should not add a border, letterbox or pillarbox yourself.

What Watch time % tells you

Spotify's video analytics page defines watch time as "the amount of time your video episode was visible on a viewer's screen", and Watch time % as "the time your audience spent time watching vs. listening." If Watch time % is low, most of your Spotify audience is effectively listening, so audio clarity matters more than B-roll there. If it's high, people are watching and visual pacing matters more.

Video metrics are on Spotify for Creators web (not the mobile app), and some take up to 48 hours to update.

The simplest workflow is one master edit made audio-first (everything understandable by ear), with a YouTube version that adds visual extras on top.

Worked example

Example: a two-host business podcast uploads its 55-minute episode to both platforms. On Spotify the Watch time % is low, so most of that audience is listening. The editor stops relying on "as you can see on this chart" and has the host read the key figure aloud. On YouTube the same section keeps the chart on screen with a zoom. Details are illustrative.

Will my Spotify video show on Apple Podcasts?

No. Spotify says video is only available on Spotify; other platforms get audio via RSS.

What aspect ratio does Spotify want for video podcasts?

16:9 is recommended. Other ratios may be letterboxed or pillarboxed in some apps.

How long can a Spotify video episode be?

Under 4 hours is recommended; up to 12 hours is compatible.

What does Watch time % mean on Spotify?

Spotify describes it as the time your audience spent watching versus listening.

Can I see Spotify video analytics on my phone?

No. Spotify says video analytics aren't available in the mobile app.
