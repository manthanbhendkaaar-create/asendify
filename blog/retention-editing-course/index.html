Retention Editing Course: How to Learn Editing That Keeps Viewers

By Manthan Bhendkar · 6 min read · Updated 18 October 2026

Retention editing means editing a video so more people watch more of it. It is less about software tricks and more about decisions: what to open with, what to cut, when to change the visuals, and how to keep a question open. If you are looking for a course or resource to learn it, this guide covers what it should teach and the ways to learn it.

Free tool: paste your video's intro script into the YouTube Hook Checker to get a score out of 100 with fixes (promise by 0:08, proof by 0:20, open loops and pacing). No sign-up.

It is written by Manthan Bhendkar, founder of Asendify, a retention-editing agency whose editing helped the finance channel Imperial grow from 200K to 500K subscribers in 8 months.

What a good retention editing course should cover

  • Reading retention data. How to read a retention graph second by second and find exactly where viewers leave.
  • Hooks. Building the first 5 to 30 seconds so viewers know they are in the right place and want to stay.
  • Open loops and structure. Teasing payoffs, delaying answers and stacking questions so there is always a reason to keep watching.
  • Pacing. Cutting pauses, filler and slow setups without making the video feel rushed.
  • Pattern interrupts and visual resets. Angle changes, B-roll, zooms, text and graphics at the moments attention dips.
  • Short-form vs long-form. How the same principles change for Shorts, Reels, TikTok and ads.
  • A feedback loop. Applying changes to real videos and checking the results, upload after upload.

Be careful with courses that only teach effects, transitions or software shortcuts. Those make videos look busier, not necessarily more watchable.
