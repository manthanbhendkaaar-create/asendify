What size should a YouTube thumbnail be?

By Manthan Bhendkar · 5 min read · Published 19 November 2026 · Last updated 19 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

YouTube now recommends 3840 x 2160 pixels for video thumbnails and 2160 x 3840 for Shorts, with a minimum of 640 pixels wide for videos (640 high for Shorts). Use a 16:9 image for videos, 9:16 for Shorts and 1:1 for podcast playlists, saved as JPG or PNG. Uploads are limited to 2 MB on mobile and 50 MB on desktop, and you need a verified account.

Key takeaways

  • Recommended size: 3840 x 2160 (videos) and 2160 x 3840 (Shorts), according to YouTube's help centre.
  • Minimum: 640 pixels wide for videos; 640 pixels high for Shorts.
  • File size: 2 MB on mobile for video thumbnails (10 MB for podcasts); 50 MB on desktop.
  • Custom thumbnails need a verified account; Shorts thumbnails can only be set in YouTube Studio on a computer.
  • Size is the easy part. A thumbnail must also be readable when tiny and must not mislead viewers about what is in the video.

Older guides often say 1280 x 720. That still meets the 16:9 ratio and the minimum width, but YouTube's current recommendation is higher, which keeps thumbnails sharp on large TV screens.

The same page notes that vertical videos with a 16:9 thumbnail get an auto-generated 4:5 image on the home, explore and subscriptions pages, while your custom thumbnail still appears on the watch feed and in watch history.

The rules your thumbnail must follow

YouTube's thumbnails policy bans, among other things, pornographic or sexually gratifying imagery, violent imagery meant to shock or disgust, graphic gore, vulgar language, and "a thumbnail that misleads viewers to think they're about to view something that's not in the video". A first violation usually brings a warning; repeat violations can lead to strikes, and the thumbnail help page says repeat offences can remove custom thumbnail privileges for 30 days.

How to make a thumbnail that works at every size

Thumbnails are seen far smaller than you design them. A few checks help:

  1. Design at 3840 x 2160, then view it at the size of a stamp. If you cannot read it, simplify.
  2. One subject, one idea. A face, an object or a result, not all three.
  3. Three words or fewer of text. Large, high-contrast and not repeating the title.
  4. Keep the bottom-right corner clear. The video length label sits there on most layouts.
  5. Check it in dark and light mode. A thumbnail that disappears on a dark background loses clicks.

Worked example: resizing an old thumbnail set

Example: a channel's archive uses 1280 x 720 JPGs, some saved at 3.5 MB.

  • Problem 1: uploading from a phone fails, because the mobile limit is 2 MB. The creator re-exports at 80% JPG quality, bringing files to about 600 KB.
  • Problem 2: on a TV, text looks soft. New thumbnails are designed at 3840 x 2160 and exported as JPG under 2 MB, or PNG when uploading from a desktop.
  • Problem 3: a vertical video uses a 16:9 thumbnail, so the home feed shows an auto-generated 4:5 crop. The creator adds a 9:16 thumbnail in YouTube Studio on a computer instead.

The fix took one afternoon and a template file, and every future thumbnail starts from the right canvas.

Thumbnail size checklist before upload

  • Canvas is 16:9 (videos) or 9:16 (Shorts).
  • Width is at least 640 pixels; ideally 3840.
  • File is JPG or PNG.
  • File is under 2 MB if you upload from a phone.
  • The main subject is still clear at about 160 pixels wide.
  • Nothing important sits under the duration label.
  • The image shows something that really appears in the video.

Keep a master template at the recommended size, so every new thumbnail starts from a canvas that already passes the first four checks.

Testing which thumbnail works

YouTube's title and thumbnail test lets you compare up to three thumbnails on long-form videos. YouTube says it optimises for overall watch time rather than click-through rate, and tests usually finish within two weeks. Results are labelled Winner, Performed Same or Inconclusive. Shorts are not eligible.

Common mistakes

  • Uploading tiny images. Under 640 pixels wide is rejected for videos.
  • Text that only works at full size. Most viewers see a thumbnail at a fraction of its design size.
  • Clutter. Three faces, arrows, circles and a paragraph of text compete for a stamp-sized space.
  • Mismatch with the opening. A thumbnail that promises something the first seconds do not show can raise clicks and lower watch time.
  • Forgetting Shorts. A 16:9 thumbnail on a vertical video is replaced by an automatic crop in key feeds.

Is 1280 x 720 still OK for a YouTube thumbnail?

It meets the 16:9 ratio and the 640-pixel minimum, but YouTube now recommends 3840 x 2160 for sharper results on large screens.

Why can't I upload a custom thumbnail?

YouTube requires a verified account. Custom Shorts thumbnails can only be added in YouTube Studio on a computer.

What is the maximum thumbnail file size?

2 MB for video thumbnails on mobile (10 MB for podcasts) and 50 MB on desktop.

Can a thumbnail get my channel a strike?

Yes. Thumbnails that break the Community Guidelines, including misleading ones, can be removed and may lead to a strike after a warning.
