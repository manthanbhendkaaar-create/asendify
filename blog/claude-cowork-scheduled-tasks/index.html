How do I use scheduled tasks in Claude Cowork?

By Manthan Bhendkar · 6 min read · Published 13 November 2026 · Last updated 13 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

To use scheduled tasks in Claude Cowork, open "Scheduled" in the left sidebar, click "New task", and either describe the job to Claude or set it up manually with a name, prompt, frequency and optional folder. Scheduled tasks are on all paid plans, run remotely even when your computer is off, and can be paused, edited or run on demand from the same page.

Key takeaways

  • There are two ways to create a task in Cowork: "Create with Claude" (a short conversation) or "Set up manually" (a form).
  • Manual frequencies are hourly, daily, weekly, weekdays or manual (on demand).
  • Tasks run remotely, so your computer can be off, unless the task needs local files or apps.
  • Start with one small, repeatable task you already understand, and check the first few runs closely.
  • Keep a person in the loop for anything involving money, messages sent on your behalf or important files.

What scheduled tasks are

Cowork is Anthropic's way of letting Claude carry out multi-step work for you. Anthropic's getting started guide describes it as bringing "the agentic architecture of Claude Code to knowledge work", so that instead of answering one prompt at a time, "Claude can take on complex, multi-step tasks and execute them on your behalf." Cowork is available on paid plans: Pro, Max, Ultra, Team and Enterprise.

A scheduled task is a Cowork job that repeats on a timetable. Anthropic's help article on scheduling recurring tasks says scheduled tasks run remotely and keep their cadence even when the computer is off or the app is closed.

Two ways to create a scheduled task

MethodHow it worksBest for

Anthropic's help article also notes that in the newer Claude experience you can describe the task and how often it should run in any conversation, answer Claude's follow-up questions, review the proposed name, schedule and instructions, and then select Schedule.

Step by step: your first scheduled task

  1. Pick a small, boring, repeatable job. A weekly summary of a folder of notes, a Monday list of open items, or a daily digest of new files are good starts.
  2. Run it once by hand first. Do the task in a normal Cowork session. Check the result is what you want.
  3. Open Scheduled in the left sidebar and click New task.
  4. Choose Create with Claude if this is your first task. Answer its questions about inputs, output and timing.
  5. Review the outline. Check the instructions, the frequency and which folders or connectors it will use.
  6. Click Schedule.
  7. Watch the first three runs. Open each run from the Scheduled page and check the output.
  8. Adjust. Edit the instructions or cadence from the same page, or pause the task if something looks wrong.

Anthropic's article says scheduled tasks work with your connectors and with files saved to your Claude account. If a task needs files that only exist on your computer, it depends on your machine being available.

Writing instructions that hold up week after week

A scheduled task runs without you watching, so the instructions have to stand on their own. Anthropic's best practices for getting started with Cowork recommend starting "with a real task you know well", giving rich context through files and connected apps, describing the deliverable, and reviewing the output. They also suggest making Claude ask you questions before it starts, which is exactly what the Create with Claude option does.

A good scheduled prompt says:

  • What to read: which folder, connector or file.
  • What to produce: format, length and where to save it.
  • What to do if something is missing: for example, "If there are no new files, say so in one line."
  • What not to do: for example, "Do not send emails; save a draft only."

Safety and approvals

Cowork lets you choose how much Claude can do without asking. Anthropic's getting started guide describes Manual, Auto and Skip permission modes, and advises using Skip only when you fully trust the task. It also says connectors can be set to "Always allow", "Needs approval" or "Blocked", and that Claude needs explicit permission before permanently deleting files. Anthropic recommends staying close to tasks with real consequences, such as money, messages sent on your behalf, or important files.

For scheduled tasks, a sensible rule is: let the task read and draft freely, but keep sending, paying and deleting behind your approval.

Example: a weekly client summary

Example: Sam runs a small bookkeeping practice. Every Friday he spends about 40 minutes writing a short status note for each of six clients from his meeting notes folder.

ItemSetting he chose
Task nameFriday client status drafts
FrequencyWeekly, Friday morning
InputHis synced client notes and his email connector (read only)
Instructions"For each client folder, summarise this week's notes in five bullet points, list open questions, and save a draft note. Do not send anything."
ApprovalDrafts only; he sends them himself

For the first three Fridays he checks every draft against the notes and fixes two prompt gaps: one client's folder name had changed, and the bullet points were too long. By week four the drafts need only light edits. The 40-minute figure and the outcome are his own estimate, not a measured result; your time savings will depend on your work.

Common problems and fixes

  • The task did something you did not expect. Tighten the "what not to do" part of the prompt and lower the approval mode.
  • It cannot find a file. Check the file is in a connected folder or saved to your Claude account; local-only files need your machine.
  • Output varies week to week. Give a fixed output format, such as a template with headings.
  • Too many tasks at once. Pause the ones you are not reviewing. A task nobody reads is not saving anything.

FAQ

Do Cowork scheduled tasks run when my computer is off?

Yes, according to Anthropic. Scheduled tasks run remotely and keep their cadence when the computer is off or the app is closed, except tasks that need local files or apps.

Which plans include scheduled tasks?

Anthropic says scheduled tasks are available on all paid plans: Pro, Max, Ultra, Team and Enterprise. Team and Enterprise admins control Cowork access.

Can I run a scheduled task straight away?

Yes. From the Scheduled page you can run a task on demand, as well as edit, pause, resume or delete it.

How often can a scheduled task run?

Manual setup offers hourly, daily, weekly, weekdays or manual runs. You can also describe a cadence when creating a task with Claude.

Is it safe to let a scheduled task send emails?

It is safer to have the task save drafts and approve sending yourself, at least until you trust it. Anthropic recommends staying close to tasks such as messages sent on your behalf.
