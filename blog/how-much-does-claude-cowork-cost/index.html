How much does Claude Cowork cost?

By Manthan Bhendkar · 5 min read · Published 19 November 2026 · Last updated 19 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

Claude Cowork has no separate price: it is included in Anthropic's paid Claude plans and is not available on the free plan. On Anthropic's pricing pages in November 2026, Pro costs $20 a month ($17 a month billed yearly), Max costs $100 a month for five times Pro's usage, and Ultra costs $200 a month. Prices exclude tax, and Team and Enterprise are priced separately for organisations.

Key takeaways

  • Cowork comes with paid plans only. Anthropic's help centre lists Pro, Max, Team and Enterprise, and the Cowork page also lists Ultra.
  • Pro: $20 a month, or $17 a month with $200 billed yearly. This is the 1x usage baseline.
  • Max: $100 a month, billed monthly only, with "five times the Pro plan's usage every five hours and every week".
  • Ultra: $200 a month, described as 10x Pro usage per week.
  • The real cost question is usage: multi-step Cowork tasks use far more of your limit than quick chat questions.

Cowork is part of the plan, not an add-on

Anthropic's help article Get started with Claude Cowork says Cowork needs a paid subscription and is available on Pro, Max, Team and Enterprise. There is no Cowork-only subscription to buy. You pay for a Claude plan, and Cowork, Claude Code and chat share that plan's usage limits.

The help centre's Max plan article lists Cowork among what the plan includes, "for longer multi-step tasks that run in the background", alongside Claude Code and monthly API credits.

Why usage matters more than the price tag

A chat question uses a small amount of your allowance. A Cowork task that reads a folder of files, runs sub-agents, browses the web and builds a spreadsheet uses much more. The Get started article says plainly that multi-step tasks consume more usage than quick questions, and suggests three habits:

  • Group related work into one task instead of many small ones.
  • Start a new conversation for unrelated work, so Claude is not rereading old context.
  • Check Settings > Usage to see where you stand and when your limit resets.

The Max article adds detail on how limits work: a five-hour limit, a weekly limit shared across models, and a fixed weekly reset time assigned to your account, which you can see under Settings > Usage. Anthropic also notes that extra limits may apply and that uninterrupted service is not guaranteed.

Which plan fits

  • Pro suits someone trying Cowork for a few tasks a week: a weekly report, tidying a folder, a research summary. If you hit limits often, that is the signal to move up.
  • Max suits people who run Cowork daily, use scheduled tasks or run long jobs with sub-agents. Five times the usage is the main reason to pay five times the price.
  • Ultra suits heavy daily users who keep hitting Max's limits.
  • Team and Enterprise suit businesses that need admin controls, such as restricting permission modes, managing connectors and plugins centrally, and audit features.

You can switch plans. The Max article says moving up to Ultra is prorated for the rest of the billing cycle, and switching from annual Pro to Max can leave an account credit. Anthropic does not offer standing discounts on individual plans.

Worked example

Example: a freelance marketer wants Cowork to build a client report every Monday and a content plan every Friday.

  • She starts on Pro at $20 a month. Each report task reads four CSV exports and builds a 10-slide deck.
  • In week two she runs both tasks plus several ad-hoc research jobs and hits the five-hour limit twice on Mondays.
  • She first tries the free fixes: one task per report instead of three, and a fresh conversation for each client. That cuts the overruns to once a week.
  • She still needs more headroom on busy days, so she compares the cost of the delay with the extra $80 a month for Max and decides to upgrade only for the months with the most clients.

Her real decision was not "what does Cowork cost" but "how much usage do my weekly tasks need", which she could only answer after two weeks of tracking.

Hidden costs to check

  • Connected services: connectors use your own accounts in tools like Google Drive or Slack. Those subscriptions are separate.
  • Plugins from partners may need an account with that partner's service.
  • Your time setting it up: writing instructions, connecting apps safely and testing a task usually takes longer than the first run.
  • Tax: prices on Anthropic's pages exclude applicable tax.

Is Claude Cowork free?

No. Anthropic's help centre says Cowork needs a paid subscription. The free plan does not include it.

Is Cowork included in Claude Pro?

Yes. Pro is listed among the plans that include Cowork. It has the lowest usage of the paid plans, so heavy users may hit limits.

Is there a yearly discount?

Pro costs $17 a month when billed yearly ($200 up front) instead of $20 monthly. Max is billed monthly only.

What happens when I hit my usage limit?

You wait for the limit to reset or upgrade. Check Settings > Usage for your reset time.

Do Team plans include Cowork?

Yes. Team and Enterprise both include Cowork; on Enterprise an owner has to switch some surfaces on first.
