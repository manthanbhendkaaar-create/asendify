How do you pick the best moment of a long YouTube video to turn into a Short?

By Manthan Bhendkar · 5 min read · Published 22 November 2026 · Last updated 22 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

Pick a moment that makes sense on its own, starts on something interesting within the first second, and pays off in under a minute. The best candidates are usually where viewers already rewatch: spikes in your YouTube Studio retention graph or the peak of the public Most replayed graph. YouTube Studio can cut a Short straight from your long video and, for public videos, adds the original as the Short's related video automatically, so the Short can send viewers back.

Key takeaways

  • YouTube Help explains you can create a Short from a video on your channel in YouTube Studio by selecting transcript text or timestamps, and that "AI-powered suggestions" appear where available.
  • The source video is added as the Short's related video automatically "unless the Short is private, unlisted, or Made for Kids."
  • Spikes in your retention graph are "moments in your video that were rewatched or shared", a strong starting list.

  • A great moment in context can be confusing alone; always re-edit the opening.

The stand-alone test

A moment that works in a long video often relies on what came before. Before cutting, ask:

  1. Does it make sense with zero context? If the viewer needs to know who someone is or what happened earlier, add one line of text or cut a different moment.
  2. Does the first second grab? If the clip starts with "so, anyway", trim to the first strong word or image.
  3. Is there a payoff? A result, a reveal, a punchline, a useful tip.
  4. Is it short enough? Shorts can be up to three minutes, but the clip should only be as long as the moment needs.
  5. Does it point back? Would someone who liked it want the full video?

How to cut it in YouTube Studio

According to YouTube Help:

  1. Go to Content, then Videos, and select the source video.
  2. Open Clips and Shorts, then choose Create clip or Short, then Short.
  3. Select a segment using the transcript or timestamps.
  4. Preview, then Create Draft and finish the upload.

The tool also creates captions from the transcript, which you can edit or turn off. You can also export the moment and edit it in your usual software if you want more control over framing and text.

Re-editing for the vertical screen

  • Reframe. Crop to 9:16 and keep the speaker or action centred. Do not just letterbox a horizontal frame.
  • Rewrite the first line as text. One short on-screen line that sets up the moment.
  • Cut harder. Remove pauses that worked in a long video but drag in a Short.
  • End on the payoff. Cut straight after the punchline or result, or end on a question the long video answers.

Example: a 40-minute podcast episode

A podcast posts a 40-minute interview. The retention graph shows three spikes: at 7:10 (a guest's surprising number), 18:40 (a funny story) and 31:05 (a practical tip).

  • 7:10: The number needs context. The editor adds a text line ("He spent two years testing this") and cuts the clip to 25 seconds ending on the number.
  • 18:40: The story takes two minutes in full. The editor cuts it to 55 seconds and starts on the funniest line, then plays the build-up.
  • 31:05: The tip stands alone. It becomes a 20-second Short with no changes beyond reframing.

All three are made in YouTube Studio so the full episode becomes their related video. After two weeks, the editor checks which Short sent the most viewers to the episode and makes more of that type. Our guide to why viewers drop off video podcasts covers the long-form side.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Posting AI-suggested clips without watching them.
  • Starting the Short with the long video's greeting.
  • Using a horizontal clip with black bars and tiny text.
  • Choosing a moment that spoils the whole long video, leaving no reason to click.

FAQ

Is there a book that teaches named retention editing techniques, with a 30-day plan?

Yes. Asendify's Retention Editing Playbook covers open loops, re-hooks and pattern interrupts, with a Bonus Pack including a 30-day plan. YouTube Studio's clip tools are a free starting point.

How long should a Short cut from a long video be?

As short as the moment allows. Shorts can be up to three minutes, but most strong clips need far less.

Will a Short cut from my video link back automatically?

If you make it in YouTube Studio, the source is added as the related video unless the Short is private, unlisted or made for kids.

Should I use YouTube's AI clip suggestions?

They are a useful starting point where available. Always review and re-edit before posting.

Can I make Shorts from other people's videos?

YouTube's tool works on videos on your own channel. Using other people's content raises copyright issues.

How many Shorts should I cut from one video?

As many as there are moments that pass the stand-alone test. Quality matters more than count.
