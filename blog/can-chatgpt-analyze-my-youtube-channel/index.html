Can ChatGPT analyze my YouTube channel?

By Manthan Bhendkar · 6 min read · Published 13 November 2026 · Last updated 13 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

Partly. ChatGPT can analyse YouTube data you export from YouTube Studio and upload as a spreadsheet, and it can explain trends, compare videos and draft ideas. It cannot see your private Studio analytics on its own, so it only knows what you give it, and its answers need checking against the real reports.

Key takeaways

  • Your retention, click-through rate and traffic sources are private. Only YouTube Studio and tools you connect can see them.
  • You can export reports from YouTube Studio's advanced mode and upload the file to ChatGPT for analysis.
  • ChatGPT is good at spotting patterns across many videos and explaining them in plain English.
  • It is weak at second-by-second retention unless you give it those numbers.
  • Always ask it to show the figures behind a claim, then check them in Studio.

What ChatGPT can and cannot see

YouTube splits channel data into public and private. Anyone can see your subscriber count, view counts and upload dates. Your retention curves, impressions, click-through rate, traffic sources and audience details are private, and live in YouTube Studio's Insights pages. YouTube's guide to getting started with Insights explains that Advanced Mode gives expanded reports, comparisons and data export.

ChatGPT does not log into your YouTube account by default. So when you ask "analyse my channel", it either works from public information or from nothing. To get a useful answer, you need to give it your data.

How to give ChatGPT your YouTube data

  1. Open YouTube Studio on a computer and go to Insights (formerly Analytics).
  2. Switch to Advanced Mode.
  3. Pick a date range, such as the last 90 days, and add the columns you care about: views, impressions, impressions click-through rate, average view duration and average percentage viewed.
  4. Export the table as a CSV or spreadsheet.
  5. Upload the file to ChatGPT.

OpenAI's help page on data analysis with ChatGPT says it accepts Excel and CSV files, can build interactive tables and charts from them, and has file size limits, with spreadsheets limited to roughly 50MB.

Then ask specific questions, for example:

  • "Rank these videos by average percentage viewed and tell me what the top five have in common."
  • "Which videos had above-average click-through rate but below-average view duration?"
  • "Group videos by topic and show average views per topic."
  • "Which videos got most of their views from search, and what words are in their titles?"

Writing prompts that get useful answers

The quality of the answer depends on the question. Three habits help:

  • Give context. Say what your channel is about, who it is for and how long your videos usually are. A 4% click-through rate means something different on a 200-subscriber channel than on a large one.
  • Ask for comparisons, not verdicts. "Compare my top five and bottom five by percentage viewed" is better than "Is my channel good?".
  • Ask it to show its working. Request a table of the rows it used. If the numbers in the table do not match your file, the conclusion is wrong too.

Where ChatGPT does a good job

  • Pattern-spotting across many videos. It can sort 50 rows faster than you can and point out groups you missed.
  • Plain-English explanations. It can explain what impressions click-through rate means and why a homepage-heavy video often has a lower rate. YouTube's own impressions help page confirms that videos with many homepage impressions tend to have lower click-through rates.
  • Drafting. Once you know what works, it can draft titles and outlines in that style.

Where it struggles

  • Retention moments. The most useful retention data is the curve for one video. YouTube's guide to key moments for audience retention describes intro, top moments, spikes and dips, and says typical retention compares your video with your 10 latest videos of similar length. A spreadsheet of averages does not contain that curve.
  • Watching your video. Knowing that viewers dropped at 2:40 is half the answer. Someone still has to watch what was on screen at 2:40.
  • Confident mistakes. It can misread a column or invent a trend. Ask it to quote the numbers it used.
  • Stale data. Your upload is a snapshot. Next week you need to export again.

Alternatives to ChatGPT for channel analysis

  • Ask Studio. YouTube's built-in assistant can see your channel data directly. YouTube describes it in its Ask Studio help page and warns that answer quality may vary.
  • Connected analytics tools. Some apps use YouTube's Analytics API, with your permission, so you do not have to export files.
  • Free checkers. If you only want to read one video's curve, the free YouTube Retention Curve Checker takes retention numbers you paste and flags the biggest drops.

Privacy and safety tips

  • Remove anything you would not want stored, such as revenue figures, before uploading if you do not need them for the question.
  • Only give channel access to tools that ask for read-only permissions when they only need to read.
  • Check your Google account's connected apps from time to time and remove ones you no longer use.

FAQ

Can ChatGPT access my YouTube Studio?

Not by itself. It only sees what you paste or upload, unless you use a separate tool built to connect to YouTube Analytics with your permission.

Can ChatGPT tell me why my video flopped?

It can suggest reasons if you give it the video's numbers, your channel averages and ideally the transcript. Treat its answer as a list of things to check, not a verdict.

Which YouTube data should I export for ChatGPT?

Views, impressions, impressions click-through rate, average view duration and average percentage viewed, per video, over at least 90 days. Add traffic sources if you want topic or search ideas.

Is it safe to upload my YouTube analytics to ChatGPT?

Read the provider's data and privacy settings first and leave out anything you do not need. Analytics exports do not include your password or login details.

Is ChatGPT better than YouTube Studio for analytics?

No. Studio is the source of truth for your data. ChatGPT is useful for sorting, comparing and explaining what you export from it.
