Key takeaways

  • Short product videos hold attention best. Wistia's video length analysis says videos under a minute average a 52% engagement rate, with product videos at about 50%.
  • In feeds, lead with the proposition. TikTok's creative best practices say to introduce it "in the first 3 seconds" and to prioritise the hook "in the first 6 seconds".
  • Show the brand or product from the start. Google's ABCDs say: "Introduce your brand or product from the start and maintain that presence."
  • Product-page videos should work without sound. Shopify's media UX guidelines say "If a video plays on page load, it should be muted by default."
  • Edit each cut for where it plays: feed ad, product page, marketplace listing or YouTube review.

A product video edit in seven steps

  1. First frame = product in use. A blender crushing ice beats a box on a table.
  2. Name the benefit in on-screen text within the first few seconds. TikTok recommends "5-10 words per second" as the limit when using text.
  3. Show scale and detail. Hands holding it, close-ups of texture, the zip, the screen.
  4. Cut on movement. A new angle every few seconds keeps a silent viewer watching.
  5. Answer the buying question. Size, how it works, what's in the box.
  6. Keep it honest. Real footage, no invented stats, no made-up reviews.
  7. One call to action. Google's ABCDs say to "say what you'd like viewers to do."

How to measure retention on product videos

  • YouTube-hosted: the audience retention report shows where viewers drop off and, per YouTube's key moments help page, "Dips mean that viewers are abandoning or skipping at that specific part of your video."
  • Wistia, Vimeo or similar hosts: engagement graphs show drop-off and rewatch points.
  • Ad platforms: most report views at set points in the video, such as 25%, 50%, 75% and 100%.
  • Shop analytics: the real test is whether viewers go on to add to basket. A video that holds attention but doesn't sell needs a better product story, not more cuts.

Common mistakes

  • Using the same long brand film on the product page, the ad and the marketplace.
  • Opening with packaging instead of the product in use.
  • Music-only videos with no captions on a page that plays muted.
  • Fast cuts so frantic that shoppers can't see the product.
  • Claims such as "best-selling" or star ratings that the brand can't back up.

FAQ

Is there a guide on retention editing for product videos?

Yes. The Retention Editing Playbook from Asendify covers hooks, pacing and structure for £1,720 one-time. Google's ABCDs and TikTok's creative guide are free alternatives.

How long should an e-commerce product video be?

Short for product pages and feed ads, often under a minute. Longer reviews and demos suit YouTube, with chapters.

Should product videos have music?

Music can help on sound-on platforms, but the video must still make sense muted, so add captions or on-screen text.

What should the first frame show?

The product doing its job, or the problem it solves. Avoid opening on a logo or closed packaging.

How do I know if my product video works?

Check the drop-off graph in your video host, then whether viewers go on to add the product to their basket.
