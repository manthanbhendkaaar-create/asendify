How many hashtags should you use on YouTube?

By the Asendify editorial team, led by Manthan Bhendkar · 5 min read · Published 20 November 2026 · Last updated 20 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

Use a small number of relevant hashtags, typically two to five. YouTube shows up to three "considered the most engaging" next to your title, and if a video has more than 60 hashtags, YouTube ignores every one of them. Hashtags must relate directly to the video and can't contain spaces. They help viewers browse a topic, but your title and thumbnail do far more to get clicks.

Key takeaways

  • You can add hashtags to a video's title or description, and to Shorts and YouTube Music playlists.
  • Up to three hashtags appear next to the title; YouTube picks the ones it considers most engaging.
  • More than 60 hashtags and YouTube ignores all of them on that video.
  • Hashtags unrelated to the video break YouTube's rules and can lead to removal.
  • Tags (the hidden keyword field) are different: YouTube says they play a minimal role in discovery.

What YouTube's rules say

YouTube's help page on using hashtags covers the essentials:

  • "You can add hashtags to a title and description when you upload a video."
  • "up to three hashtags that are considered the most engaging will appear by your video title."
  • "If a video or playlist has more than 60 hashtags, we'll ignore each hashtag on that content."
  • "Hashtags don't contain any spaces," so multi-word hashtags are written as one word, like #HomeWorkout.
  • "Don't add hashtags that are not directly related to the video or playlist."
  • "Hashtags in the title and description will link to a results page featuring other videos sharing that hashtag."

Hashtags that break the rules won't appear and can lead to content being removed.

YouTube's Edit video settings page says tags are useful for commonly misspelled search terms but otherwise "play a minimal role" in discovery. It also gives the limits: "Video titles have a character limit of 100 characters" and descriptions 5,000. Our page on how many tags a YouTube video should have covers tags in depth.

Why stuffing hashtags backfires

Beyond the 60-hashtag cut-off, YouTube's spam policy says: "We do not allow content, metadata or behaviour designed to take advantage of the YouTube community." It also covers "maliciously misleading titles, thumbnails, descriptions or imagery." A string of trending but unrelated hashtags is the kind of metadata that can fall foul of this, and it makes your description look spammy to real viewers.

There's also a simpler cost: hashtags in a title use up characters. With a 100-character limit and only part of the title visible on many screens, a hashtag at the front can push your actual hook out of view. Our guide to how long a YouTube title should be explains why the first words matter most.

For most long videos, a sensible default is two to five specific hashtags at the end of the description: one for the broad topic, one or two for the specific subject, and one for your series or channel if you use one.

Worked example: a cooking channel

Example: Maya uploads "I Tried Making Ramen From Scratch in 24 Hours". Her first draft description ends with 25 hashtags, including #viral, #fyp, #trending and #food.

Her 25 hashtags were under the 60 limit, so YouTube wouldn't have ignored them, but most were unrelated to the specific video and some, like #fyp, describe another platform. Her new set is short, specific and directly related, which is what YouTube's rules ask for, and her title now leads with the hook instead of a hashtag. Hashtags won't fix a weak title or thumbnail, though: that is where most clicks are won or lost.

Yes. Asendify's free YouTube Channel Roast scores your latest public uploads out of 100 and gives three fixes, with no sign-up. YouTube Studio analytics are a fair free alternative.

What happens if I use more than 60 hashtags on YouTube?

YouTube ignores every hashtag on that video or playlist.

How many hashtags show above a YouTube title?

Up to three, chosen by YouTube as the most engaging.

Should I put hashtags in my YouTube title?

You can, but they use up title characters. Most creators put them at the end of the description, where they can still appear by the title.

Do hashtags help YouTube SEO?

They help viewers browse related videos through hashtag pages. They matter far less than your title, thumbnail and how well the video holds viewers.

Are hashtags the same as tags on YouTube?

No. Hashtags are visible and clickable in the title or description; tags are hidden keywords that YouTube says play a minimal role in discovery.
