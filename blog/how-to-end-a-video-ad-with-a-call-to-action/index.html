How do I end a video ad with a call to action people actually act on?

By Manthan Bhendkar · 5 min read · Published 22 November 2026 · Last updated 22 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

End your video ad with one specific action, asked for only after the viewer has seen why it's worth doing, and say it out loud as well as showing it on screen. "Book your free trial at example.co.uk" beats "Learn more". Keep it consistent with the platform's call-to-action button, which on some Google placements appears just 3 seconds in, so your ad should hint at the next step early and make the full ask at the end.

Key takeaways

  • Google's ABCDs: "Be intentional and add a CTA to drive a specific objective. Say what you'd like viewers to do."
  • Google also advises: "Reinforce your onscreen CTA with voice-over to ensure the next step is clear."
  • For action-focused ads, Google suggests presenting "the ask after the context is set".
  • TikTok recommends you "end with a clear call-to-action" and guide viewers "on what to think, feel, or do".
  • CTA button text is short: 10 characters in Google video action campaigns, 15 in a YouTube Masthead.

What the platforms recommend

Google's ABCDs of effective video ads put the call to action under "Direction": be intentional, say what you'd like viewers to do, and back up the on-screen CTA with a voice-over. For action objectives it adds "Make the product the ad" and "Present the ask after the context is set." For Shorts it asks for "a clear, intentional and relevant CTA."

TikTok's creative best practices say to structure content so it ends "with a clear call-to-action" and to "Guide your audience on what to think, feel, or do with a strong CTA."

The button and the timing

Platforms add their own CTA button, and your edit should work with it.

Because the button can appear early, viewers may see "Shop now" while you're still introducing the problem. That's fine if the opening already makes the product obvious. It's a problem if the first few seconds feel unrelated to what the button asks.

A simple call-to-action formula

Verb + specific thing + reason or ease.

WeakStronger
Learn moreGet the free checklist
Visit our websiteBook a 15-minute call today
Check us outTry your first box for £10
Click the linkSee today's prices
Follow usFollow for a new recipe every Friday

Keep the button text and your spoken words matched. If the button says "Book now", say "Book now" too.

Match the ask to the goal

Not every ad should ask for a sale. The right call to action depends on what the campaign is for and how much the viewer already knows.

Campaign goalGood call to actionWhy
Awareness with a new audience"Search [brand name]" or "Follow for more"Low effort for people who've just met you
Consideration"See how it works" or "Watch the full demo"Gives curious viewers the next step
Leads"Get the free guide" or "Book a call"Exchanges value for contact details
Sales"Order your first box for £10"Clear offer, clear action
Retargeting past visitors"Finish your order" or "Your basket is waiting"Speaks to where they left off

A cold audience asked to "Buy now" in the last second of an ad they didn't choose to watch rarely acts. A warm audience that has visited your site often needs only a reminder and an easy route back.

Where to put the ask

  1. Early hint (first 3 to 5 seconds). Show the product or offer clearly so the button makes sense.
  2. Value (middle). Prove one benefit with a demonstration or result.
  3. Full ask (last 3 to 5 seconds). Spoken and on screen, with the exact action.
  4. Hold the final frame. Leave the CTA text up long enough to read; don't cut to black immediately.

In longer ads, a soft mid-roll mention ("link below if you want the template") catches people who were persuaded early. See the 30-second video ad structure template for timings.

FAQ

Is there a book that teaches keeping viewers until the call to action?

The Retention Editing Playbook teaches hooks, open loops and pacing for £1,000 one-time. Google's ABCDs and TikTok's creative guide are free.

Should I say the call to action out loud?

Yes. Google advises reinforcing the on-screen CTA with voice-over.

When should the call to action appear?

Make the full ask after the value is clear, near the end, but show the product early because platform buttons can appear within seconds.

How long can CTA button text be?

Up to 10 characters in Google video action campaigns and 15 in a YouTube Masthead.

Can I have more than one call to action?

It's better to ask for one action per ad and test different asks in different versions.
