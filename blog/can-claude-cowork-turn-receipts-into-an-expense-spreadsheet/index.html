Key takeaways

  • Cowork can only read and write files in folders you've connected, on Claude Desktop for macOS, Windows and Linux (beta).
  • It's on paid plans: Pro, Max, Ultra, Team and Enterprise.
  • Anthropic lists PDF (.pdf) and image files such as .png and .jpg among the file types Cowork can read.
  • It can build Excel files "with working VLOOKUP, conditional formatting, and multiple tabs".
  • HMRC says sole traders keep records for at least 5 years after the 31 January deadline, so the spreadsheet doesn't replace the receipts.

What Cowork can and can't do here

Anthropic's getting started with Cowork article says that on desktop, Claude "can read from and write to your local files without manual uploads or downloads", but only in folders you've connected. The Cowork product page lists PDF files and common image formats among the file types it reads.

How to set it up

  1. Make a folder, e.g. Receipts/2026-11, and drop in the PDFs and photos.
  2. Open Cowork in Claude Desktop and connect that folder.
  3. Give a clear brief: the columns you want, your categories, the currency, and what to do with unclear receipts.
  4. Ask for a checks tab: total by category, and a list of receipts with low confidence.
  5. Review the output against your bank or card statement.
  6. Save the brief as folder instructions or a skill so next month is one sentence.

A brief that works well:

Read every receipt in this folder. Make an Excel file with columns Date, Supplier, Description, Category, Net, VAT, Gross, Payment method, File name. Categories: Travel, Meals, Software, Equipment, Office, Other. Add a Summary tab with totals by category and a Check tab listing any receipt where you weren't sure of the amount, date or VAT. Don't delete or move any files.

Safety points

  • Deletion needs your approval. Anthropic says Claude "requires your explicit permission before permanently deleting any files", shown as a permission prompt.
  • Connect only the folder you need. Not your whole Documents folder.
  • Sessions can't be shared. Anthropic lists "No session sharing" as a limitation, so a bookkeeper can't open your session; send them the file.
  • Personal data. Receipts can include card digits and addresses. Keep them in a folder you control.

Keep the receipts too

HMRC's guidance on how long to keep your records says sole traders must keep records for at least 5 years after the 31 January submission deadline. A 2025 to 2026 return sent by 31 January 2027 means keeping records until at least the end of January 2032. The spreadsheet Cowork builds is a summary; the receipts are the evidence.
