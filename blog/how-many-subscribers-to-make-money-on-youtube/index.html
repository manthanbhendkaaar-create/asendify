How many subscribers do you need to make money on YouTube?

By Manthan Bhendkar · 6 min read · Published 14 November 2026 · Last updated 20 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

You need 500 subscribers to start earning through YouTube's Partner Program features like channel memberships, Super Thanks and Shopping, and 1,000 subscribers to earn ad revenue. Alongside subscribers you need watch time: currently 4,000 qualified watch hours in 12 months or 10 million Shorts views in 90 days for ads. YouTube has announced that from 1 February 2027 new applicants for ad revenue will need 8,000 watch hours or 20 million Shorts views instead.

Key takeaways

  • 500 subscribers plus 3 uploads in 90 days plus 3,000 watch hours (12 months) or 3 million Shorts views (90 days): fan funding and Shopping.
  • 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 watch hours (12 months) or 10 million Shorts views (90 days): ad revenue and YouTube Premium revenue.
  • From 1 February 2027, new ad-revenue applicants need 1,000 subscribers plus 8,000 watch hours or 20 million Shorts views.
  • From the same date, Shorts ad revenue requires at least 10 million Shorts views in a rolling 90 days, even for existing partners.
  • You also need 2-Step Verification, a linked AdSense account, a channel in an eligible country, and to follow monetisation policies.

The two levels of the YouTube Partner Program

From YouTube's overview of the expanded Partner Program:

LevelSubscribersPlusPlus one of
Fan funding and Shopping5003 valid public uploads in the last 90 days3,000 qualified watch hours in 12 months, or 3 million qualified Shorts views in 90 days
Ad revenue sharing and Premium revenue1,000-4,000 qualified watch hours in 12 months, or 10 million qualified public Shorts views in 90 days

Both levels also require following YouTube's channel monetisation policies, living in an eligible country or region (more than 100 are listed), 2-Step Verification on your Google account, and one AdSense for YouTube account linked through YouTube Studio. YouTube reviews the whole channel, usually within about a month. If you are rejected you can reapply after 30 days, or 90 days after later rejections.

Channels below the Shorts threshold stay in the programme and keep earning from long-form videos.

Can you make money on YouTube with 500 subscribers?

Yes, through fan funding (memberships, Super Chat, Super Stickers, Super Thanks) and Shopping, if you also meet the watch hours or Shorts views and upload requirements. Ad revenue needs 1,000 subscribers.

How much does YouTube pay per 1,000 views?

YouTube does not publish a fixed rate. Earnings depend on ad rates in your niche, viewer country, video length and how many viewers see ads.

Do Shorts views count towards monetisation?

Yes, as a separate route: 3 million Shorts views in 90 days for the lower tier, and 10 million for ad revenue until 1 February 2027, when new applicants need 20 million.

Do I lose monetisation if I am already in YPP after February 2027?

No. YouTube said existing partners do not have to meet the new entry thresholds, but they must accept the updated terms by 31 January 2027, and Shorts revenue needs 10 million Shorts views in a rolling 90 days.

Do private or unlisted videos count towards watch hours?

YouTube counts "qualified" and, for some thresholds, "public" watch hours and views. Check the YouTube Partner Program help page for exactly what qualifies.
