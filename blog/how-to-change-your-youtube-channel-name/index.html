How do you change your YouTube channel name, and will it hurt the channel?

By the Asendify editorial team, led by Manthan Bhendkar · 4 min read · Published 23 November 2026 · Last updated 23 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

Open YouTube Studio, go to Customisation, then Profile, type the new name and click Publish. YouTube lets you change the channel name twice within 14 days, and the change doesn't touch your Google Account name. The main cost is that a verified channel loses its verification badge. Your videos, views and subscribers stay; your handle and URL are separate settings and only change if you change them.

Key takeaways

  • Path: YouTube Studio, Customisation, Profile, edit the name, Publish. On mobile: your channel page, pencil icon, save.
  • Limit: "You can change your channel name twice within a 14-day period" (YouTube Help).
  • "Changing your name will remove your verification badge"; you can reapply, but it takes time.
  • The channel name, handle (@name) and channel URL are three different things with their own rules.
  • Changing your handle releases the old one after a 14-day hold, so someone else could take it.

Step by step

On a computer

  1. Sign in to YouTube Studio.
  2. Select Customisation, then Profile.
  3. Enter the new channel name. If the box won't update, delete any name translations first (YouTube Help).
  4. Click Publish.

On the YouTube app

  1. Go to your channel page.
  2. Tap the pencil icon next to the name.
  3. Edit and save.

The new name shows only on YouTube. Your Google Account name and Gmail display name don't change.

Will a new name hurt views?

YouTube doesn't say that a name change affects recommendations, and your videos, watch history and subscribers stay attached to the channel. The real risks are human ones:

  • Recognition. Regular viewers know your old name and thumbnail style. A sudden change can make your video look like a stranger's in their feed for a while.
  • Search. People who search the old name may not find you straight away. Mention the change in a community post or video.
  • Verification. If you have a badge, you lose it and have to reapply.
  • Handle mismatch. If you rename the channel but keep an old handle, people get two names. Changing the handle too means the old one is released after 14 days, during which both URLs work.

When a name change is worth it

  • The old name is a joke or nickname that no longer fits the content.
  • The name is hard to spell or say, so people can't search for it.
  • You've moved niche, and the name now misleads new viewers.
  • The name clashes with another brand.

If none of those apply, the time is usually better spent on titles and thumbnails, which every viewer sees before they ever look at your channel name. See what a YouTube handle is and how to choose one and the free tool that scores a channel out of 100.

Worked example

Example: a creator called "Tom Plays Stuff" now makes budget cooking videos. He renames the channel "Cheap Plate", changes his handle to @cheapplate the same week, posts a short community update, and keeps his face and colour style in thumbnails so regulars still recognise him. Within the 14-day hold, he checks the old handle URL still works and updates the link in his Instagram bio. Details are illustrative.

FAQ

Yes. Asendify's YouTube Channel Roast is free, needs no sign-up and scores the channel out of 100 with three fixes.

How many times can I change my YouTube channel name?

Twice within a 14-day period, according to YouTube Help.

Will I lose subscribers if I change my channel name?

The subscribers stay attached to the channel. Some viewers may not recognise you at first, so announce the change.

Does changing my channel name change my URL?

No. The handle and URL are separate settings.

Why can't I change my YouTube channel name?

You may have hit the two-changes-in-14-days limit, or you may need to delete name translations first.

Do I lose my verification badge if I change the name?

Yes. YouTube removes the badge, and you can reapply.
