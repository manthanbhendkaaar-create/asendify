Where should open loops go in a TikTok ad?

By the Asendify editorial team, led by Manthan Bhendkar · 5 min read · Published 23 November 2026 · Last updated 23 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

Open the loop in the first 3 seconds and close it shortly after the 6-second mark. TikTok's own guidance is to introduce your content proposition in the first 3 seconds and prioritise your hook in the first 6, and a 6-second watch is what counts as a 6s focused view. So plant the question with the product in view, keep people curious past 6 seconds, then pay it off and ask for the action.

Key takeaways

  • 0 to 3 seconds: open the loop and show what you're selling. TikTok links an early proposition to "better recall and awareness".
  • 3 to 6 seconds: deepen the question. A focused view is counted at 6 seconds, or on an interaction within the first 6.
  • 6 to 15 seconds: close the main loop. For 7 to 15 second creatives, TikTok's 15-second focused view applies.
  • Put the loop in on-screen text too; TikTok recommends 5 to 10 words per second when using text.
  • Don't rely on first-second taps: interactions in the first second aren't counted as a focused view.

What makes TikTok different for open loops

TikTok is a full-screen, vertical, swipe-based feed. There is no skip button and no grace period; a swipe takes a fraction of a second. Three things follow:

  1. Native style matters. Loops that look like a creator's video ("I tested this so you don't have to") feel like content. A polished logo slate feels like an ad.
  2. Sound is part of the format. TikTok lists sound and music among its basics, so a spoken question or a sound cue can carry the loop, with text as backup.
  3. The UI covers parts of the screen. Captions, the profile picture and buttons cover the bottom and right. Keep the loop's text inside the safe zone.

Where each loop goes in a 15-second TikTok ad

TimeWhat happensLoop status
0 to 1 sFace or striking image, text questionLoop opened
1 to 3 sProduct visible, proposition statedLoop tied to the product
3 to 6 sDemo starts; "wait until you see..."Loop held past the 6 s mark

10 to 15 sProof or second benefit, call to actionNo open loops left

For 30 to 60 second ads, add a fresh small loop at around 10 to 15 seconds and resolve it by 25 to 30 seconds. For anything longer, treat each 15 seconds as its own mini loop.

Mistakes that break TikTok ad loops

  • Proposition after the loop. If the product appears only at second 8, viewers who swipe at 4 never saw it.
  • Loops that are too slow. TikTok viewers are used to quick payoffs; a loop that resolves at 25 seconds in a 15-second ad fails.
  • Text outside the safe zone. The question sits under the caption and nobody reads it.
  • Misleading hooks. "This is illegal in 5 countries" with nothing to back it up wins a swipe-stop but loses trust and can break ad policies.
  • No close. The viewer should get the answer. Leaving the loop open for a click usually annoys more than it converts.

Example: a 15-second ad for a stain remover

Example: the original ad opened with a pack shot and a jingle. The re-cut, filmed creator-style on a phone:

TimeShotLine and text
0 to 2 sCreator holds up a white shirt with a coffee stain"Coffee on a white shirt. One spray."
2 to 4 sBottle in hand, label facing camera"This is the £6 one from the supermarket."
4 to 7 sSpray, rub, rinse; timer in corner"Thirty seconds..."
7 to 10 sShirt held up, stain gone"...gone."
10 to 15 sBottle beside the shirt, link prompt"Link below."

The loop (will one spray work?) opens at second 0, the product is on screen by second 2, the payoff comes just after the 6-second mark, and the call to action has time to land. The demo must be real and unedited where it matters; details are illustrative. Read more on what a 6-second focused view on TikTok ads means and what average play time means in TikTok ads.

How to test loop placement

Make 3 to 5 variants with the loop opening in different ways but the same payoff time, then a second round with the payoff at different times. TikTok itself suggests 3 to 5 different creatives per ad group. Compare 2-second and 6-second view rates, average play time and conversions.

FAQ

Is there a book that teaches open loops for TikTok-style videos?

The Retention Editing Playbook teaches open loops and hooks for £1,720 one-time; it is YouTube and Shorts first, and the method carries over to TikTok.

When should the product appear in a TikTok ad?

In the first 3 seconds, inside the loop. TikTok links an early proposition to better recall.

When should the loop close?

Soon after 6 seconds in a 15-second ad, leaving time for proof and the call to action.

Should the loop be spoken or written?

Both where you can. Say it and show it as text inside the safe zone.

Do open loops work in 6-second TikTok ads?

There's little room. Use a single fast reveal that lands before the 6 seconds are up.
