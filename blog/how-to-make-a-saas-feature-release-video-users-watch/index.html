How do I make a SaaS feature release video that users actually watch?

By Manthan Bhendkar · 4 min read · Published 23 November 2026 · Last updated 23 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

Lead with the annoyance the feature removes ("no more exporting to a spreadsheet to compare months"), show the feature working in realistic data within the first few seconds, and keep the video to roughly 30 to 90 seconds focused on one feature. Caption it so it works muted, and put it where users will actually meet the feature: in-app, in the changelog and in the release email, not only on social. Release videos fail when they open on a logo, list five features at once and talk about "excited to announce".

Key takeaways

  • Wistia's 2026 data reports that videos under a minute average a 52% engagement rate and product videos under a minute 50%.
  • Nielsen Norman Group's video guidelines say "shorter is often better", warn against introductions that "don't get to the point quickly enough", and recommend one short video per step for multistep processes.
  • NN/g also advises including "captions or other text within the video", because many people won't or can't play sound.
  • Google's ABCDs advise you to "avoid doing too much" and to "say what you'd like viewers to do".
  • One feature, one video. Bundle a "what's new" round-up separately if you must.

A 60-second structure

TimeSectionWhat to show
0 to 5 sThe painThe old, clunky way, or a one-line statement of it
5 to 15 sThe resultThe feature's output: the chart, the automation running, the finished task
15 to 45 sHow to use itTwo or three real clicks, zoomed in, cursor highlighted
45 to 55 sOne tip or limitA useful detail, or who it's available to (plan, role)
55 to 60 sWhere to find itExact menu path or a button

Showing the result before the steps is an open loop: users want to know how to get that output, so they watch the steps.

Editing tips for screen recordings

  1. Record at a sensible window size and zoom in during editing so text is readable on a phone.
  2. Cut every wait. Loading spinners and typing can be sped up or jump-cut.
  3. Highlight clicks and add short on-screen labels for each step.
  4. Use realistic data, with no real customer information.
  5. Voiceover plus captions. NN/g found captions matter because many people browse muted.
  6. Keep the UI current. Re-record when the interface changes, or the video will confuse users.
  7. State availability honestly. If the feature is limited to a plan, beta or region, say so.

How to tell if it worked

Compare feature adoption (users who used it in the first week) between people who saw the video and those who didn't, and check where viewers drop off in your video host's analytics. A drop before the result appears means the opening is too slow.

FAQ

Is there a book that teaches how to open a product video so people keep watching?

Yes. The Retention Editing Playbook covers hooks and pacing for £1,720 one-time. NN/g's video guidelines are free.

How long should a feature release video be?

About 30 to 90 seconds for one feature; shorter in-app versions work well.

Should I make one video for each feature?

Yes, where possible. A round-up of several features can link to the individual videos.

Do release videos need a voiceover?

Not always, but they need on-screen text or captions so they work muted.

Where should the release video go?

In-app next to the feature, in the changelog, and linked from the release email.
