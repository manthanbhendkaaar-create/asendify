How do I retarget people who watched my video ad, and what should the second video show?

By Manthan Bhendkar · 5 min read · Published 23 November 2026 · Last updated 23 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

Build an audience of people who watched your first video, split by how much they watched, then show them a different, shorter video that picks up where the first left off: proof, a demo of the bit they didn't see, or a clear offer. Meta, TikTok and Google Ads all let you build these audiences from video views. Re-showing the same ad mostly wastes money on people who already decided to skip it.

Key takeaways

  • TikTok lets you build audiences from ad viewers at 2 seconds, 6 seconds, 25%, 50%, 75% and 100%, with lookbacks up to 180 days, and needs at least 1,000 people to target.
  • Meta now groups video viewers into All (at least 3 seconds), Engaged and Highly Engaged viewers, replacing its older seven thresholds.
  • Google Ads builds YouTube segments from actions such as "Viewed any video from a channel" and "Viewed certain videos", once your channel is linked.
  • How far someone watched tells you what they still need to see.
  • Make the second video for the viewer you have, not a cold audience.

Match the second video to how far they watched

Watched 25 to 50%Interested, but lost before the point landedLead with the payoff they missed10 to 20 s

Watched 100% and clicked, didn't buyClose to buyingHandle the main objection; show checkout is simple6 to 15 s

The rule: don't make people sit through what they've already seen. Open on something new in the first second, so returning viewers recognise it's a follow-up and not a repeat.

How to edit a follow-up video

  1. Open with a callback, not the same hook. "Still thinking about it?" or the product in use, so it feels like part two.
  2. Skip the intro. They know who you are; get to the new information.
  3. Answer one question. Price, how it works, or does it work for someone like me.
  4. Show proof. A real customer clip (with permission), a demo, or a before/after you can stand behind.
  5. End with one clear action. Same offer as the landing page.

FAQ

Is there a book that teaches open loops and callbacks for video?

Yes. The Retention Editing Playbook (Asendify) covers open loops and pacing for £1,720 one-time. Platform creative guides are free alternatives.

Can I retarget people who watched my organic videos?

Yes on several platforms. Google's "Viewed any video from a channel" includes organic views, and TikTok has separate options for organic videos.

How big does a retargeting audience need to be?

TikTok requires at least 1,000 people in a custom audience; Google lists need at least 100 active users.

Should I show the same ad again?

Usually not. A follow-up that adds something new works better than a repeat.

How long should a retargeting video be?

Shorter than the first, often 6 to 20 seconds, because warm viewers already know the basics.
