How do I edit a client case study video that people watch to the end?

By the Asendify editorial team, led by Manthan Bhendkar · 4 min read · Published 23 November 2026 · Last updated 23 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

Open on the result or the moment of change, not the company logo; tell one story (problem, turning point, result) with the customer as the hero; cut every line that repeats; and put the call to action where interested viewers are still watching. Make two versions: a 60- to 90-second cut for social and email, and a longer one for the client's website and sales team. Every number the customer says on camera needs the client's permission and evidence behind it.

Key takeaways

  • Wistia's length data says videos under a minute average a 52% engagement rate (testimonials 46%), and that longer case studies should "lead with value and answer real questions".
  • Wistia notes the first, second and fourth quarters of a video are "prime spots" for a lead form or call to action.
  • Google's ABCDs advise getting "to the heart of the story faster" and to "feature people to help you tell a better product story".
  • The ASA says ads "must not feature a testimonial without permission" and marketers must hold evidence it's genuine.
  • One customer, one problem, one turning point beats a tour of every feature.

Place an early, soft call to action in the long version (a link to "see how it works") rather than only at the end, in line with Wistia's point that the first quarters are strong spots.

Interview to edit: what to keep

  1. Transcribe the interview and highlight lines that are specific, emotional or surprising.
  2. Find the hook line. Usually a short, concrete result ("We stopped losing Friday afternoons to invoices") or a vivid problem ("I was doing payroll at midnight").
  3. Cut repetition hard. Customers tend to say the same thing three ways; keep the best one.
  4. Cover cuts with real footage. B-roll of the customer actually using the product keeps the eye busy and hides jump cuts honestly.
  5. Caption everything. Many viewers start muted.
  6. Check every claim. If the customer says "saved us 40%", the client needs evidence and permission before it runs as an ad.

FAQ

Is there a book that teaches story structure for case study videos?

Yes. The Retention Editing Playbook covers open loops and pacing for £1,720 one-time. Wistia's video length guide is free.

How long should a case study video be?

Make a short cut of about 60 to 90 seconds for social and email, and a longer one for the website if the story needs it.

What's the difference between a case study and a testimonial?

A testimonial is a short endorsement. A case study tells the story: problem, turning point and result.

Should the client's logo open the video?

Brand early, but don't open on a logo animation. Show the brand within the story from the start.

Can the customer quote figures on camera?

Yes, if they're accurate and the client can support them. Objective claims in ads need evidence.
