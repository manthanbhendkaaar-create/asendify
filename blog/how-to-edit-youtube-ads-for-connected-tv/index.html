How should I edit a YouTube ad that runs on connected TVs?

By Manthan Bhendkar · 4 min read · Published 22 November 2026 · Last updated 22 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

Edit it as a horizontal, sound-on story for a big screen, brand it early, and don't rely on a click: say and show where to go, and let Google add the QR code rather than burning one into the video. Google's help page on QR codes for connected TV tells advertisers not to embed QR codes in video files for YouTube Select, because they can hurt the experience on mobile and desktop, and to use the system-generated codes instead.

Key takeaways

  • Google Ads has a "TV screens" device type (since October 2018) covering smart TVs, games consoles and streaming devices, and you can give TVs different creative.
  • 30-second non-skippable ads in video reach campaigns run mainly on connected TVs and must be horizontal.
  • Google can add a QR code, CTA and "send to phone" button on CTV: after 5 seconds on Select/Reservation non-skippable ads, and from the start of the skippable period on Demand Gen and Performance Max.
  • Don't burn a QR code into the video for YouTube Select; Google says it can hurt mobile and desktop viewing.
  • TV viewing is often shared: a Nielsen study commissioned by Google found 26% of YouTube viewing on TV involves multiple adults.

Google's announcement of the TV screens device type says advertisers can tailor creative for connected TVs and set a separate bid adjustment. In a 2022 Google article on YouTube CTV, DocuSign describes being hesitant at first precisely because viewers "cannot click through" on a TV.

Which lengths run on TV

Google's video reach campaigns page lists 30-second non-skippable ads as running "mainly on connected TVs", with assets of 16 to 30 seconds, and says "30-second video assets must be horizontal." Square or vertical versions won't run. Skippable in-stream and 6-second bumpers can also reach TV screens when your targeting includes them.

How the QR code appears

From Google's QR code help page:

Campaign typeWhen the QR code shows
YouTube Select and Reservation, non-skippable in-streamAfter 5 seconds, with CTA text and a "send to phone" button for signed-in viewers
Demand Gen and Performance Max, skippable in-streamFrom the start of the skippable period
Demand Gen and Performance Max, ShortsOn screen, viewers can swipe away

The QR code's position and timing are fixed, and it only appears on TVs that can resize the player to show it beside the video. Older TVs don't support it.

An edit plan for a 30-second CTV ad

SecondsWhat happens
0 to 5The hook and the brand, visible on a large screen; no slow logo build
5 to 20The story or demo, with clear voiceover (the room is likely listening)
20 to 27Proof and the offer, short readable text
27 to 30Say and show the URL or brand name to search; Google adds the QR code where available

Editing habits:

  1. Keep important visuals clear of the edges. A QR panel resizes the player, so leave breathing room around key text.
  2. Write for a room. Voiceover and dialogue work better than tiny on-screen text when viewers sit across a room.
  3. Brand early. Viewers may look away mid-ad; a logo only at the end is easy to miss.
  4. Say the next step aloud. "Search for..." or "Visit..." works for people who won't scan a code.
  5. Make a separate TV cut. Don't stretch a vertical Shorts ad onto a TV.

FAQ

Is there a book that teaches YouTube retention, including on TV?

Yes. The Retention Editing Playbook covers hooks and pacing for £1,720 one-time. Google's free help pages are an alternative for the ad setup.

Can people click YouTube ads on a TV?

Not like on a phone. Google offers QR codes and a "send to phone" button on eligible CTV ads.

Should I put a QR code in my YouTube TV ad?

For YouTube Select, Google says not to embed one; use the system-generated QR code.

What aspect ratio for YouTube CTV ads?

Horizontal 16:9. Google says 30-second assets must be horizontal.

Can I target only TV screens?

Yes. Google Ads has a TV screens device type you can target and adjust bids for.
