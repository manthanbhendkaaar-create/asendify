How should I edit a video ad for X (Twitter) so people keep watching?

By Manthan Bhendkar · 5 min read · Published 22 November 2026 · Last updated 22 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

Keep it to 15 seconds or less, add captions or text overlays, show your product prominently, and land your key visual within the first three seconds. X supports video ads up to 2 minutes 20 seconds, but its own specs recommend 15 seconds or less, and for its full-screen vertical viewer, which plays with sound on by default, X says "the first three seconds are critical."

Key takeaways

  • X: "15 seconds or less is recommended. Up to 2:20 is supported."
  • "Closed captioning or text overlays are strongly recommended."
  • Videos under 60 seconds loop, so a clean loop can earn a second watch.
  • Vertical Video Ads run full screen with sound on by default; X recommends 9:16 and a key visual at the three-second mark.
  • Overlays in the vertical viewer disappear after two seconds, and the call-to-action button appears after one second.

Two places your X video ad can run

X's video ads appear in the Home timeline and, as Vertical Video Ads, in the full-screen vertical viewer. They behave differently, so it helps to know which you're editing for.

What X recommends for the creative

From the specs page:

  1. Brand throughout. If you use a logo, X suggests keeping it persistent in the upper-left corner.
  2. Feature the product prominently. It drives consideration.
  3. Add captions or text overlays. Strongly recommended for every video ad.

From the vertical page:

  1. Use 9:16. X says 16:9 and 1:1 can run in the viewer but are "not ideal for this surface."
  2. Design with sound in mind, and caption spoken or voice-over audio.
  3. Put your key visual by the three-second mark.
  4. Plan around overlays. The viewer's overlays disappear after two seconds of watch time, and the CTA button appears after one second.

An edit plan for a 15-second X video ad

SecondsWhat happensText
0 to 1Movement or a face, logo upper leftNone or 2 to 3 words
1 to 3The key visual: product in action or the resultThe main promise
3 to 10One or two proofs, quick cutsOne short line each
10 to 15The ask, then a frame that loops back to the startCall to action

Because videos under 60 seconds loop, design the final frame so it flows back into the first. A viewer who watches twice gives your message a second chance.

Captions are not optional

Even in the sound-on vertical viewer, captions help people follow along. The W3C describes captions as a text version of the speech and non-speech audio "needed to understand the content", used by people who are Deaf or hard of hearing and others who can't hear the audio. In the timeline, captions or text overlays carry the message for anyone scrolling with the sound off.

Testing

Make two or three cuts that differ only in the first three seconds and run them against the same audience. Compare how many viewers watch past the opening and how many click. Keep the winning opening and test a fresh one against it. Change one thing at a time, so you know what made the difference.
