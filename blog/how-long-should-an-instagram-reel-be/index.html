How long should an Instagram Reel be to keep people watching?

By Manthan Bhendkar · 5 min read · Published 23 November 2026 · Last updated 23 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

An Instagram Reel can now be up to 3 minutes long and still be recommended to people who don't follow you, so there is no single right length. Make it as long as the idea needs and no longer: quick tips, jokes and reveals usually work best in well under 30 seconds, tutorials and stories can take a minute or more. Because Instagram predicts whether people will watch a reel all the way through, a short reel people finish beats a long one they abandon.

Key takeaways

  • Adam Mosseri announced in January 2025 that Reels can be uploaded at up to 3 minutes, up from 90 seconds.
  • Instagram says reels up to 3 minutes are now eligible to be recommended to non-followers in the Reels tab and Explore; before, only reels up to 90 seconds were.
  • In May 2024 Instagram had advised creators to avoid reels over 90 seconds because it did not recommend them as widely; that guidance moved with the new limit.
  • Instagram's ranking explainer lists the chance someone will watch a reel all the way through among its most important predictions.
  • The Retention chart in each reel's insights shows whether your length was right.

What Instagram has said about length

Instagram's creator blog post Finding success on reels in 2025 (January 2025) says creators can publish reels of up to 3 minutes, and that reels up to 3 minutes "can now reach non-followers" in the Reels tab and Explore, where previously only reels up to 90 seconds were eligible. Marketing Dive reported that at a May 2024 creator event Instagram had advised against reels over 90 seconds because it did not recommend them as widely, and that at a March 2025 event its tips were the same apart from the new 3-minute limit.

Instagram's ranking explainer says its most important Reels predictions include how likely you are to reshare a reel, watch it all the way through, like it and go to the audio page.

Reel typeLength to start withWhy
Joke, reaction, reveal5 to 15 sOne beat; easy to finish and rewatch
Quick tip or hack15 to 30 sOne idea, shown once
Tutorial or recipe30 to 90 sEnough to show every step
Story, explainer, mini-vlog60 s to 3 minOnly if each section earns its place

These ranges are working starting points, not Instagram rules. The only official limits are the 3-minute upload and recommendation thresholds.

Long or short: how to decide

  • Write the reel as one sentence. If the sentence is "this is how to fold a fitted sheet", it is a 20-second reel. If it is "how I paid off £8,000 of debt in a year", it may need two minutes.
  • Cut, then measure. Remove every pause and repeat. The length that is left is the length the idea needs.
  • Check the Retention chart. In August 2025 Instagram added a per-reel Retention chart; Social Media Today quotes Instagram saying "the flatter the line, the more engaged your audience is". A steep drop at 20 seconds on a 60-second reel tells you where to cut next time.
  • Do not pad. Stretching a 20-second idea to 90 seconds lowers the share of people who finish.

Keeping a long reel watchable

  1. Hook in the first second. The skip rate in insights counts views skipped in the first 3 seconds.
  2. Tell people what they will get. "Five steps, the fourth is the one most people skip" gives a reason to stay.
  3. Show progress. Number the steps on screen.
  4. Change the picture often. New angle, close-up or B-roll every few seconds.
  5. Pay off at the end. Hold the result or the best step until late, then end straight after it.

FAQ

Is there a book that teaches pacing for short videos?

Yes. The Retention Editing Playbook (Asendify) covers hooks and pacing for £1,720 one-time. Instagram's creator blog is a free alternative.

What is the maximum length of an Instagram Reel?

Instagram announced in January 2025 that Reels can be up to 3 minutes long when uploaded.

Will a 3-minute reel be shown to non-followers?

Instagram says reels up to 3 minutes are now eligible for recommendation in the Reels tab and Explore.

Are shorter reels always better?

No. A reel should be as long as its idea. A short reel that drags still loses people; a longer one that holds them can do well.

How do I know if my reel was too long?

Open the reel's Retention chart. A steep, early drop suggests the opening or pacing needs work; a drop near the end suggests the ending runs on.
