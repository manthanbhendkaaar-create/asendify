How do you get your first YouTube video editing clients?

By Manthan Bhendkar · 4 min read · Published 23 November 2026 · Last updated 23 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

Start with proof, not a CV. Make three short sample edits in the niche you want to work in, pick ten small creators whose videos you could clearly improve, and send each a short re-edit of one of their own clips with a plain note on what you changed and why. When someone says yes, agree scope and price in writing, ask for channel access through YouTube's permission roles rather than a password, and keep records from day one.

Key takeaways

  • YouTube's channel permissions let an owner add an Editor who "Can edit everything" but "Can't manage permissions", or an Editor (limited) without revenue data, so you never need a client's password.
  • A Viewer role "Can view (but not edit) all channel details", which is useful for checking retention before you pitch changes.
  • In the UK you must register for Self Assessment as a sole trader "if you earn more than £1,000 in a tax year".
  • The £1,000 trading allowance is a tax exemption for small amounts of self-employment income.
  • Creators hire editors who can show they'll keep viewers watching, so lead with before-and-after examples.

Example: from cold pitch to first invoice

Example: Kai, 19, wants to edit for personal-finance creators.

  1. He makes three sample edits from public-domain or self-shot footage.
  2. He re-edits the first 45 seconds of a small creator's latest video, moving the main point up front and cutting a long introduction.
  3. He emails it with: "I tightened your intro and moved your best line to the start. Happy to do your next full video as a paid trial."
  4. The creator says yes, adds Kai as Editor (limited) in YouTube Studio, and they agree two revision rounds by email.
  5. Kai records the payment and registers for Self Assessment once his yearly earnings pass £1,000.

Figures and outcomes are illustrative; replies vary a lot by niche and pitch quality.

FAQ

Is there a retention editing course with assignments?

Yes. Asendify's Retention Editing Accelerator has 24 lessons with an assignment in each. Practising on public-domain footage is a free alternative.

How should a client give me access to their channel?

Through YouTube Studio's channel permissions, usually as Editor or Editor (limited). You should never need their password.

Do I need to register as self-employed to edit videos?

In the UK, you must register for Self Assessment once your self-employment earnings pass £1,000 in a tax year.

Should I work for free to get started?

A short re-edit of a clip as a pitch is common; full free videos usually aren't necessary. Offer a paid trial instead.

How many sample edits do I need?

Three strong samples in one niche are usually more convincing than many in mixed styles.
