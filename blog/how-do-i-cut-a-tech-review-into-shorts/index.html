How do I cut a tech review into Shorts?

By the Asendify editorial team, led by Manthan Bhendkar · 3 min read · Published 26 November 2026 · Last updated 26 November 2026

Pick one finding from the review, such as battery life or a camera comparison, open on the product or the result, state the claim accurately in a single breath, and end when the point is made. Add captions, keep the full-review context honest, and link the long video.

Key takeaways

  • YouTube's Shorts analytics help has "How many chose to view", the share who watched versus swiped away, so the first frame matters.
  • The same page says watch time and audience retention are key metrics and advises comparing strong and weak content rather than chasing a viral hit.
  • YouTube's retention report says spikes in a long review show moments viewers rewatched or shared, good candidates for Shorts.
  • YouTube Help on captions says captions reach a larger audience.
  • A Short must stay accurate when stripped of context; do not exaggerate a verdict.

Steps

  1. Open the long review's graph and find spikes and strong moments.
  2. Pick one finding that makes sense alone.
  3. Start on the product or result, not a logo.
  4. State the claim in one sentence and show the proof.
  5. Cut anything that needs earlier context, or add a text line for it.
  6. Add captions and check numbers.
  7. End when the point is made.
  8. Link or mention the full review.
  9. Check "How many chose to view" and compare across Shorts.

Worked example

Example: A long phone review has a spike at 06:40 where the presenter drops the phone in water for 30 seconds and it still works. The Short opens on the phone in water, says "Survived 30 seconds at 1 metre", shows the label rating on screen and ends with "full test on the channel". The scenario is invented, and the claim would need to match the maker's stated rating.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Exaggerating a result without its conditions.
  • Opening on a logo.
  • Cutting out the caveat that changes the meaning.
  • Using audio that needs the long video's context.
  • Measuring against a viral hit.
