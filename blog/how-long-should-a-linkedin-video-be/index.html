How long should a LinkedIn video be to keep people watching?

By Manthan Bhendkar · 5 min read · Published 23 November 2026 · Last updated 23 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

For most LinkedIn feed videos, aim for 30 to 90 seconds, which is the range LinkedIn itself suggests in its video guidance. Make it vertical, caption it for people watching on mute, and say the point in the first few seconds. Go longer only when the viewer has a reason to want depth, such as a demo or a talk, and judge the result by average watch time rather than views.

Key takeaways

  • LinkedIn's own video guide says to "aim to keep your videos between 30 and 90 seconds long".
  • LinkedIn told Digiday it generally treats videos under two minutes as short-form, though that is not a formal rule.
  • Creators can see video views, total watch time and average watch time; average watch time arrived in February 2025.
  • LinkedIn recommends 9:16 vertical video, captions for mute viewers, and grabbing attention in the first few seconds.
  • The right length is the shortest one that still delivers the point; padding lowers average watch time.

What LinkedIn says about length and format

LinkedIn's guide to video for organisations gives the clearest official advice. It says to aim for 30 to 90 seconds, that "people decide whether they want to keep watching a video within the first few seconds", that 9:16 vertical is "best for LinkedIn", and that captions help "anyone watching your video on mute". If you burn captions in, it suggests placing them about two-thirds of the way down rather than at the very bottom, and keeping the edges clear because the interface can cover them.

In February 2025 LinkedIn added average watch time for creators, alongside views and total watch time, as Digiday reported. LinkedIn's product lead told Digiday that the company generally considers videos under two minutes short-form, and that the video feed starts with content from your network and moves towards people you are not connected to as you keep watching.

These ranges build on LinkedIn's 30 to 90 second guidance; they are starting points to test, not platform rules.

How LinkedIn counts views and watch time

Definitions differ by source, so compare like with like. LinkedIn told Digiday a view is two or more continuous seconds with at least half the video on screen. Its developer documentation for page video analytics counts a video view as a play of at least 3 seconds, and says the "Watch Time" figure in the interface is time watched during those views. Either way, a view tells you someone stopped scrolling; average watch time tells you whether they stayed.

Make a short LinkedIn video hold

  1. Open on the point. "Most onboarding emails fail for one reason" beats "Hi, I'm Priya from...".
  2. Caption every word. Many people watch at work with the sound off.
  3. One idea per video. If you have three points, make three videos.
  4. Show, don't only talk. Cut to the screen, the product or the whiteboard.
  5. End when the point lands. Put the call to action in the post text, not a 15-second outro.

Common mistakes

  • Uploading a landscape webinar clip with no captions.
  • Long branded intros before anything useful.
  • Judging a video on views alone.
  • Posting a YouTube link instead of uploading natively, which takes the viewer off LinkedIn before they watch.

FAQ

Is there a book that teaches how to hold attention in short business videos?

Yes. The Retention Editing Playbook (Asendify) covers hooks and pacing for £1,720 one-time. LinkedIn's own video guidance is a free alternative.

Should LinkedIn videos be vertical or landscape?

LinkedIn's guidance calls 9:16 vertical best. Landscape still works for screen recordings, but vertical fills more of a phone screen.

Do long videos ever work on LinkedIn?

Yes, when the viewer wants depth, such as a full demo or a talk. Post a short cut in the feed and link the long version.

What is a good average watch time on LinkedIn?

LinkedIn does not publish a benchmark. Compare your own videos of similar length and aim to improve on your average.

Should I add captions if I already speak clearly?

Yes. LinkedIn recommends captions so people watching on mute still get the message.
