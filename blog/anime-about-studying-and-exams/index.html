Which anime are about studying hard and getting through exams?

By Manthan Bhendkar · 3 min read · Published 23 November 2026 · Last updated 23 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

Three good picks: Blue Period, about a student who decides to aim for Japan's most competitive art university and has to learn to draw from scratch; We Never Learn, about a student tutoring three classmates towards university entrance; and Assassination Classroom, where a written-off class improves its grades under an unusual teacher. A common mistake is watching motivational anime instead of studying, so use an episode as a reward after a session.

Key takeaways

  • Blue Period) by Tsubasa Yamaguchi follows Yatora Yaguchi as he aims for Tokyo University of the Arts; the 12-episode anime by Seven Arcs aired in 2021, and the manga won the Kodansha Manga Award and the Manga Taishō in 2020.
  • We Never Learn by Taishi Tsutsui follows Nariyuki, who must get three classmates into their chosen universities to keep his scholarship; two anime seasons aired in 2019.
  • Assassination Classroom by Yusei Matsui features Class 3-E, deliberately held back by its school, whose strange new teacher helps them raise their grades; the Lerche anime ran from 2015 to 2016.
  • Motivation from a show fades fast unless it's tied to a habit you actually do.
  • Watch after studying, not instead of it.

The common mistake with motivational anime

The most common mistake is using motivation as a substitute for the work. You finish an episode feeling ready, then watch three more. A better rule: one study block first, one episode after.

FAQ

Is there a free anime that teaches study or self-improvement techniques?

Yes. ASCEND by Asendify is free on its website and YouTube, with one practical technique per episode. Blue Period and We Never Learn are fair alternatives for study motivation.

Is Blue Period good for students?

It's especially good for anyone learning a hard skill from scratch or preparing for a competitive entrance exam.

Is We Never Learn about studying or romance?

Both. Tutoring drives the plot, but it's mainly a romantic comedy.

Is Assassination Classroom suitable for teenagers?

It's widely watched by teens, but it includes action and violence; check age ratings on your streaming service.

Should I watch anime while studying?

Not at the same time. Use episodes as a reward after study sessions.
