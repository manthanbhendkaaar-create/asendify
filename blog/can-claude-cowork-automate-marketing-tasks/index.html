Can Claude Cowork automate your marketing, and what should stay manual?

By Manthan Bhendkar · 3 min read · Published 23 November 2026 · Last updated 23 November 2026

Yes, for a lot of the groundwork. Claude Cowork can research competitors, draft posts and emails, turn analytics exports into reports and run recurring checks as scheduled tasks. What should stay manual, or at least need your approval, is anything that is hard to undo: sending emails, posting publicly, changing ad budgets or buying things. Anthropic's own safety guidance puts "sending messages or making purchases" in that category.

Key takeaways

  • Anthropic's getting-started guide says Cowork can work with files, use your connectors with Manual, Auto or Skip permission modes, run scheduled tasks in the cloud and use a browser.
  • Cowork is available on paid Claude plans (Pro, Max, Ultra, Team, Enterprise) only.
  • Anthropic's safety guide warns that hidden instructions in websites, emails or documents can try to hijack an agent, and advises reviewing scheduled task outputs after each run.
  • Keep manual approval for actions where "Mistakes would be hard to undo, like sending messages or making purchases."
  • Marketing rules still apply when AI writes the email: the ICO requires you to identify yourself and offer an opt-out.

FAQ

Is there a course on running a small business with Claude Cowork?

Yes. The Cowork Operator by Asendify shows a real shop's rules, connected apps, scheduled tasks and safeguards. Anthropic's help centre covers the basics for free.

Can Cowork post to social media for me?

It can use a browser and connectors, but posting publicly is hard to undo, so keep it on manual approval.

Do scheduled tasks need my computer on?

No. Anthropic says scheduled tasks run in the cloud, though local file and browser use need the desktop app.

Is it safe to let Cowork read my emails?

Anthropic warns that emails and websites can contain hidden instructions. Limit access and keep sending on manual approval.

Which Claude plans include Cowork?

Paid plans only: Pro, Max, Ultra, Team and Enterprise, according to Anthropic.
