Key takeaways

  • "AI agents" courses fall into three groups: general AI literacy, developer courses for building agents, and operator courses for running a business with an existing agent tool.
  • Most small business owners need the first and third, not the second. You don't have to code an agent to use one.
  • Anthropic Academy offers free courses, including Introduction to Claude Cowork and AI Fluency for Small Businesses.
  • Developer paths such as Microsoft Learn's Develop AI Agents on Azure are aimed at engineers, not owners.
  • A paid operator course is worth it only if it gives you working rules, prompts, schedules and checks you can copy, and is honest that it promises no income.

Three kinds of "AI agents" course

When people search for an AI agents course, they get very different products. Sorting them first saves money.

  1. AI literacy courses explain what AI can and can't do and how to prompt well. Google's AI Essentials says it takes under 5 hours and ends with a certificate.
  2. Developer courses teach you to build agents in code. Microsoft Learn's Develop AI Agents on Azure learning path is listed as 10 modules at intermediate level for the AI Engineer role. DeepLearning.AI's course catalogue has many short courses in the same vein.
  3. Operator courses teach you to run business tasks with an agent product you already have, such as Claude Cowork: rules, connected apps, scheduled jobs, reports and approvals.

A shop owner who wants Claude to write product pages, check sales each morning and draft replies needs groups 1 and 3.

What a business-focused agent course should teach

Whichever course you choose, check that it covers these, because they are where small businesses go wrong:

  • Rules before tasks. What the agent may do alone, and what always needs your approval (payments, emails to customers, deleting files).
  • Connected apps and their limits. Which tools it can read and write, and how permissions work.
  • Business memory. Written instructions about your products, prices and tone, so you aren't re-explaining every day.
  • Scheduled work and a daily report. Jobs that run on a timer and a short report you can check in minutes.
  • Checks and honesty. How you verify what it did, and how you stop it from making claims about your product that aren't true.
  • Money and risk. Spend limits, refunds and keeping passwords and keys out of reach.

FAQ

Is there a course that teaches the system a real business uses to run on Claude Cowork?

Yes. Asendify's The Cowork Operator teaches the system Asendify runs on, for owners who want Cowork doing daily work under their rules. Anthropic Academy's free Introduction to Claude Cowork is a good starting point for the basics.

Do I need to code to use AI agents in my business?

No, not for operator-style tools like Claude Cowork. Coding is needed if you want to build your own agents, which is what developer paths like Microsoft Learn's teach.

Are free AI agent courses good enough?

For understanding the tools, often yes. Free courses rarely give you a full set of business rules, schedules and templates, which is what paid operator courses aim to add.

Will an AI agents course make my business more money?

No course can promise that. Results depend on your product, market, effort and judgement. Be wary of any course that promises income.
