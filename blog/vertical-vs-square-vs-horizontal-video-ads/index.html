Should my video ads be vertical, square or horizontal?

By Manthan Bhendkar · 5 min read · Published 22 November 2026 · Last updated 22 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

Make a vertical 9:16 version your main cut if your ads run on YouTube Shorts, Instagram or Facebook Reels, Stories or TikTok, because those placements are full-screen vertical. Then add square (1:1) and horizontal (16:9) versions so the same campaign looks right in feeds and on standard YouTube. Google recommends uploading all three, and warns that campaigns may not serve if they only have vertical videos. Frame every version for its shape rather than letting the platform crop one master.

Key takeaways

  • Google says vertical videos are "best suited for the Shorts format and deliver better performance compared to landscape assets."
  • Google's internal 2024 data: adding vertical video to landscape-only ad groups raised view-through rate on Shorts by over 40% in video view campaigns.
  • Google also says square video is "still recommended" for App, Demand Gen and Performance Max campaigns, and suggests uploading vertical, square and horizontal versions.
  • Meta's Reels ads documentation calls for full-screen 9:16 and keeping the bottom 35% free of text and logos.
  • TikTok recommends "orienting vertically at 9:16" and keeping key content inside the safe zone.

Google's numbers on vertical

Google's page cites its own global 2024 data: in Demand Gen, vertical video "leads to more than 35% increase in conversions on Shorts"; in video view campaigns, adding vertical to landscape-only ad groups "increases VTR on Shorts by over 40%"; in video reach campaigns, it raised viewability on Shorts by over 45%. These are Google's internal figures about its own placements, so they show direction rather than what your ad will do. The same page warns: "Campaigns may not serve if they only have vertical videos."

A production plan for three shapes

Making three versions sounds like triple the work, but most of it can be planned into one shoot and one edit session.

Before the shoot. Decide which shape is the priority. For most advertisers running on Reels, Shorts and TikTok, that's 9:16. Draw a simple frame guide: a vertical rectangle inside a horizontal one. Everything essential, such as faces, the product and any action, stays inside the vertical area.

During the shoot. Shoot in horizontal 4K if your camera allows and frame loosely, so you can crop vertical and square versions without losing quality. If you're filming on a phone and the vertical version matters most, shoot vertical and accept that the horizontal version may need a different shot or a background fill. Capture a few extra close-ups; they work in every shape.

In the edit. Build the vertical version first. Then duplicate the timeline for square and horizontal, reposition each shot, and move text to suit the shape.

ElementVertical 9:16Square 1:1Horizontal 16:9
Face positionCentre, upper halfCentreCentre or rule of thirds
Headline textUpper middleUpper or lower thirdLower third
CaptionsAbove the bottom 35% on ReelsLower thirdLower third
LogoInside the scene, away from bottomCorner or in sceneCorner or in scene

Name files clearly. For example "spring-sale_hookA_9x16.mp4". It saves mistakes when uploading many versions.

Check on a phone. Before uploading, watch every version on an actual phone. Text that looks fine on a monitor is often too small on a phone, and interface elements cover more than you expect.

Compare by placement. After launch, look at results per placement rather than overall. You may find the vertical version wins on Shorts and Reels while horizontal still drives most in-stream results, which tells you where to put future effort. For keeping each version tight, see how long a video ad should be and pattern interrupts in video ads.

Start with what you have. If budget is tight, one vertical version plus one horizontal version covers most placements well.
