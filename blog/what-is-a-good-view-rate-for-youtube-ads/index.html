What is a good view rate for YouTube ads?

By Manthan Bhendkar · 5 min read · Published 22 November 2026 · Last updated 22 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

Google says a TrueView view rate of about 10 to 15% is considered average for in-stream ads across all industries, though it "can vary significantly". View rate is views divided by impressions, and on a skippable in-stream ad a view means someone watched 30 seconds (or the whole ad if shorter) or clicked. So a good view rate is one that beats your own average for the same format, audience and ad length, while still bringing the results you're paying for.

Key takeaways

  • Google's view metrics guide: "About 10–15% is considered average for the In-stream format across all industries, but it can vary significantly."
  • TrueView view rate = TrueView views ÷ impressions.
  • A skippable in-stream view is 30 seconds or the end of the ad, whichever comes first, or a click. In Shorts it's 10 seconds or the end.
  • Bumper and non-skippable ads don't count TrueView views, so they have no view rate to compare.
  • Shorter ads reach "a view" more easily. Don't compare a 20-second ad's view rate with a 2-minute ad's.

How Google counts a view

Google's guide to YouTube ads and view metrics sets out the rules (Google renamed "Views" to "TrueView views" in October 2025, with billing unchanged):

FormatWhat counts as a TrueView view
Skippable in-stream30 seconds watched, or the whole ad if shorter, or a click
In-feed videoA click to watch; in video view campaigns, also 10 seconds of autoplay
Shorts10 seconds watched or the end of the ad, or a CTA click, like, comment or share
Bumper, non-skippableNot counted as TrueView views

Because the rule differs by format, Google warns that comparisons across formats need care.

What moves view rate

Ad length. A 20-second skippable ad counts a view when someone watches to the end. A 90-second one needs 30 seconds. Same audience, same quality, very different view rates.

The first five seconds. On skippable in-stream ads, viewers can skip after five seconds, according to Google's video ad formats guide. Every viewer who skips at five seconds is an impression with no view. The opening is the biggest lever you have; see how to stop viewers skipping your YouTube ad after 5 seconds.

Targeting. An ad shown to people who've never heard of the problem it solves will get a lower view rate than the same ad shown to people searching for a solution.

Bidding. On CPV bidding you pay for views, so the system looks for people likely to watch. On Target CPM you pay per impression, and view rate may differ.

Look past view rate

A high view rate isn't the goal on its own. Google's own "video played to" metrics show the share of viewers reaching 25%, 50%, 75% and 100% of the ad, which shows where people leave. Pair them with clicks and conversions:

PatternWhat it suggests
Low view rate, low played-to-25%The opening isn't holding people
Good played-to-25%, steep fall by 50%The middle drags or the promise isn't kept
High view rate, few clicksInteresting ad, weak offer or call to action
Low view rate, strong conversionsThe ad filters for the right people; may be fine

Worked example

Illustrative figures for two skippable ads in the same campaign:

Ad A (75 s)Ad B (25 s cut)
Impressions40,00040,000
TrueView views3,6006,400
View rate9%16%
Clicks120150

Ad B looks far better on view rate, partly because a "view" for it means watching to the end at 25 seconds. The fairer comparison is clicks and conversions per impression, where B is still ahead, so the shorter cut wins this test.

How to raise view rate honestly

  1. Start on the most interesting moment, not a logo. Google's ABCDs put it as "jump in" and "get to the heart of the story faster".
  2. Show your brand inside the opening, not before it.
  3. Open a question in the first five seconds that the ad answers later.
  4. Make a shorter version and test it.
  5. Narrow targeting to people with the problem the ad solves.

Avoid fake skip buttons, misleading openings or bait that the ad doesn't pay off: they break platform policies and the UK CAP Code says ads "must not materially mislead". For opening ideas, see how to make the first 3 seconds of a video ad stop people scrolling.

View rate by format: what to compare

Because each format counts views differently, keep comparisons within the same format and similar lengths.

CompareFair?Why
Two 30-second skippable ads, same audienceYesSame view rule and length
A 20-second and a 2-minute skippable adWith careThe shorter ad reaches a "view" at its end
Skippable in-stream vs ShortsNoViews count at 30 s vs 10 s
Any format vs bumper or non-skippableNoThose don't count TrueView views

Track the trend. A falling view rate on the same ad over several weeks, with stable targeting, often signals creative fatigue; see how to tell if a video ad has creative fatigue.

Segment before you judge. View rate can differ a lot by device, age group and placement. An ad that looks average overall may be strong on one segment and weak on another. Shift budget to the segments where viewers both watch and act.

Keep the goal in view. If your goal is sales, a lower view rate with more conversions beats a high view rate with none.

Combine with quartiles. View rate tells you how many reached 30 seconds or the end; "video played to" 25%, 50% and 75% show where the rest left. Read them together.

FAQ

Is there a book that helps raise YouTube ad view rate?

The Retention Editing Playbook covers hooks and pacing at £1,000 one-time. Google's ABCDs and Google Ads Help are free alternatives.

What is the average view rate for YouTube ads?

Google says about 10 to 15% is average for in-stream ads across industries, with wide variation.

Why is my YouTube ad view rate so low?

Usually a slow opening, a long ad, or targeting people who don't have the problem it solves.

Does a high view rate mean the ad is working?

Not on its own. Check played-to-50% and conversions too.
