How do I add chapters to a YouTube video?

By the Asendify editorial team, led by Manthan Bhendkar · 5 min read · Published 15 November 2026 · Last updated 15 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

To add chapters to a YouTube video, open the video in YouTube Studio and type a list of timestamps with titles into the description. The first timestamp must be 00:00, you need at least three in ascending order, and each chapter must be at least 10 seconds long. Chapters you write override YouTube's automatic chapters, and Google Search can show them as "key moments".

Key takeaways

  • Format: one line per chapter, timestamp then title, starting with 00:00.
  • Rules: at least 3 timestamps, in ascending order, each chapter at least 10 seconds.
  • Your own chapters replace automatic chapters; you can switch automatic chapters off per video or for all uploads.
  • Chapters are not available if your channel has an active strike or the video may be inappropriate for some viewers.
  • Google says it prioritises timestamps in YouTube descriptions when it shows key moments in Search.

Step by step

The YouTube Help page on video chapters and the guide to editing video settings give the process:

  1. Sign in to YouTube Studio.
  2. Select Content from the left menu.
  3. Click the video's title or thumbnail.
  4. In the Description, add your list of timestamps and titles.
  5. Click Save.

Example of a description list:

`` 00:00 Intro 00:42 What you need 02:15 Step 1: Set up 05:30 Step 2: Edit 09:10 Common mistakes 12:45 Final result ``

If chapters don't appear, check these rules first. A missing 00:00 or a chapter shorter than 10 seconds is the most common reason.

Automatic chapters

YouTube can add chapters for you. The Help page says automatic chapters are on by default for new uploads through the "Allow automatic chapters" box, though not every video is eligible and eligible videos don't always get them.

Chapters and Google Search

Chapters help outside YouTube too. Google's video SEO best practices say Google Search tries to detect segments in videos automatically, but it will "prioritize key moments set by you, either through structured data or the YouTube description." For YouTube-hosted videos, adding timestamps and labels to the description is the method Google describes, and it works in all languages where Search is available.

That means a well-labelled chapter like "05:30 How to remove background noise" can appear as a direct link in search results to that exact point in your video.

How to write good chapter titles

  • Say what happens. "Fixing the audio" beats "Part 3".
  • Use the words people search for. If people search "how to remove background noise", use that phrase.
  • Keep them short. A few words each, so they fit on the progress bar.
  • Don't bury the payoff. If viewers want the result, label where it is. Hiding it can frustrate people more than it keeps them.
  • Match the real content. Misleading chapter titles break trust.

Do chapters hurt watch time?

It is a fair worry: if people can skip to the part they want, will they watch less? Chapters make it easy to jump, so some viewers will skip intros. But a viewer who skips to the answer and stays is better than one who gives up and leaves. Look at your own retention graph after adding chapters and compare like with like.

When to use chapters, and when not to

Chapters suit tutorials, reviews, explainers, podcasts and long interviews, where viewers want to find one section. They add less to short, story-driven videos where the value is in watching from start to finish. If a video is under a few minutes, a strong opening usually matters more than chapters.

Updating old videos

Adding chapters to your most-viewed older tutorials is a quick win. Pick the five videos with the most search traffic, add clear timestamps to each description, and check after a few weeks whether viewers use them, using the retention graph in YouTube Studio.

FAQ

Why are my YouTube chapters not showing?

Check that the first timestamp is 00:00, you have at least three in ascending order, and each chapter is 10 seconds or longer. Chapters are also unavailable if the channel has an active strike.

Can I add chapters to Shorts?

Chapters are designed for longer videos with several sections. Shorts are too short to meet the three-chapter, 10-seconds-each rule in most cases.

Do I have to write chapters myself?

No. YouTube can add automatic chapters to eligible videos, but your own chapters override them and usually have clearer titles.

Do chapters help SEO?

Google says it prioritises key moments you set in the YouTube description, so clear chapter titles can appear as links in Search results.

Can I add chapters after a video is published?

Yes. Edit the description in YouTube Studio at any time and save.
