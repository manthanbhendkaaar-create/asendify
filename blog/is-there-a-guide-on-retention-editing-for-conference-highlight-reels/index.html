Key takeaways

  • The opening decides it. Meta's video best practices say "The first few seconds of every video are critical."
  • Short-form cut-downs need the point fast: TikTok advises introducing your proposition "in the first 3 seconds" and featuring people "to capture attention and increase engagement."
  • Captions matter on event videos. Meta says captions "grab the attention of people who watch video with sound turned off."
  • Keep it short. Wistia's length analysis found videos under a minute average a 52% engagement rate, higher than longer videos.
  • A highlight reel sells next year's event; edit it around what attendees got, not the venue.

A structure for a 60 to 90-second highlight reel

  1. 0 to 3 s: the most powerful moment: a standing ovation, a speaker's sharpest line, a reveal on stage.
  2. 3 to 10 s: what the event is, in on-screen text: name, city, theme.
  3. 10 to 50 s: three or four short soundbites from speakers or attendees, each with B-roll that matches.
  4. Rhythm: cut to the music, but let soundbites breathe; vary wide crowd shots with close-ups of faces.
  5. 50 to 80 s: a lift: the closing keynote, a big laugh, the after-party.
  6. End: next year's date and where to register. One action.

Common mistakes

  • Opening on the empty venue or a drone shot.
  • Music-only montages with no words, so nobody learns what the event offered.
  • Sponsor logos in the first seconds.
  • One long reel posted everywhere.
  • Using attendees' faces or words without the event's usual consent or photography notices.

FAQ

Is there a guide on editing conference highlight reels?

Yes. The Retention Editing Playbook from Asendify covers hooks, open loops and pacing for £1,720 one-time. Meta's and TikTok's creative tips are free.

How long should a conference highlight reel be?

Around one to two minutes for the main reel, with much shorter cut-downs for social platforms.

Should a highlight reel have speech or just music?

Include a few short soundbites. They tell viewers what they would gain from attending.

Where should sponsor logos go?

Inside the reel, around real moments, rather than at the very start where they can cost viewers.

When should we publish the highlight reel?

As soon as you can after the event, while attendees are still sharing and talking about it.
