How should you use YouTube posts (the Community tab)?

By the Asendify editorial team, led by Manthan Bhendkar · 5 min read · Published 21 November 2026 · Last updated 21 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

YouTube posts, formerly the Community tab, let you share text, images, GIFs, polls, quizzes and videos between uploads. They appear on your channel's Posts tab and can show up in viewers' homepages and Subscriptions feeds, and image posts can appear in the Shorts feed. Use them to ask your audience what they want next, tease upcoming videos and keep in touch between uploads. They aren't available on channels set as made for kids.

Key takeaways

  • YouTube says posts can include "polls, quizzes, GIFs, text, music, images, and video."
  • Posts appear on your channel, in viewers' homepages and Subscriptions feeds, and image posts can appear in the Shorts feed; subscribers may be notified.
  • Image posts allow up to 10 images, 16 MB each, with a suggested 1:1 aspect ratio.
  • Text polls allow up to 4 answers of up to 65 characters; quizzes allow up to 4 answers and one correct one.
  • Made for kids channels can't create new posts, and there's a limit on posts per 24 hours.

Where posts appear

YouTube's Learn about posts page lists:

PlaceNotes
Your channelThe Posts shelf on the Home tab, or the Posts tab
Viewers' homepagesPosts can be shown there
Subscriptions feedPosts can appear for subscribers
Shorts feedImage posts only
NotificationsSubscribers may get one when you post

Posts aren't available to supervised accounts or channels set as made for kids, and YouTube notes access can vary by channel role and isn't currently available in Germany.

The formats and their limits

YouTube's Create a post page gives the details:

FormatLimits
TextA message on its own
ImageUp to 10 images, 16 MB max, JPG, PNG, GIF or WEBP; 1:1 suggested
Text pollUp to 4 answers, 65 characters each
Image pollUp to 4 images, captions of 36 characters
QuizUp to 4 answers (80 characters, or 30 with an image), one correct answer, optional 500-character explanation
VideoShare one of your videos with a message

To post: Create > Create post, write your message or choose a format, then Post. To schedule, click the arrow next to Post, choose Schedule Post, and set a date, time and time zone. Editing and deleting are done on a computer from your History, under Posts. Quizzes can't be edited once saved, so check them before posting.

Who can post

Access has widened over time. Search Engine Journal reported in 2021 that the subscriber threshold for posting was dropping from 1,000 to 500, with smaller channels to follow later. Today YouTube's help pages describe eligibility in terms of channel settings and roles rather than a subscriber count, so check the Create menu on your own channel.

Search Engine Journal lists similar uses: polls for ideas, sharing older content, rich media between uploads, channel updates and promoting upcoming videos.

How often to post

There is no official ideal number, and YouTube applies a limit on posts per 24 hours anyway. A sensible rhythm for a small channel is one or two posts between uploads, each with a clear job: gather an opinion, share something useful, or point to a video. Posting for the sake of it trains people to scroll past your name. Look at which posts get replies and votes, and do more of those.

Keep posts consistent with your channel

Posts carry your channel name and profile picture into people's feeds, so the same packaging rules apply as for videos: a clear message, a readable image and a reason to care. A consistent look across your banner, thumbnails and handle makes posts instantly recognisable.

Worked example: a small baking channel

Example: Nadia has 800 subscribers and uploads one video every two weeks. She plans posts for the gap.

DayPostPurpose
Day 2 after uploadImage post: three photos of the finished bakeReminds people of the latest video
Day 5Text poll: "Croissants or bagels next?" (2 answers)Picks the next topic
Day 9Quiz: "Why did this loaf collapse?" with an explanationFun, teaches something
Day 12Teaser: still from the next video, release timeBuilds interest

The poll tells her bagels win by a wide margin, so that's what she films. Whether posts lift her views is something she checks in YouTube Studio analytics; the point is that every post either helps a viewer or helps her plan. The example is illustrative.

Is the Community tab the same as the Posts tab?

Yes. YouTube's help pages now call them posts and the Posts tab.

How many images can a YouTube post have?

Up to 10, each up to 16 MB.

Can I schedule YouTube posts?

Yes. Use the arrow next to Post, choose Schedule Post, and set a date, time and time zone.

Do subscribers get notified about posts?
