Does a client's video ad need Clearcast approval before it runs?

By Manthan Bhendkar · 4 min read · Published 23 November 2026 · Last updated 23 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

Only if it's going on UK TV or on-demand channels that Clearcast clears for. Clearcast checks commercials for those broadcasters against the UK broadcast code before they air, in three stages: script, optional rough cut, then the final ad. Ads that only run on social media or YouTube don't go through Clearcast, but they still have to follow the CAP Code and can be investigated by the ASA. If a social ad might be re-cut for TV later, plan the retention edit around Clearcast's script stage from the start.

Key takeaways

  • The ASA describes Clearcast as running clearance for "TV and Video-on-Demand commercials", checking them against the BCAP Code, and defending complaints about ads it cleared.
  • Clearcast's guide for new advertisers says "Clearance is a three-stage process", and you must submit a script first even if the ad is already made.
  • Allow at least two weeks for script approval; final clearance usually takes two to three working days if the ad matches the approved script.
  • Per Clearcast's FAQ, standard clearance is paid by Clearcast's stakeholders when the ad airs on a channel it clears for and is five minutes or shorter; Fast Track and late clearance cost extra.
  • Linear TV ads follow the BCAP Code; on-demand ads follow the CAP Code, which also covers social ads.

The three stages

StageWhat you sendTypical time
1. ScriptA written breakdown on Clearcast's template, plus evidence for claimsFeedback within days; approval averages about two weeks
2. Rough cut (optional, recommended)An early edit to check it matches the scriptVaries
3. Final adThe finished, clocked adUsually two working days

The script stage is where most changes happen. Clearcast asks for supporting evidence for claims at this point, so a claim that can't be proven is cut before any edit is locked.

What this means for a retention edit

Social ads are usually edited fast and iterated: new hooks, new cuts, new versions every week. TV clearance doesn't work like that. Every new version needs to match an approved script.

Social habitTV-ready version
Test five hooks this weekPick the winning hook from social data before the script goes in
Change captions on the flyLock on-screen text in the script
Add a new claim if it performsOnly claims with evidence approved at script stage
Re-cut weeklyPlan alternate lengths (10, 20, 30 seconds) inside one script

The best workflow is to let social testing find the strongest hook and pacing, then write the TV script from the winner.

Example: a client moving from TikTok to TV

Example: a UK snack brand has run a 15-second TikTok ad for two months and now has a TV budget.

  1. The agency picks the TikTok version with the best hold rate (product on screen in the first second, a taste-test reaction at 5 seconds).
  2. The writer turns it into a 20-second TV script with a 10-second cut-down and lists every claim, such as "30% less sugar than our original".
  3. The client supplies lab evidence for the sugar claim; the "UK's favourite" line is dropped because there's no proof.
  4. Script approved after about two weeks. The editor rebuilds at broadcast spec, keeping the same hook and pacing.
  5. Final ad cleared in two working days and delivered to the booked channels.

The social testing did the retention work; the script stage made it TV-safe. Details are illustrative.

FAQ

Is there a book that helps pick the best hook before a TV script?

Yes. The Retention Editing Playbook covers hooks and pacing for £1,720 one-time. Clearcast's help centre covers clearance for free.

Do YouTube ads need Clearcast approval?

No. YouTube ads follow the CAP Code and Google's ad policies, not Clearcast clearance.

How long does Clearcast take?

Allow at least two weeks for the script and about two working days for the final ad.

Does Clearcast charge?

Standard clearance is paid by its stakeholders when the ad airs on a cleared channel and is five minutes or shorter; premium services cost extra.

Can I submit a finished ad without a script?

No. Clearcast says you need to submit a script first, even if the ad is already made.
