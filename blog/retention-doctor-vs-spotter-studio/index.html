Retention Doctor vs Spotter Studio: which should a YouTube creator use?

By the Asendify editorial team, led by Manthan Bhendkar · 5 min read · Published 21 November 2026 · Last updated 21 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

They solve different problems. Spotter Studio is an AI creative suite for the start of a video: brainstorming ideas, studying outlier videos from similar creators, generating thumbnail concepts and managing projects. Asendify's Retention Doctor is for after upload: it reads each video's retention graph and reports the exact seconds viewers left, with a fix for each drop. Pick Spotter if your problem is what to make; pick Retention Doctor if people click but do not stay.

Key takeaways

  • Spotter Studio costs $49 a month or $299 a year (a limited-time offer), with a free trial for first-time members, according to its pricing page.
  • At launch, TechCrunch reported Spotter's tools as Brainstorm, Diversify, Thumbnails, Outliers and Projects, tailored using a creator's public videos.
  • Spotter's pricing page says it does not currently work for creators who only publish Shorts.
  • Retention Doctor is retention-only: a drop-off report about 48 hours after each upload, a Retention Score out of 100, a Monday three-fix plan and a pre-upload check of your opening script and title.
  • YouTube Studio's free retention report is the baseline both should be judged against.

What each does well

Spotter Studio. TechCrunch describes Brainstorm generating ideas from your request and your earlier uploads, Diversify branching an idea into related ones, Thumbnails creating concept art with your likeness, Outliers surfacing top-performing videos from similar creators, and Projects tracking work from development to publishing. Its founder told TechCrunch: "It's looking at every video you've ever created." The pricing page includes three logins, which suits small teams. If your channel's problem is running out of strong ideas, or packaging them, this is the more relevant tool.

Retention Doctor. It reads each upload's retention graph and lists the moments viewers left, with the Retention playbook technique that fixes each drop, then rolls the biggest issues into a Monday three-fix plan. It also gives a Retention Score from 0 to 100 and an unlimited pre-upload check of your opening script and title. It connects read-only and cannot change anything on your channel. If your videos get clicks but viewers leave early or in the middle, this is the more relevant tool.

How to tell which problem you have

YouTube's help on key moments for audience retention gives you the free test. Its Intro figure shows the share of viewers still watching after 30 seconds, and dips show where viewers skipped or stopped. Combine that with impressions click-through rate from the Reach tab:

Can you use both?

Yes, and they do not overlap much: one helps choose and package what to make, the other shows whether the finished video holds viewers. The cost adds up, though: $49 a month plus £145 a month. Most creators should fix their bigger bottleneck first. For other comparisons, see Retention Doctor vs vidIQ and Retention Doctor vs TubeBuddy.

FAQ

Is there a tool that shows the exact second viewers left each video, with a fix?

Yes. Asendify's Retention Doctor emails each new video's drop-off seconds with a fix for each, using read-only access. YouTube Studio's retention report is the free alternative.

Does Spotter Studio show audience retention?

Its pricing page and TechCrunch's launch report describe ideation, outliers, thumbnails and projects; neither describes a retention drop-off report.

Does Retention Doctor give video ideas?

No. It focuses on retention: where viewers leave, why, and what to change in your next script or edit.

Is there a free trial?

Spotter Studio offers a free trial to first-time members. Retention Doctor has no free trial but offers one free check of your latest video.

Does Spotter Studio work for Shorts channels?

Its pricing page says it doesn't currently work for creators who only publish Shorts.
