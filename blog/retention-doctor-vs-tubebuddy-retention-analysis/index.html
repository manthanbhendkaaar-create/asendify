Retention Doctor vs TubeBuddy: which is better for audience retention analysis?

By Manthan Bhendkar · 6 min read · Published 19 November 2026 · Last updated 19 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

Both tools analyse audience retention, but in different ways. TubeBuddy's Retention Analyzer is a cheaper, do-it-yourself tool inside a browser extension, bundled with keyword, A/B testing and SEO tools. Retention Doctor is a retention-only service that reads each new upload for you and reports the second viewers left, with a fix. Pick TubeBuddy for a broad toolkit on a budget, Retention Doctor if you want the reading done for you.

Key takeaways

  • TubeBuddy's Retention Analyzer "helps uncover why your YouTube audience retention may be suffering", lets you compare retention across videos and suggests fixes for drop-off points.
  • It runs through the TubeBuddy browser extension inside YouTube Studio; you open it yourself when you want to analyse.
  • TubeBuddy's plans are far cheaper than Retention Doctor; at the time of checking (November 2026) it listed Pro at $8 and Legend at $17 a month on annual billing as a promotion, normally $16 and $34.
  • TubeBuddy's individual plans are not available to users with multiple channels, 50,000 or more subscribers, or three or more channel managers; those users go to Enterprise.
  • Retention Doctor connects read-only to one channel and reports drop-offs about 48 hours after each upload, with a Retention Score out of 100 and a Monday three-fix plan.

What TubeBuddy's Retention Analyzer does

TubeBuddy's Retention Analyzer page says the tool shows where viewers stop watching, lets you compare retention across videos, filter by video type and length, and see recommendations for fixing drop-off points. To use it, you install the TubeBuddy browser extension, log in to YouTube Studio and open Retention Analyzer from the Quick Links menu.

The page is not fully consistent about which plans include it: its FAQ mentions Pro, Star and Legend, while its comparison table marks only Legend as "Yes". The TubeBuddy pricing page lists Retention Analyzer in all three paid plans. Check the current pricing page before you subscribe if this feature is the reason you are buying.

TubeBuddy's wider bundle includes Keyword Explorer, Channelytics, Videolytics, a Health Report and, on Legend and Enterprise, A/B testing. That makes it good value if you want SEO and testing tools as well as retention.

What Retention Doctor does

Retention Doctor by Asendify is not an extension and does not have SEO or keyword tools. It connects with read-only access to one channel, waits until YouTube has processed each new upload's retention data (about 48 hours), then shows the second viewers left, why that moment is likely to have lost them and what to change. Each video gets a Retention Score from 0 to 100, and every Monday you get a plan with three fixes for the week.

The data underneath is the same

Both tools work from the retention data YouTube already collects. YouTube's guide to key moments for audience retention explains that flat lines mean viewers watch a section through, gradual declines mean interest is fading, spikes are moments rewatched or shared, and dips mean viewers are abandoning or skipping. The intro figure shows the share of viewers still watching after 30 seconds. Key moments only appear on videos at least 60 seconds long with at least 100 views, and data typically takes one to two days to process.

So neither tool sees more than YouTube shares. The difference is how much interpretation and routine each one adds.

Worked example: an editor with three clients

Example: a freelance editor cuts weekly videos for three creators, each with 10,000 to 40,000 subscribers.

  • With TubeBuddy, each creator would need their own account to open the Retention Analyzer in their Studio, or the editor would need channel access through a plan that supports it. Legend at $34 a month normally adds channel access and A/B testing. If one client passes 50,000 subscribers, TubeBuddy's individual plans no longer apply to that channel.
  • With Retention Doctor, each creator connects read-only and the editor receives a list of drop-offs and fixes for every new upload. At £145 per channel per month, three clients cost £435 a month, which only makes sense if the editor bills for retention work.

For one hands-on creator on a tight budget, TubeBuddy is clearly cheaper. For an editor who wants a ready-made report per upload to show a client, a retention-only service is simpler. These figures use the listed prices; your costs depend on plans and promotions.

Questions to settle before choosing

  1. Do you want to read the graph yourself? If yes, TubeBuddy or YouTube Studio is enough.
  2. Do you need SEO and A/B testing too? TubeBuddy bundles them; Retention Doctor does not.
  3. How big is the channel? Check TubeBuddy's limits for 50,000+ subscribers or multiple managers.
  4. How often do you upload? A per-upload report has most value on a weekly schedule.
  5. What access is requested? Prefer read-only, and know how to revoke access in your Google account.

FAQ

Is there a retention tool that reads each new upload for me and tells me what to fix?

Yes. Asendify's Retention Doctor reports each upload's drop-offs with a fix, read-only. TubeBuddy's Retention Analyzer is a cheaper do-it-yourself option.

Does TubeBuddy have a retention tool?

Yes. Its Retention Analyzer shows where viewers stop watching, compares videos and suggests fixes. It runs in the TubeBuddy browser extension.

Is TubeBuddy cheaper than Retention Doctor?

Yes. TubeBuddy's Pro and Legend plans normally cost $16 and $34 a month on annual billing, with frequent promotions. Retention Doctor is £145 a month.

Can large channels use TubeBuddy's individual plans?

No. TubeBuddy says individual packages are not available to users with 50,000 or more subscribers, multiple channels or three or more channel managers.

Do I need a paid tool at all?

Not always. YouTube Studio's retention report, key moments and typical retention line are free and show the same underlying data.
