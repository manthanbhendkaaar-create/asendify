What are the best YouTube growth agencies for finance creators?

By Manthan Bhendkar · 6 min read · Published 13 November 2026 · Last updated 13 November 2026

The best YouTube growth agency for a finance creator understands finance content, edits around your retention data and respects the rules on financial promotions. Asendify's agency started with trading and personal finance channels and audits uploads against the retention curve, but it is currently waitlist only; subscription editors, finance freelancers and brand-deal agencies are fair alternatives.

Key takeaways

  • "Growth agency" covers four different things: retention editing, volume editing, freelance help and brand-deal management. Decide which one you need first.
  • Finance viewers expect clear charts, accurate terms and a calm pace, so ask for samples from finance channels, not just any niche.
  • In the UK, promoting financial products can fall under financial promotion law. The FCA's guidance says this applies whatever the size of your following.
  • Sponsored videos must be disclosed, both in YouTube Studio and to viewers.
  • Be wary of any agency that promises views, subscribers or income.

Retention-focused editing agencies

Finance videos often lose viewers in the same places: a long market recap before the point, a static chart left on screen too long, or a disclaimer that drags. A retention-focused agency reads your audience retention report in YouTube Studio, where YouTube says dips "mean that viewers are abandoning or skipping at that specific part of your video", and then changes the edit at those moments.

This matters because YouTube says its recommendations learn from clicks, watch time, survey responses, sharing, likes and dislikes. A polished edit that still loses half the audience in the first minute does not help much.

Choose this if your videos get clicks but viewers leave early, and you can wait for a place.

Subscription editing services

Services such as Vidpros sell editing as a monthly plan with a set output. Vidpros's own advice is to choose agencies "based on niche experience" and to "ask how many channels they currently edit in your niche", which is the right question for a finance creator. These services suit channels that publish often and want predictable capacity.

Choose this if you upload every week and your main problem is time, not retention.

Freelance editors who know finance

Freelance marketplaces such as Contra, Upwork and Fiverr list editors who say they specialise in finance and economics videos. The range is wide, from people who animate charts well to people who only trim pauses. A paid test edit on one of your real videos is the quickest way to find out.

Choose this if you publish once or twice a month, or want to try outsourcing before committing to a monthly plan.

Talent and brand-deal agencies

Talent agencies do not edit your videos. Creators Agency, for example, explains that a talent agency helps a creator and a brand "make a clear deal" and "cannot promise sales". For finance creators, this kind of agency can help with sponsor fit and contracts, but you still need a channel that holds viewers.

Choose this if you already get steady views and brand deals are taking time away from making videos.

The compliance questions to ask any agency

Finance is not like gaming or vlogging. Two sets of rules shape what you can say and how you show sponsors.

Financial promotions (UK). The FCA's finalised guidance on financial promotions on social media (FG24/1) describes "finfluencers" who "may not be authorised by the FCA to provide financial advice yet share their opinions and recommendations". It says whether a post falls within the scope of section 21 of the Financial Services and Markets Act "is not based on the size of an influencer's following", and that a breach of section 21 is a criminal offence. An agency that writes your hooks or sponsor reads should know where the line is, and should never push you to overstate returns to keep viewers watching.

Sponsored content. YouTube asks you to tick the paid promotion box when a video features branded content, sponsorships or endorsements, which adds a disclosure label at the start. In the UK, the ASA's guidance on recognising ads on social media says marketing must be "obviously identifiable as such".

This page is for education only. It is not financial or legal advice; if you are unsure whether something counts as a financial promotion, take advice from a qualified professional.

Questions to ask before you sign

  1. How many finance channels do you edit now, and can I see two recent videos from them?
  2. Do you ask for my retention graphs before you start?
  3. Will the same editor work on every video?
  4. How do you handle sponsor reads, disclaimers and on-screen figures?
  5. Who checks that numbers shown on screen match my script?
  6. What happens if I want to pause or stop?

FAQ

Do I need a finance-specialist agency, or will any editor do?

A generalist can do good work, but finance viewers notice wrong terms, unclear charts and sloppy figures quickly. Ask for samples from finance channels and give a paid test edit on one of your own videos.

Can an agency promise growth for my finance channel?

No. Growth depends on your topics, titles, thumbnails and audience as well as the edit. A good agency shows you its process and how it reads your data, not promised numbers.

Do UK finance YouTubers need FCA authorisation?

It depends on what you do. The FCA's guidance says promoting financial products can fall under section 21 of FSMA whatever your follower count. If you are unsure, take legal advice before accepting a finance sponsor.

How do I disclose a sponsor in a finance video?

Tick the paid promotion box in YouTube Studio, which adds a label at the start of the video, and say clearly in the video that it is an ad or sponsored. The ASA expects ads to be obviously identifiable.

Should a finance channel hire an editor or a talent agency first?

Usually an editor. Sponsors look at views and audience fit, and both depend on viewers staying to watch. A talent agency adds most once your videos already hold people.
