What are the best export settings for YouTube?

By Manthan Bhendkar · 5 min read · Published 20 November 2026 · Last updated 20 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

YouTube's recommended export for a standard video is an MP4 file using H.264 (High Profile, progressive scan, 4:2:0 chroma) with AAC-LC audio at 48 kHz, exported at the same frame rate you recorded. For SDR video, YouTube recommends about 8 Mbps for 1080p at 24 to 30fps, 12 Mbps at 48 to 60fps, and 35 to 45 Mbps for 4K at standard frame rates. Higher-quality uploads give YouTube more to work with when it re-encodes.

Key takeaways

  • Container: MP4, with no edit lists and the moov atom at the front ("fast start").
  • Video: H.264, High Profile, progressive, CABAC, 2 consecutive B-frames, closed GOP of half the frame rate, variable bitrate, 4:2:0.
  • Audio: AAC-LC, Opus or Eclipsa Audio at 48 kHz, in stereo or stereo plus 5.1.
  • Frame rate: "Content should be encoded and uploaded using the same frame rate that was used during recording."
  • Deinterlace interlaced footage before upload, for example 1080i60 to 1080p30.

Why match the frame rate?

Converting frame rates on export, for example from 25fps footage to a 30fps file, adds duplicated or blended frames that can look like stutter. YouTube's advice is simply to upload at the rate you recorded. Our page on what frame rate to use for YouTube covers choosing a recording frame rate in the first place.

A 30-second export check

Before you upload, confirm five things:

  1. The file is an MP4 using H.264.
  2. The frame rate matches your footage.
  3. The bitrate is at least YouTube's figure for your resolution and frame rate.
  4. Audio is AAC at 48 kHz.
  5. You watched the first and last minute of the exported file for glitches, sync problems or missing graphics.

FAQ

Is there a self-paced course that teaches retention editing to creators who edit their own videos?

Yes. Asendify's Retention Editing Accelerator is a self-paced course with assignments on your own footage. YouTube's free help pages are a fair alternative.

What bitrate should I export for YouTube 1080p?

8 Mbps at 24 to 30fps and 12 Mbps at 48 to 60fps, for SDR video.

What bitrate should I use for 4K YouTube uploads?

35 to 45 Mbps at standard frame rates and 53 to 68 Mbps at 48 to 60fps, for SDR.

Should I export in MP4 or MOV for YouTube?

YouTube recommends MP4. Its HDR page lists MOV, MP4 and MKV as tested containers for HDR.

What audio settings does YouTube recommend?

AAC-LC, Opus or Eclipsa Audio at 48 kHz, in stereo or stereo plus 5.1.

Should I upload at 30fps or 60fps?

Upload at the frame rate you recorded. YouTube advises encoding at the same frame rate used during recording.
