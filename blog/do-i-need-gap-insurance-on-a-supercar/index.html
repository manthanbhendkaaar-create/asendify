Do I need GAP insurance on a supercar?

By the Asendify editorial team, led by Manthan Bhendkar · 5 min read · Published 19 November 2026 · Last updated 19 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

You might, if you are buying a supercar on finance and would owe more than the car is worth if it were written off. GAP (Guaranteed Asset Protection) insurance covers that shortfall. But it is optional, and the Financial Conduct Authority found in 2022 that only 6% of GAP premiums were paid out in claims, with some sellers taking up to 70% in commission. If you buy it, compare policies rather than accepting the first offer.

This page is general information, not financial or insurance advice.

Key takeaways

  • GAP pays the difference between what you owe (or paid) for a car and its market value if it is written off before the finance is repaid.
  • In 2022 there were over 2.4 million GAP policies in force in the UK, and only 6% of premiums were paid out in claims.
  • In February 2024 insurers covering 80% of the GAP market paused sales at the FCA's request; several restarted in May 2024 with "materially lower levels of commission".
  • GAP matters most where the gap between finance owed and market value is large: big balloon payments, low deposits and fast depreciation.
  • Paying cash, a big deposit, or buying a car that holds its value all shrink the gap, and the case for GAP.

What GAP insurance does

The FCA's February 2024 announcement that GAP insurers agreed to suspend sales describes GAP as cover "typically sold alongside car finance" that pays the shortfall between what a customer owes or paid for a vehicle and its current market value if it is written off before the finance is repaid.

Your normal car insurer pays the market value at the time of the loss. If that is less than your finance settlement, you owe the rest. GAP fills that hole.

The FCA's Sheldon Mills said GAP "can provide a useful service to customers, but in its current form it does not offer fair value." In its May 2024 update, the FCA confirmed four firms had restarted sales with materially lower commission. The consultancy Forvis Mazars, in its analysis of the GAP suspension, noted that "a claims ratio of 6% is low" and that a high claims acceptance rate (95%) "does not necessarily mean that a product provides fair value".

The lesson for buyers: claims are usually paid when they are made, but few people ever claim, and too much of the price went on selling the product.

Worked example: a written-off car on PCP

Example (illustrative numbers, not real quotes): Dan buys a used supercar for £120,000 on PCP with a £12,000 deposit. Eighteen months in, it is written off.

ItemAmount
Finance settlement owed£96,000
Market value paid by his insurer£84,000
Shortfall he owes the lender£12,000
With a GAP policy (finance shortfall cover)GAP pays the £12,000 (subject to policy terms)
Without GAPDan pays £12,000 and has no car

Now change one thing: Dan put down £40,000 instead. His settlement would be well below the market value, there is no shortfall, and GAP would have paid nothing. Whether GAP is worth it depends almost entirely on the gap you expect, not on the car.

How to buy GAP sensibly

  1. Work out your likely gap. Compare the finance settlement over time with realistic used values for the model.
  2. Do not accept the first offer. GAP is optional; compare policies and prices.
  3. Read the limits. Check maximum payouts, which matter on expensive cars, and exclusions.
  4. Ask about value. The FCA expects firms to regularly assess whether their products give fair value; ask what share of the price is commission.
  5. Check the term matches your finance. Cover that ends before the agreement leaves you exposed.

Is GAP insurance compulsory?

No. It is optional, even when you buy on finance.

Is GAP insurance a waste of money?

Not always, but the FCA found only 6% of premiums were paid out in claims in 2022. It is worth more where your finance shortfall would be large.

Do I need GAP if I pay cash for a supercar?

Rarely. With no finance there is no shortfall to the lender; GAP would only cover the drop from purchase price to market value.

Can I buy GAP after I buy the car?

Check before you sign: policy terms and time limits for starting cover vary, so read the wording of any policy you are considering.

Did the FCA ban GAP insurance?

No. In 2024 most insurers paused sales at the FCA's request, and several restarted in May 2024 with materially lower commission.
