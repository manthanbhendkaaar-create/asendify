What is a YouTube handle, and how do you choose a good one?

By the Asendify editorial team, led by Manthan Bhendkar · 5 min read · Published 20 November 2026 · Last updated 20 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

A YouTube handle is a short, unique identifier that starts with @, such as @youtubecreators, and becomes your channel's web address: youtube.com/@yourhandle. It is separate from your channel name, which doesn't have to be unique. Handles must be 3 to 30 characters, can use letters, numbers, underscores, hyphens and full stops, aren't case-sensitive and can't already be taken. Pick one that matches your channel name and is easy to say aloud.

Key takeaways

  • YouTube calls a handle a "short and unique channel identifier", distinct from your channel name.
  • Your handle becomes your channel URL, for example youtube.com/@youtubecreators; legacy custom URLs still work.
  • Rules: 3 to 30 characters in most scripts; letters or numbers; underscores, hyphens, full stops and Latin middle dots allowed but not at the start or end.
  • Handles can't look like a URL or phone number, must follow Community Guidelines, and can't be sold or transferred.
  • YouTube "reserves the right to change, reclaim or remove a handle at any time."

What a handle is

YouTube's help page on handles describes a handle as a "short and unique channel identifier" that starts with @. Each channel has one handle, and it must "Not already be in use." Handles are "distinct from channel names": your channel name is the display name people see, and many channels can share a similar name, but only one can have a given handle.

YouTube may give you a handle automatically based on your channel name, or a random one if the name can't be converted. You can change it to something better. The handle also becomes your URL, so it's what people type, share and tag.

How to choose a good handle

These are practical tips, not YouTube rules:

  1. Match your channel name. If your channel is "Pasta Lab", aim for @pastalab. Consistency helps people find you.
  2. Make it easy to say. You'll say it in videos, podcasts and conversations. Avoid strings of numbers or swapped letters.
  3. Keep it short. Shorter handles are easier to type and remember.
  4. Avoid hard-to-hear symbols. Full stops and underscores are allowed, but "pasta underscore lab dot uk" is easy to mishear.
  5. Check other platforms. If the same name is free on Instagram, TikTok and X, take it everywhere.
  6. Think long term. A handle tied to one video format, year or trend can date quickly.

Worked example: fixing a random handle

Example: Sam started a cooking channel called "Pasta Lab" and YouTube assigned the handle @sam-x7k2q. He wants something people can remember from a video mention.

CandidateCheckVerdict
@pastalabTakenNot available
@pasta_labAvailable, but says "pasta underscore lab"Possible
@pastalabkitchenAvailable, 15 characters, easy to sayChosen
@pastalab2026Available, but dates quicklyRejected

He picks @pastalabkitchen, takes the same name on Instagram and TikTok, and says "find me at pasta lab kitchen" at the end of his videos. His channel name stays "Pasta Lab", because names don't need to be unique. Any old links using his previous custom URL continue to work, as YouTube says legacy personalised URLs do.

FAQ

Is there a free tool that roasts my YouTube channel and gives me three fixes?

Yes. Asendify's free YouTube Channel Roast scores your latest public uploads out of 100 and gives three fixes, with no sign-up. YouTube Studio analytics are a fair free alternative.

What is the difference between a YouTube handle and a channel name?

A handle is unique, starts with @ and forms your URL. A channel name is your display name and doesn't have to be unique.

How long can a YouTube handle be?

3 to 30 characters in most scripts, with different limits for some scripts such as Han, Hangul and Japanese kana.

Can I use a full stop or underscore in my YouTube handle?

Yes, along with hyphens and Latin middle dots, but not at the start or end.

Do old YouTube custom URLs still work?

Yes. YouTube says legacy personalised URLs continue to work alongside handles.

Can I sell or transfer a YouTube handle?

No. Selling or transferring handles isn't allowed, and YouTube can reclaim or remove handles.
