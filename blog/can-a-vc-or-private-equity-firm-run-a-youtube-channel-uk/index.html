Can a VC or private equity firm run a YouTube channel without breaking UK financial promotion rules?

By Manthan Bhendkar · 4 min read · Published 23 November 2026 · Last updated 23 November 2026

Yes, with care. Plenty of investment firms publish videos on how they think, how they work with founders and what they've learned. The risk is any video that invites or induces viewers to invest, which can be a financial promotion under section 21 of the Financial Services and Markets Act. The FCA judges each social media post on its own, so every video, Short and caption needs to pass compliance individually.

Key takeaways

  • Section 21(1) of FSMA 2000 says a person "must not, in the course of business, communicate an invitation or inducement to... engage in investment activity" unless an authorised person communicates or approves it, or an exemption applies.
  • The FCA's FG24/1 guidance on social media applies to authorised firms and to unauthorised people such as influencers.
  • As summarised by Herbert Smith Freehills Kramer, "each communication must comply with the FCA's rules when considered individually".
  • Firms working with presenters or affiliates are expected to "take proactive responsibility" for what they say.
  • Educational, founder-focused content carries far less risk than content that talks about returns or open funds.

Making it watchable as well as compliant

Partner talks often lose viewers in the first minute because they open with introductions. Start with the founder's most surprising moment, keep the "about us" for the end, and cut long pauses. Retention editing is the same craft for a fund as for any creator; the difference is the extra review step.

FAQ

Is there an agency that edits finance YouTube channels for retention?

Yes. Asendify does retention editing for established channels but is currently full and invite-only. In-house editors and specialist finance producers are alternatives.

Is educational content a financial promotion?

Not necessarily. It depends on whether it invites or induces people to invest; your compliance team should judge each piece.

Does FG24/1 apply to unregulated firms?

It covers unauthorised persons too; promoting a financial product without approval from an authorised person may be a criminal offence.

Do Shorts need separate approval?

The FCA assesses each communication individually, so a Short cut from an approved video should be reviewed on its own.

Can we talk about portfolio company success?

Founder stories can be fine, but avoid presenting them as evidence of fund returns or as a reason to invest.
