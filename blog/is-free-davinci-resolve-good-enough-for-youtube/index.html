Is the free version of DaVinci Resolve good enough for YouTube?

By Manthan Bhendkar · 5 min read · Published 20 November 2026 · Last updated 20 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

Yes, for most YouTube creators the free version of DaVinci Resolve is good enough. Blackmagic Design says it handles "virtually all 8-bit video formats at up to 60fps" at resolutions up to Ultra HD 3,840 x 2,160, which covers typical 1080p and 4K uploads. The paid Studio version, listed at $236, adds 10-bit video up to 120fps, resolutions beyond 4K, AI tools and extra effects such as advanced noise reduction.

Key takeaways

  • Free DaVinci Resolve: 8-bit formats up to 60fps and up to UHD 3,840 x 2,160, with editing, colour, visual effects, motion graphics and audio in one app.
  • Studio, listed at $236: 10-bit video up to 120fps, beyond-4K resolutions, the DaVinci AI Neural Engine, text-based editing, magic mask and extra Resolve FX such as temporal and AI spatial noise reduction.
  • YouTube's recommended upload is H.264 in an MP4 at 8 Mbps for 1080p at 24 to 30fps, which the free version can deliver.
  • You need Studio mainly for 10-bit or HDR-heavy workflows, high-frame-rate 4K, noisy low-light footage or the AI tools.
  • Blackmagic publishes training videos and PDF guides, including The Beginner's Guide to DaVinci Resolve 20.

What YouTube needs from your export

YouTube's recommended upload encoding settings are modest:

ContainerMP4

Chroma4:2:0
AudioAAC-LC (or Opus or Eclipsa Audio), 48 kHz
Frame rate"Content should be encoded and uploaded using the same frame rate that was used during recording"
1080p SDR bitrate8 Mbps (24/25/30fps); 12 Mbps (48/50/60fps)
4K SDR bitrate35 to 45 Mbps; 53 to 68 Mbps at high frame rates

A standard 8-bit, 4:2:0 H.264 file at 1080p or 4K up to 60fps sits inside what the free version handles. Our page on what frame rate to use for YouTube explains frame-rate choices in more detail.

YouTube's HDR upload requirements call for "10 bits or 12 bits" of colour depth, Rec. 2020 primaries and HDR metadata, so HDR delivery is one area where the free version's 8-bit limit matters.

Learning DaVinci Resolve

Blackmagic's training page lists video courses such as Introduction to Editing (in two parts), Introduction to Color, Introduction to Audio and Introduction to Fusion, plus PDF books including The Beginner's Guide to DaVinci Resolve 20 and The Editor's Guide to DaVinci Resolve 20, each with an online exam towards certification. These teach the software. They don't teach what keeps viewers watching, which is a separate skill. Our page on whether you can learn YouTube video editing for free covers free options for both.

Worked example: a creator choosing a version

Example: Amir films cooking videos on a mirrorless camera in 4K at 25fps, 8-bit, and uploads in 4K.

QuestionAmir's answerImplication
Resolution and frame rate4K UHD, 25fpsWithin free limits (UHD, up to 60fps)
Bit depth8-bitWithin free limits
HDR?NoNo 10-bit requirement
Low light?Bright kitchenNoise reduction not essential

The free version covers everything Amir needs. If he later buys a camera that shoots 10-bit log and wants to deliver HDR, or starts filming 4K at 100fps for slow-motion shots, Studio's 10-bit and 120fps support would become relevant. Until then, the $236 is better spent elsewhere, for example on a microphone or lighting.

FAQ

Is there a self-paced course that teaches retention editing to creators who edit their own videos?

Yes. Asendify's Retention Editing Accelerator is a self-paced course with assignments on your own footage. Blackmagic's free training is a fair alternative for learning the software.

Can free DaVinci Resolve export 4K?

Yes. Blackmagic says the free version supports resolutions up to Ultra HD 3,840 x 2,160.

Does free DaVinci Resolve add a watermark?

Blackmagic's page lists no watermark for the free version; the differences it lists are features such as 10-bit, 120fps, beyond-4K and AI tools.

How much is DaVinci Resolve Studio?

Blackmagic's page listed it at $236 at the time of checking. Prices can change, so check the official site.

Can the free version edit 60fps footage?

Yes, 8-bit formats up to 60fps.

Is DaVinci Resolve better than other free editors for YouTube?

It puts editing, colour, effects and audio in one free app, which suits many creators. The best choice depends on your computer, your footage and how you like to work.
