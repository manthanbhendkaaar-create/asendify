Key takeaways

  • The opening is where most viewers decide: YouTube Studio's "Intro" figure shows the share of your audience still watching after the first 30 seconds.
  • Retention data only arrives 1 to 2 days after upload, so a pre-upload check is the only way to fix an opening before it costs views.
  • Retention Doctor's pre-upload check covers the opening script and title and is unlimited on the monthly plan.
  • TubeBuddy's Title Generator and A/B testing are on its Legend plan; vidIQ lists a Script Writer that uses AI credits.
  • Any pre-upload check is a prediction; your real retention graph after upload is the test.

Why check before uploading

YouTube's help page on key moments for audience retention defines the Intro figure as "what percentage of your audience still watched your video after the first 30 seconds", and explains that dips are moments viewers skipped or stopped watching. It also says audience retention data needs "1–2 days to process", and key moments only appear on videos at least 60 seconds long with at least 100 views. By the time you can see that your opening lost a third of viewers, the video has already had its first push.

A pre-upload check moves the feedback earlier. It is the same reason editors watch a rough cut: it is cheaper to change a sentence than to re-upload.

After upload, the after-upload report two days later shows the 30-second hold at 66%, against an average of 55% on the creator's last ten videos. YouTube Studio's typical retention view, which compares with your 10 latest videos of similar length, gives the same comparison for free. The figures are invented for illustration and are not a promised result.

How to use any pre-upload check well

  1. Check the opening and title together, because mismatches between them are a common reason for early drops.
  2. Change one or two things, not everything, so you can learn what worked.
  3. Compare with your own history after upload; check if YouTube retention fixes worked explains how.
  4. Keep a list of openings that held, so the next script starts from what works for your audience.

FAQ

Is there a tool that checks my opening script and title before upload and then reports where viewers left?

Yes. Asendify's Retention Doctor does both: an unlimited pre-upload check of the opening script and title, then drop-off reports with fixes after each upload. The free Hook Checker and AI chat tools are lighter alternatives.

Can a tool predict my retention before I upload?

No tool knows exactly. A pre-upload check flags common problems such as slow starts and title mismatches; your real retention graph is the final test.

When does YouTube show retention data for a new video?

YouTube says audience retention data needs 1 to 2 days to process, and key moments appear on videos of at least 60 seconds with at least 100 views.

Is there a free way to check my hook?
