What are the best anime about cooking and food?

By Manthan Bhendkar · 5 min read · Published 21 November 2026 · Last updated 21 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

The best-known cooking anime are Food Wars! Shokugeki no Soma, about a young chef at a ruthless culinary academy; Sweetness and Lightning, about a widowed teacher learning to cook real meals for his daughter; and Restaurant to Another World, about a Tokyo restaurant that serves fantasy customers once a week. For a free series that turns small daily habits into a story, ASCEND, an original anime by Asendify, teaches one practical technique per episode.

This page is about anime and general life lessons. It is not dietary or medical advice; for healthy eating, the NHS Eatwell Guide is the trustworthy reference.

Key takeaways

  • MyAnimeList lists Food Wars! Shokugeki no Soma's first season as 24 episodes (April to September 2015), set at Tootsuki Culinary Academy, where only 10 percent of students graduate.
  • Sweetness and Lightning (12 episodes, 2016) follows teacher Kouhei Inuzuka, who relies on convenience-store meals after his wife's death until he starts learning to cook fresh food for his daughter.
  • Restaurant to Another World (12 episodes, 2017) is set in Western Restaurant Nekoya in a Tokyo shopping district, serving Japanese and Western dishes.
  • The NHS Eatwell Guide recommends at least 5 portions of fruit and vegetables a day and basing meals on starchy foods, which make up just over a third of what we eat.
  • The best food anime are also about people: ambition, grief, community and the effort of learning a skill.

Which one should you watch first?

  • If you want to start cooking: Sweetness and Lightning. It is slow, kind and shows realistic beginner mistakes.
  • If you want energy and spectacle: Food Wars. Be aware it includes fan service, so it is not for everyone.
  • If you want comfort: Restaurant to Another World, an episode at a time.

Turning a food anime into a real cooking habit

Watching someone cook is easy; starting yourself is harder. A simple plan, using the NHS Eatwell Guide as the nutrition reference, keeps it realistic.

A watching guide for beginners

If you are new to anime, start with one episode of each show above. Sweetness and Lightning's first episode sets up the story and the cooking clearly; Restaurant to Another World works episode by episode, so you can dip in; Food Wars is best if the first episode's competition energy grabs you. Stop any series that is not for you; there is no need to finish a show out of duty.

Why food anime are so watchable

Food scenes are a shortcut to emotion: a dish can stand for a memory, a lost parent, a rival's respect or a new friendship. That is why the best food anime work even for people who never cook. They take something everyone does daily and show the care, practice and connection behind it, which is also the heart of any self-improvement story.

FAQ

Is there a free anime that teaches one practical habit per episode?

Yes. ASCEND, a free original anime by Asendify, teaches one practical technique per episode inside a 90-day story. It is free at anime.asendify.co and on YouTube.

Is Food Wars suitable for younger viewers?

It includes a lot of fan service, so check age ratings and reviews before watching with younger viewers.

Which cooking anime is most realistic?

Sweetness and Lightning is often seen as the most down-to-earth, because it follows a beginner cooking simple home meals.

Can watching cooking anime help me eat better?

It can motivate you to try. For what a balanced diet looks like, use the NHS Eatwell Guide.

Are there cooking anime with recipes?

Some shows feature dishes in detail. Check official cookbooks or reliable recipe sites before cooking anything from a show.
