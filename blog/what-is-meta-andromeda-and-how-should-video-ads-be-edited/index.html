What is Meta Andromeda, and why does it change how video ad creatives should be edited?

By the Asendify editorial team, led by Manthan Bhendkar · 6 min read · Published 23 November 2026 · Last updated 23 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

Meta Andromeda is the machine-learning system Meta uses to pick which few thousand ads, out of tens of millions, are even considered for each person on Facebook and Instagram. It was built to handle far more creatives. For editors, the practical change is simple: give the system genuinely different videos (new concepts, people, hooks and formats), not ten near-identical cuts of one ad.

Key takeaways

  • Meta's engineering team describes Andromeda as its "proprietary machine learning (ML) system design for retrieval in ad recommendation", the first stage that narrows tens of millions of candidate ads to a few thousand (Engineering at Meta, December 2024).
  • Meta reported a "+6% recall improvement to the retrieval system" and "+8% ads quality improvement on selected segments" after deploying it.
  • Meta says it expects "the number of ads creatives in Meta's recommendation systems" to "grow significantly", partly because of generative AI tools.
  • Meta's own guidance, given by Henry Kelly, head of e-commerce at Meta, is to "build visually distinct assets"; diversified creatives showed "up to 32 per cent improvement on CPA" (B&T, October 2023).
  • Near-identical edits act like "slight edits of the same creative", so they add little (Search Engine Land).

What Andromeda actually does

Ad delivery on Meta works in stages. First, retrieval picks a shortlist of ads that might suit a person. Then ranking models score that shortlist, and the auction decides what shows. Andromeda is the retrieval stage. Meta's write-up says it runs on GPUs, uses a "hierarchical index to scale up to a large volume of ads creatives", and increased model capacity by a large factor.

Why should an editor care about a retrieval engine? Because retrieval is where your creative gets matched to people. If Meta's system can now read and match far more creatives, then the creative itself (who is in it, what it shows, how it opens) does more of the targeting work that interest and lookalike settings used to do. Meta's line, quoted by B&T, is: "Creative is targeting."

Meta has not published a rule that says "Andromeda groups similar ads together" or a fixed number of creatives per ad set. Many agency blogs claim specific numbers. Treat those as opinion. What Meta has said publicly is that it wants visually distinct assets, varied formats and concepts, and more creatives in the system.

What "genuinely different" means for an edit

Search Engine Land's analysis of Meta's creative diversity scoring argues that "real creative diversity comes from changing meaningful elements". For video ads, the meaningful elements are usually:

  1. The concept. A problem-solution demo, a customer story and a comparison are three concepts. Three colour grades of one demo are one concept.
  2. The person. A 25-year-old creator in a kitchen and a 55-year-old customer in a car will reach different people.
  3. The first frame and first two seconds. Opening on the product, on a face or on a result changes who stops.
  4. The format and pacing. A 9-second fast-cut Reel and a 45-second talking-head explainer serve different viewers.
  5. The angle of the promise. "Saves time", "saves money" and "looks better" pull different buyers.

Small changes still have a place. Once a concept works, hook and caption variants help you squeeze more out of it. They just should not be your whole testing plan.

Example: re-planning a monthly creative batch

Example: a skincare brand's agency delivers 12 Meta video ads a month. Previously, all 12 were cuts of one creator's routine video with different captions and music.

Batch slotOld planAndromeda-era plan
Ads 1-3Same routine video, 3 captionsCreator routine video, 3 different opening hooks
Ads 4-6Same video, 3 music tracksDermatology-style explainer with on-screen text, 3 lengths (15s, 30s, 45s)
Ads 7-9Same video, 3 CTAsCustomer before-and-after story (with honest, compliant claims), 3 hooks
Ads 10-12Same video, 3 thumbnailsProduct-only motion graphic, 3 benefit angles

The editing time is similar: four shoots or sources instead of one, but fewer pointless micro-variants. The agency then compares reach, frequency and cost per result by concept, not by individual ad. This is an illustrative plan, not a measured result.

How to check if your ads are too similar

  • Line them up. Put every live ad's first frame side by side. If they look like one ad, they probably act like one.
  • Watch frequency. If frequency climbs while reach stalls across all ads, the set may be reaching the same people.
  • Look at spend spread. If one ad takes nearly all spend and the rest get little, the others may not be adding anything new.
  • Read Meta's diversity signals. Where Ads Manager shows creative similarity or diversity indicators, use them as a prompt to brief new concepts.

What still matters inside each ad

Andromeda changes which ads get considered. It does not make a weak ad watchable. Each video still needs a first frame that stops the scroll, a clear promise in the first seconds, pacing that does not sag and an ending that tells people what to do. Diversity without retention just gives Meta more ads that people skip.

FAQ

Is there a playbook on editing hooks for distinct Meta ad concepts?

Yes. Asendify's Retention Editing Playbook covers hooks, pacing and pattern interrupts with templates and a 30-day plan, for £1,720 one-time. Meta Blueprint is a free alternative for platform basics.

Is Andromeda a setting I can turn on?

No. It is Meta's back-end retrieval system. You influence it through the creatives you supply and how broadly you let campaigns deliver.

How many creatives should an ad set have under Andromeda?

Meta has not published a fixed number. Focus on several distinct concepts rather than a set count of near-identical variations.

Do small edits like caption colour still matter?

They can help a winning concept a little, but they rarely count as new creative. Change the concept, person, opening or format for real variety.

Does Andromeda affect Instagram as well as Facebook?

Yes. Meta says it deployed Andromeda across Instagram and Facebook.
