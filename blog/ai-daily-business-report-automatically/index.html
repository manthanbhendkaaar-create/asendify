How do I get AI to send me a daily business report automatically?

By Manthan Bhendkar · 6 min read · Published 13 November 2026 · Last updated 13 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

The simplest way is to set up a scheduled task in an AI agent tool such as Claude Cowork: connect it to the places your numbers live (email, spreadsheets, files), write one clear prompt describing the report, and set it to run every weekday morning. Have it save or draft the report first, check the first week of runs against your own figures, and only then let it send.

Key takeaways

  • A daily report needs three things: access to your data, a fixed report template, and a schedule.
  • In Claude Cowork, scheduled tasks run remotely on paid plans, so the report can be ready before you wake up.
  • Keep the report short: five to eight numbers and a few lines of "what changed and why".
  • Start with read-only access and drafts. Approve sending once you trust the output.
  • Check the AI's figures against the source for at least a week; AI can misread data.

What a useful daily business report contains

A daily report is only worth automating if you would act on it. For a small online business, that usually means sales, cash and anything that needs a reply.

The cash line matters more than many owners think. The British Business Bank's guide to cash flow warns that "It's possible for a business to be profitable while having a poor cash flow", and advises: "Never ignore a cash shortfall." A daily glance at cash in and out is a simple guard against surprises.

How to set it up in Claude Cowork

Claude Cowork is Anthropic's agent mode for multi-step work. Its getting started guide says Cowork is on paid plans (Pro, Max, Ultra, Team and Enterprise), that Claude can only read and write files in folders you have connected, and that each connector can be set to "Always allow", "Needs approval" or "Blocked".

Anthropic's help article on scheduling recurring tasks explains the scheduling part:

  1. Open Scheduled in the left sidebar and click New task.
  2. Choose Create with Claude to describe the report in conversation, or Set up manually to fill in a name, prompt, approval mode, frequency, optional model and optional working folder.
  3. For a daily report, pick weekdays or daily as the frequency.
  4. Save, then check each run from the Scheduled page, where you can edit, pause, resume, delete or run the task on demand.

Scheduled tasks run remotely and keep going when your computer is off or the app is closed, except tasks that need local files or apps, which only run locally. So keep the report's inputs in connected services or files saved to your Claude account.

Writing the report prompt

The prompt is your report template. A good one covers:

  • Sources: "Read yesterday's orders from the sales export in the Reports folder and yesterday's emails in the inbox."
  • Calculations: "Compare revenue with the same weekday last week; show the percentage change."
  • Format: "Use these headings: Sales, Cash, Leads, Issues, Marketing, Actions. Under 250 words."
  • Rules: "If a source is missing, say which one in the first line. Do not guess numbers. Do not send; save as a draft."
  • Tone: "Plain English. No filler."

Anthropic's best practices for getting started with Cowork suggest starting with a real task you know well, giving rich context, describing the deliverable and reviewing the output. A report you already write by hand is ideal, because you know what good looks like.

Example: a one-person online shop

Example: Rhea sells printed planners online. Each morning she used to open four tabs and spend about 25 minutes piecing together yesterday.

SettingWhat she chose
FrequencyWeekdays, early morning
InputsHer shop's daily sales export saved to a connected folder; her support inbox via a connector set to read only
OutputA draft note titled with the date, saved to her Reports folder
TemplateSales vs same day last week; refunds; new emails needing a reply; three actions
RulesNever send emails; never invent numbers; flag missing files

In week one she compared every figure with her shop dashboard. On day two the task misread a refund as a sale; she added "Refunds are rows where type = refund" to the prompt, and the error did not recur. By week two she was reading the note in about five minutes. These are her own estimates, not measured results, and your setup will differ.

Safety and accuracy checklist

  • Least access. Give the task only the folders and connectors it needs. Use "Needs approval" for anything that can send or change things.
  • No silent guessing. Tell the task to flag missing data rather than estimate.
  • Spot-check. Compare one or two figures with the source every week, even after it is working.
  • Keep money decisions human. Anthropic recommends staying close to tasks with real consequences, such as money, messages sent on your behalf or important files.
  • Watch for sensitive data. Avoid pulling personal customer details into the report unless you need them.

FAQ

Can AI email me a daily report automatically?

Yes, if the tool can run on a schedule and has access to your data and email. It is safer to start with drafts you send or read yourself, then allow sending once you trust it.

Does my computer need to be on for a Cowork scheduled task?

Not usually. Anthropic says scheduled tasks run remotely, except tasks that need local files or apps.

What should a small business track daily?

Typically sales, cash in and out, new leads, customer issues and marketing spend. Track only what you would act on.

How accurate are AI-generated business reports?

They can be accurate when the data is clean and the instructions are precise, but AI can misread columns or formats. Check figures against the source, especially in the first weeks.

Is it safe to connect my business email to an AI tool?

Use the narrowest permissions you can, such as read only or "Needs approval". Review which connectors are allowed and remove ones you no longer use.
