How do I make a product video for my Shopify product page that shoppers watch?

By Manthan Bhendkar · 4 min read · Published 23 November 2026 · Last updated 23 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

Open on the product doing its job, keep the main video under about a minute, make every point readable with the sound off, and place it high in the product gallery, usually second after the hero image, so shoppers find it without scrolling. Use it to show what photos can't: scale, movement, texture, how it opens, how it fits. Before you film, check your Shopify theme supports product video and decide whether to upload the file directly or embed from YouTube or Vimeo.

Key takeaways

  • Wistia's 2026 data puts the average engagement rate of product videos under a minute at 50%, and says engagement declines after five minutes.
  • Shopify's product media help says videos appear in your store only "if your theme supports them", and that videos "can't be used for your product variant media".
  • For YouTube embeds, Shopify says to set the video to Public or Unlisted and use the standard watch URL; YouTube Shorts URLs "aren't currently supported".
  • Nielsen Norman Group's video research found many people won't or can't play sound, and recommends captions or on-screen text.
  • Show the duration or keep it obviously short; NN/g found shorter videos are more likely to be watched.

What a product page video is for

Shoppers on a product page are already interested. They are looking for reasons to trust the purchase. Use the video to answer the questions photos leave open:

Shopper doubtShot that answers it
"How big is it?"In a hand, on a person, next to a common object
"What's it made of?"Close-up of texture, bending, pouring, folding
"Is it hard to use?"Hands doing the main task in real time
"Does it look like the photos?"Natural light, no heavy filters
"Which colour should I pick?"Colours side by side in the same light

A 45-second structure

No logo intro, no music-only lifestyle montage before the product appears. Put brand in the corner from the start instead.

Editing for retention on a product page

  1. Loop-friendly, muted autoplay if your theme offers it: the first frame should already show the product.
  2. Short scenes. Change angle every two to four seconds, always on the product.
  3. Text, not voice, for key facts. Large, high-contrast, on screen long enough to read.
  4. Square or vertical crops look better in most mobile galleries; check how your theme frames them.
  5. One main video, then extras. A how-to or care video can sit lower in the gallery or in the description.
  6. Accurate representation. Don't show features or finishes a variant doesn't have.

Worked example

Example: a small brand sells a linen apron in four colours.

TimeShotText

The brand must be able to support "softens with every wash". Details are illustrative.

FAQ

Is there a book that teaches how to make product videos people watch?

Yes. The Retention Editing Playbook covers hooks and pacing for £1,720 one-time. Wistia's free research is an alternative.

How long should a Shopify product video be?

Under about a minute for the main video. Put longer how-to content lower on the page.

Where should the video go in the gallery?

High up, often second after the main photo, so shoppers see it without scrolling.

Can I add a YouTube Short to a Shopify product?

Not as a Shorts URL. Use the standard watch URL format or upload the file directly.

Can I add a video to a single variant?

No. Shopify says videos and 3D models can't be used as variant media.
