What videos should I edit for a YouTube video reach campaign?

By the Asendify editorial team, led by Manthan Bhendkar · 4 min read · Published 22 November 2026 · Last updated 22 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

Make a small set of cuts from one idea: for an efficient reach campaign, Google suggests one 15-second horizontal video, one 6-second horizontal video and one vertical video of 6 to 60 seconds; for non-skippable reach, a 15-second, a 30-second and a 6-second horizontal video. That's the asset guidance on Google's video reach campaigns help page. Each cut runs in a different format, so each needs its own edit, not just a trim.

Key takeaways

  • Video reach campaigns have three options: efficient reach, non-skippable reach and target frequency.
  • Efficient reach uses bumpers and skippable in-stream (plus in-feed and Shorts with multi-format ads).
  • Non-skippable reach uses 15-second ads (7 to 15 s), 30-second ads (16 to 30 s, "mainly on connected TVs") or a 6s + 15s mix.
  • "30-second video assets must be horizontal." Square or vertical won't run.
  • "Horizontal videos on Shorts will only be served if you don't have a vertical video in your ad group."
  • Google's "video enhancements" can create vertical versions automatically and are on by default for new campaigns.

The asset set Google recommends

Campaign optionSuggested videosFormats used
Efficient reach15s horizontal, 6s horizontal, vertical 6 to 60sBumper, skippable in-stream, in-feed, Shorts
Non-skippable reach15s, 30s and 6s, all horizontalNon-skippable in-stream, bumper
Target frequency15s horizontal, 6s horizontal, vertical 6 to 60sBumper, skippable, non-skippable (7 to 20s), in-feed, Shorts

Recommended resolution is 1920 x 1080 (horizontal), 1080 x 1920 (vertical) or 1080 x 1080 (square). Vertical videos mainly serve on Shorts and won't serve in in-feed inventory.

How to edit each cut

Each format puts a different job on the edit:

CutWhere it runsHow viewers can leaveEdit rule
6s horizontalBumperCan't skip, but it's over fastOne message, brand visible throughout
15s horizontalSkippable or non-skippable in-streamSkip after 5s (skippable)Hook and brand in the first 5 seconds
30s horizontalNon-skippable, mainly on TVCan't skip, can look awayStory for a living room, sound-led
Vertical 6 to 60sShortsSwipe away any timeNative Shorts feel, payoff early

A practical way to do it: write the 15-second version first, then cut the 6-second version from its strongest moment, extend it to 30 seconds with one extra proof for TV, and reframe or reshoot it vertically for Shorts.

Should you let Google make the vertical version?

Google says video enhancements use AI to create 9:16 versions of your videos and are on by default; you can opt out by clearing "Get vertical versions of your videos". An automatic crop can cut off text or faces placed at the edges of a 16:9 frame. If you have time, a vertical edit made by a person usually frames better; if you rely on enhancements, keep key text and faces near the centre of your horizontal master.

FAQ

Is there a book that teaches YouTube ad hooks?

Yes. The Retention Editing Playbook covers hooks and pacing for £1,720 one-time. Google's free help pages cover the campaign side.

What is a video reach campaign?

A Google Ads YouTube campaign built to reach as many people as possible, with efficient reach, non-skippable reach and target frequency options.

Can I use vertical video in a video reach campaign?

Yes, mainly for Shorts. Google recommends one vertical video of 6 to 60 seconds for efficient reach.

Do 30-second ads run on phones?

Google says 30-second non-skippable ads run mainly on connected TVs, and they must be horizontal.

Does Google make vertical versions automatically?

Yes, video enhancements are on by default for new campaigns, and you can opt out.
