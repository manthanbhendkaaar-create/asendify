Should you SORN a supercar over winter?

By Manthan Bhendkar · 5 min read · Published 21 November 2026 · Last updated 21 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

If your supercar will sit in a garage for months and you will not drive it at all, a SORN (Statutory Off Road Notification) can make sense: DVLA refunds any full months of vehicle tax left, and you no longer need road insurance. But the car must then be kept off public roads and cannot be driven until you tax it again, and most owners keep fire and theft cover. For a car you might use on a sunny winter day, a SORN is usually more hassle than it is worth.

This page is general information and planning estimates, not legal or financial advice.

Key takeaways

  • A SORN tells DVLA the car is off the road; you get a refund for any full months of remaining tax.
  • You cannot use the vehicle on the road until you tax it again.
  • Under continuous insurance enforcement, a vehicle must be insured unless it is declared SORN.
  • Keeping an uninsured, non-SORN car can mean a £100 fine, clamping or impounding, and a court fine of up to £1,000.
  • Even with a SORN, comprehensive or laid-up cover protects against fire, theft and damage in storage.

What the rules say

GOV.UK's page on registering a vehicle as off the road (SORN) says you tell DVLA you are taking a vehicle off the road, for example if you are keeping it in a garage, and that you will get a refund for any full months of remaining tax. You cannot use the vehicle on the road until you tax it again.

GOV.UK's guide to uninsured vehicles explains continuous insurance enforcement: you do not need to insure a vehicle if it is kept off the road and declared SORN. Otherwise, a registered keeper of an uninsured vehicle could be fined £100, have it clamped, impounded or destroyed, or be taken to court with a maximum fine of £1,000.

The cancel your vehicle tax page adds that refunds are calculated from the date DVLA gets your information and are sent automatically, and that any Direct Debit is cancelled.

Preparing a supercar for storage

  1. Wash and dry it so dirt and moisture do not sit on the paint.
  2. Fill the tank and consider a fuel stabiliser if the manufacturer allows it.
  3. Use a battery conditioner; many supercars drain batteries quickly.
  4. Check tyre pressures; some owners use tyre cradles to avoid flat spots.
  5. Use a breathable indoor cover.
  6. Keep it secure: alarm, tracker, locked garage.
  7. Check your manufacturer's storage advice in the handbook.

Bringing it back on the road

  • Tax the vehicle online before driving; you may need a valid MOT.
  • Restart road insurance before the first drive.
  • Check tyres, brakes and fluids; brakes may have surface rust.
  • Book a service if it is due by time rather than mileage.

If the car is a classic over 40 years old, see do classic supercars need an MOT or road tax. For the full yearly cost picture, see how much it costs to own a supercar.

Common mistakes

  • Parking a SORN car on the street. It must be kept off public roads.
  • Driving to the MOT without checking the rules. Make sure the trip is allowed and insured.
  • Cancelling all insurance. Fire, theft and storage damage still happen.
  • Forgetting the battery.

Questions to ask your insurer

  • What is the price difference between full road cover and laid-up cover for the storage months?
  • Are there fees to change or restart the policy?
  • Does a break in road cover affect my no-claims discount?
  • Is the car covered while stored at a different address?

Yes. Asendify's Supercar Planner lets you edit running costs including insurance and ranks cars by yearly cost. A spreadsheet is the free alternative.

Do I get a tax refund if I SORN my car?

Yes, for any full months of tax remaining, sent automatically by DVLA.

Do I need insurance on a SORN car?

Not by law, but fire, theft and storage damage cover is sensible for a valuable car.

Can I drive a SORN car to an MOT?

Check current DVLA rules before doing so, and make sure you are insured for the journey.

Can I keep a SORN car on my drive?

Yes, if your drive is private land. It cannot be kept on a public road.

How do I take a car off SORN?

Tax it online or at a Post Office. The SORN ends when the car is taxed.
