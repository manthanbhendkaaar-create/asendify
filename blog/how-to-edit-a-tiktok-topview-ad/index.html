How should I edit a TikTok TopView ad, the video people see when they open the app?

By Manthan Bhendkar · 4 min read · Published 22 November 2026 · Last updated 22 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

Build it around two stages: a clean, vertical first three seconds that work as a full-screen opener, then a 9 to 15 second in-feed story. TikTok's TopView ad specifications say "TopView is a video ad that is shown to users upon opening TikTok", playing as a full-screen takeover for about the first three seconds before it turns into an in-feed video. Length can be 5 to 60 seconds, and TikTok recommends 9 to 15.

Key takeaways

  • TopView shows on app open, full screen for about 3 seconds, then continues as an in-feed video.
  • "Video Duration: 5-60s, recommend 9-15s." Vertical 9:16, at least 540 x 960 px.
  • The video can't be silent; avoid harsh noises (alarms, screams, phone rings), especially in the first 3 seconds.
  • In the first 3 seconds, text and logos must not overlap the TikTok logo, skip button or swipe-up link, and plain white backgrounds aren't allowed.
  • No still pictures in the first 3 seconds, no fake play buttons or call screens, and no QR codes or phone numbers.
  • TopView is bought through a sales representative, assets are reviewed within 2 days, and they can't be changed after launch.

The two stages of a TopView

StageWhat the viewer seesEdit priority
0 to ~3 s: open screenFull-screen takeover with a skip buttonA strong, clean visual; nothing hidden under TikTok's logo or skip button
~3 s onwards: in-feedNormal feed post with caption, username and buttonsStory and product inside the stricter in-feed safe zone

TikTok supplies two safe-zone files, one for each stage, and says the in-feed zone is more restrictive. Longer captions shrink the safe zone, so check the final caption in TikTok's preview tool.

TikTok's first-3-second rules

From TikTok's spec page:

  1. Vertical from the first frame. Horizontal trailers are only allowed if they stay vertical for the first 3 seconds.
  2. No plain white. It makes the TikTok logo hard to see.
  3. Keep clear of the overlays. Text, logos or key messages can't overlap the TikTok logo, skip button or swipe-up link.
  4. No stills. Still-picture elements can't appear in the first 3 seconds and can't fill more than half the video.
  5. No jarring sound. Avoid alarms, screams or phone rings, especially at the start.
  6. No tricks. Fake play buttons, call screens or text-message screens aren't allowed.

These rules shape the hook. You can't shock people into watching with noise or a fake notification, so the opening has to earn attention with motion, a person or the product.

A 12-second edit plan

SecondsStageWhat happens
0 to 3Open screenA person or product in motion, rich colour, brand subtly visible clear of the overlays
3 to 8In-feedThe benefit, shown; short captions inside the safe zone
8 to 11In-feedProof: a demo, reaction or result
11 to 12In-feedThe ask, matched to your chosen CTA

Remember the audio rules: the soundtrack must be present and clear, can't be purely verbal, and a promotional line can't be repeated more than twice in a row.
