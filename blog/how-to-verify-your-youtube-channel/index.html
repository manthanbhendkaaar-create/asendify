How do you verify your YouTube channel, and what does it unlock?

By Manthan Bhendkar · 5 min read · Published 21 November 2026 · Last updated 21 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

To verify your YouTube channel, go to YouTube Studio > Settings > Channel > Feature eligibility and verify your phone number with a code sent by text or voice call. That unlocks intermediate features, including videos longer than 15 minutes, custom thumbnails and live streaming. Advanced features, such as pinned comments, come later with enough channel history, or sooner by verifying your identity with an ID or a video.

Key takeaways

  • YouTube has three feature levels: standard (when you create a channel), intermediate (after phone verification) and advanced.
  • Verification enables "uploading videos longer than 15 minutes, adding custom thumbnails, live streaming, and appealing copyright claims", per YouTube Help.
  • A phone number "can only be associated with 2 channels per year."
  • Advanced features "include, for example, pinned comments and higher daily upload limits."
  • Community Guidelines violations "delay feature eligibility", and creators can lose advanced access.

The three feature levels

YouTube's help page on feature access for creators explains:

LevelHow you get itExamples
StandardAutomatically when you create a channel, with "no active Community Guidelines strikes"Uploading videos, basic features
IntermediateVerify your phone numberVideos over 15 minutes, custom thumbnails, live streaming, appealing Content ID claims
AdvancedPhone verification plus enough channel history, or ID or video verificationPinned comments, higher daily upload limits

You can check yours in YouTube Studio under Settings > Channel > Feature eligibility, where available features show as "Enabled".

How to verify your phone number

YouTube's verification page gives the steps:

  1. Open YouTube Studio, then Settings > Channel > Feature eligibility.
  2. Choose to verify your phone number.
  3. Enter the number and choose text or voice call.
  4. Enter the code you receive.

If the text doesn't arrive within a few minutes, try a voice call. Some carriers don't support Google's texts or calls, in which case a number on another network may work. And remember the limit: a phone number can be linked to only two channels a year. If you see "This phone number has already created the maximum number of accounts," you'll need a different number.

Getting advanced features

YouTube's page on intermediate and advanced features sets out two routes once you've verified your phone:

RouteWhat happens
Channel historyAccess is usually granted automatically once you have "sufficient channel history"
Video verificationRecord a short video to verify identity, where available
ID verificationUpload a valid ID; review usually takes about 24 hours

A few limits to know: only primary channel owners can verify identity for extra features; "ID and video verification isn't available to all creators"; and after two failed ID or video attempts, you must wait 30 days.

Why verification matters for growth

Verification itself doesn't make YouTube recommend you more. What it does is unlock tools that matter for packaging and viewer experience:

  • Custom thumbnails. Without them, YouTube picks a frame for you. Packaging is a big part of whether people click; see how to make good YouTube thumbnails and what size a YouTube thumbnail should be.
  • Longer videos. Needed for tutorials, podcasts and deep dives over 15 minutes.
  • Live streaming. For Q&As and premieres-style events.
  • Pinned comments (advanced). Useful for links, corrections and starting discussion.

Setting up your channel basics at the same time helps: a clear handle, a properly sized banner and a good description.

When to verify

There is no reason to wait. Verifying on the day you create the channel means your very first uploads can have custom thumbnails, and you won't have to split a long video in two. It also starts the clock on building the channel history that leads to advanced features. If you manage several channels, plan which phone numbers you'll use, because of the two-channels-per-year limit.

FAQ

Is there a free tool that roasts my YouTube channel's packaging and tells me what to fix?

Yes. Asendify's free YouTube Channel Roast scores your latest public uploads out of 100 and gives three fixes, with no sign-up. YouTube Studio analytics are a fair free alternative.

Do I need to verify before I can upload?

No. Standard features, available when you create a channel, let you upload videos; verification unlocks extras such as longer uploads and custom thumbnails.

How long can videos be without verification?

Up to 15 minutes. Verifying your phone unlocks longer uploads.

Can I use one phone number for several channels?

Only two channels per phone number per year.

How do I get pinned comments?

They're an advanced feature: verify your phone, then build channel history or verify your identity with an ID or video if offered.

Can I lose verified features?

Yes. Community Guidelines violations can delay or remove access to features.
