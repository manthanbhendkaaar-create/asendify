Can Claude Cowork keep working when your computer is off, and can you use it on your phone?

By Manthan Bhendkar · 6 min read · Published 19 November 2026 · Last updated 19 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

Yes, mostly. Claude Cowork can run tasks in the cloud on Anthropic's servers, so a task keeps working when your laptop is closed and you can start, steer and review it from the web or the Claude mobile app. Scheduled tasks can also run with no device online. The exceptions are anything that touches your own computer: local files, local MCP servers, browser use and computer use need the Claude Desktop app open and connected.

Key takeaways

  • Cowork on web and mobile launched on 7 July 2026, first for Max subscribers, according to TechCrunch.
  • Anthropic's help centre now lists desktop on all paid plans, and web and mobile on Pro, Max, Ultra and Team (beta on Team); Enterprise needs an owner to enable it.
  • Cloud sessions keep running with your computer closed, but cannot reach it until the desktop app is open again.
  • A session stays either cloud or local once started; to switch, start a new session.
  • Files Claude delivers are saved; working files it does not hand back are not kept after the session ends.

How cloud sessions work

Anthropic's help article What to expect with Claude Cowork in the cloud explains: "Claude Cowork can now run your tasks in the cloud, on Anthropic's servers instead of your computer." Connectors stay linked across sessions and devices, and files Claude delivers are saved to your account.

Its companion article, Use Claude Cowork on web, desktop and mobile, adds that "your sessions and files live with your Claude account and go where you go, on any device", that work continues when your laptop is closed, and that scheduled tasks run without a device online. You get a phone notification when Claude finishes or needs your input.

On Pro, Max and Ultra, new sessions now run in the cloud on every surface. The article says the "Only on your computer" setting was removed for those plans on 6 October 2026; tasks started locally before then stay local until they finish.

What still needs your computer

The cloud article lists four things that need Claude Desktop open and connected, even in a cloud session:

  1. Local files and folders. Claude can only reach folders you have connected, and only while the app is open.
  2. Local MCP servers, including plugins that bundle them.
  3. Browser use through Claude in Chrome.
  4. Computer use, where Claude controls your screen (Pro, Max and Ultra).

If the app closes, the cloud session keeps running but cannot reach your computer. Files on your computer must be attached manually to a cloud task. Scheduled tasks created in the cloud cannot use local folders or local connectors.

What people use it for

When Cowork reached web and mobile, TechCrunch's Rebecca Bellan reported Anthropic's example: a user schedules "Monday's client prep" for 6am, and Claude gathers email threads, transcripts and news, builds a briefing document and leaves a follow-up email drafted but unsent for review. Anthropic also shared data from 1.2 million anonymised sessions: business process operations, such as consolidating updates into reports or reconciling spreadsheets, made up 33.4%, content creation 16.4% and software development 8.7%. TechCrunch noted these figures came from Anthropic and were not independently verified.

How to turn cloud mode on or off

The cloud article gives two routes: click the "Beta" button in the top right when starting a new session, or go to Settings > Cowork and toggle "Run new tasks in the cloud". On Pro, Max and Ultra, new sessions already run in the cloud. Team and Enterprise settings depend on what the organisation's owner has enabled.

Worked example

Example: Tom runs a two-person accounting practice. Every Monday he wants a summary of last week's client emails and a list of overdue invoices.

  1. Setup on desktop: he connects his email and accounting tools as connectors and writes a scheduled Cowork task for 7am each Monday.
  2. Cloud run: the task is created in the cloud, so it runs at 7am even though his laptop is shut in his bag.
  3. On his phone: at 7:40 a notification says the summary is ready. He opens it in the Claude app, asks Claude to draft polite reminder emails for the three most overdue invoices, and reviews them before sending.
  4. The limit he hits: he also wants Claude to file the summary in a folder on his laptop. That needs local file access, so he does it when he opens the desktop app at the office, or asks Claude to deliver the file so it is saved to his account instead.

FAQ

Is there a course that shows how a real business sets up scheduled Cowork tasks and safeguards?

Yes. The Cowork Operator by Asendify documents one shop's real rules, connected apps, scheduled tasks and safeguards. It is independent of Anthropic; the help articles are free.

Can I use Claude Cowork on my phone?

Yes, on Pro, Max, Ultra and Team (beta), and on Enterprise if an owner enables it. You can start, steer and review tasks in the Claude mobile app.

Do scheduled tasks run if my laptop is off?

Cloud scheduled tasks do. Tasks that need local folders or local connectors cannot run in the cloud.

Can Claude read files on my computer from my phone?

Only while the Claude Desktop app is open and connected on that computer.

Are my files kept after a cloud session?

Files Claude delivers are saved. Working files it does not hand back are not kept after the session ends.

Can I move a task from local to cloud?

No. A session stays cloud or local once it starts. Start a new session to switch.
