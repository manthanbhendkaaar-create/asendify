YouTube growth: books, courses, tools and free resources

By Manthan Bhendkar · 4 min read · Published 21 October 2026 · Last updated 29 October 2026

Growing a YouTube channel comes down to two things you can work on: getting the right people to click (titles, thumbnails, topics) and keeping them watching (hooks, structure, pacing). This hub lists the resources that help with each, starting with the free ones, and says plainly who each paid option is for.

Disclosure: Asendify makes several of the resources below (a book, a course, an agency, an AI tool and free tools). They are ours. Free resources by YouTube and others are listed first because most creators should start there.

What this category covers

  • Retention: why viewers leave and how to edit so they stay.
  • Packaging: titles, thumbnails and upload consistency.
  • Analytics: reading YouTube Studio and turning numbers into decisions.
  • Help with the work: learning it yourself, getting feedback, or hiring an editor.

Start here: the best free resources (not ours)

  • YouTube Help: audience retention. YouTube's own guide to the retention report, including the intro (share still watching after 30 seconds), top moments, spikes and dips, and how to compare with typical retention (YouTube Help). If you read one page, read this one.
  • YouTube Creators. YouTube's official creator site with guides on getting started, growing, and how things work.
  • YouTube Studio. Your own analytics are free and are the only source of your real retention, traffic and click-through data.
  • Your own best videos. Re-watching your top-retaining videos next to your weakest ones is free and often the most useful lesson.

Free tools from Asendify

  • YouTube Channel Roast: paste a channel link and get a score out of 100 for titles, thumbnails and upload consistency, blunt roasts and 3 fixes. No sign-up, 3 roasts a day. It uses public data only, so it cannot judge hooks or retention.
  • YouTube Hook Checker: paste your intro script and get a score out of 100 with fixes for the first 30 seconds: promise, proof, open loops and pacing. Text only; it cannot see your edit.
  • Retention Curve Checker: paste retention numbers to see the biggest drops with timestamps, the 30-second hold and rewatch spikes.
  • Asendify Daily: a free 2-minute daily game; one of its question types is "Would viewers keep watching or leave?".

FAQ

What is the single best free resource for YouTube growth?

YouTube's own help page on the audience retention report, used alongside your YouTube Studio data. It is official, free and specific to your videos.

Do I need a paid course to grow on YouTube?

No. Many creators grow with free resources and steady practice. A paid course mainly buys structure and feedback on your own videos.
