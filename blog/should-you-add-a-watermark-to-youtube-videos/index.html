Should you add a subscribe watermark to your YouTube videos?

By the Asendify editorial team, led by Manthan Bhendkar · 5 min read · Published 20 November 2026 · Last updated 20 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

Yes, a video watermark is worth adding for most channels because it costs nothing and works on every video at once. It is a small square image, at least 150 x 150 pixels and under 1 MB, that YouTube can show for the last 15 seconds, from a custom start time or for the whole video, as a small branded reminder of your channel. It isn't available on videos set as Made for Kids, and it complements end screens rather than replacing them.

Key takeaways

  • YouTube's branding settings let you add one watermark image across your videos.
  • Specs: at least 150 x 150 px, square, under 1 MB.
  • Display options: last 15 seconds (the default wording on YouTube's page), a custom start time, or the entire video.
  • "Video watermarks aren't available on videos set as Made for Kids."
  • End screens are the bigger subscribe tool: up to four elements in the last 5 to 20 seconds of videos at least 25 seconds long.

What YouTube says about watermarks

YouTube's help page on managing your channel branding lists the specifications for the video watermark alongside your profile picture and banner:

SettingDetail
SizeAt least 150 x 150 px
ShapeSquare
File sizeUnder 1 MB
When it showsLast 15 seconds, a custom start time, or the entire video
Made for Kids videosNot available

The page notes: "The video watermark will show for the last 15 seconds of the video" if you choose that option, and "Video watermarks aren't available on videos set as Made for Kids."

Watermark vs end screen vs channel trailer

ToolWhere and whenWhat it doesLimits
WatermarkCorner of the video, at your chosen timeBrands the video and reminds viewers of your channelSmall; not on Made for Kids
End screenLast 5 to 20 secondsUp to 4 elements: videos, playlists, subscribe, channel, linkVideo must be at least 25 seconds; not on Made for Kids or some players
Channel trailerYour Home tab, for non-subscribersPitches the channel to visitorsOnly seen on your channel page

YouTube's end screens help page says "End screens can be added to the last 5–20 seconds of a video", with up to four elements on standard 16:9 videos. Viewers can't see end screens on mobile web (except iPad), in the YouTube Music or YouTube Kids apps, or on 360 videos, and they can hide them. The channel layout help page explains that the channel trailer "offers a preview of your channel so that viewers can learn more and subscribe." Our page on whether a channel trailer matters covers that tool.

Designing a watermark that works

These are practical design suggestions, not YouTube rules:

  • Keep it simple. At this size, a bold icon or your logo works better than text.
  • Match your profile picture. Consistent branding helps viewers recognise you.
  • Check contrast. It sits over your video, so it needs to stand out on light and dark scenes.
  • Use a transparent PNG if your logo isn't square, so it doesn't appear as a block.
  • Avoid clashing with on-screen text. Keep captions and graphics away from the corner where it appears.

Which display option to choose

OptionBest forDownside
Last 15 secondsMost channels; asks for the subscribe after value is deliveredMissed by viewers who leave earlier
Custom start timeVideos with a natural midpoint, like after the main tipNeeds a time that suits most videos
Entire videoStrong, recognisable brandsCan distract or cover on-screen elements

Since the watermark applies across your videos, choose an option that works for your typical upload, not your exceptions.

Worked example: a tutorial channel

Example: Grace runs a spreadsheet-tips channel. Her videos average 8 minutes, and many viewers leave once they've got the tip, around halfway.

DecisionChoiceReason
ImageHer green logo icon, 300 x 300 px PNG, 120 KBSquare, above 150 x 150, under 1 MB
Display timeCustom start at 3 minutesMost viewers are still watching then
End screenSubscribe element plus her best related videoCatches those who stay to the end
Made for Kids videosNone on her channelWatermark works on all her uploads

She adds the watermark once in YouTube Studio's branding settings, and it appears across her videos. Because many viewers leave before the end, a mid-video watermark gives them a subscribe option they wouldn't otherwise see, while the end screen serves those who finish. She reviews her subscriber numbers in YouTube Studio after a couple of months rather than expecting an instant jump.

FAQ

Yes. Asendify's free YouTube Channel Roast scores your latest public uploads out of 100 and gives three fixes, with no sign-up. YouTube Studio analytics are a fair free alternative.

What size should a YouTube watermark be?

At least 150 x 150 pixels, square, and under 1 MB.

How long does a YouTube watermark show?

You can choose the last 15 seconds, a custom start time or the entire video.

Why can't I add a watermark to some videos?

Watermarks aren't available on videos set as Made for Kids.

Is a watermark better than an end screen?

They do different jobs. A watermark can appear earlier in the video; an end screen offers up to four elements in the last 5 to 20 seconds. Many channels use both.

Does a watermark show on mobile?

Visibility can vary by device and player. Check how your videos look on the devices your viewers use.
