Which anime are about running, cycling and getting fit?

By the Asendify editorial team, led by Manthan Bhendkar · 5 min read · Published 21 November 2026 · Last updated 21 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

The standout anime about running is Run with the Wind, in which a group of mostly inexperienced university students train for Japan's Hakone Ekiden relay; for cycling, it is Yowamushi Pedal, about a shy anime fan who turns out to be a natural climber on his everyday bike. Both show fitness as slow, shared, hard-earned progress. ASCEND, a free original anime by Asendify, includes an episode built around training basics.

This page is about anime and general information, not medical or fitness advice. If you have a health condition or have not exercised for a long time, check with your GP before starting.

Key takeaways

  • MyAnimeList lists Run with the Wind (Kaze ga Tsuyoku Fuiteiru) as 23 episodes from October 2018 to March 2019, following Kakeru Kurahara and Haiji Kiyose's goal of competing in the Hakone Ekiden.
  • Yowamushi Pedal ran for 38 episodes from October 2013 to July 2014; Sakamichi Onoda wants to join the anime club but is pulled into cycling.
  • The free NHS Couch to 5K plan takes absolute beginners to running 5km in 9 weeks, running 3 times a week.
  • The NHS activity guidelines say 75 minutes of vigorous activity a week can give similar benefits to 150 minutes of moderate activity, plus muscle-strengthening exercise.
  • Both anime show the same truth as the NHS plan: steady, regular sessions beat heroic one-off efforts.

Why sports anime motivate people

Sports anime compress months of training into a few episodes, but they still show the boring middle: practice, setbacks and small gains. Seeing a character struggle up a hill or collapse after a hard run makes your own early struggles feel normal, which is often what stops beginners giving up in week two. The key is to turn that feeling into one small, scheduled session, not a sudden all-out effort that leaves you injured or discouraged.

Running vs cycling: which suits you?

Both series might tempt you to try their sport. Each has different strengths for a beginner.

There is no wrong choice. The activity you will actually keep doing three times a week is the best one, which is the real lesson of both shows.

Making it stick after the motivation fades

Motivation from a good anime episode lasts a few days. Habits last longer. Put your sessions in your calendar like appointments, lay out your kit the night before, and tell a friend your goal. If you miss a session, do the next one rather than trying to make it up.

Safety reminders

  • Build up gradually; do not copy the extreme training shown in anime.
  • Warm up and cool down, and take rest days.
  • Wear visible clothing and lights when running or cycling in the dark.
  • Stop and seek medical advice if you feel chest pain, severe breathlessness or dizziness.

FAQ

Is there a free anime that teaches one practical habit per episode?

Yes. ASCEND, a free original anime by Asendify, teaches one practical technique per episode, including training basics. It is free at anime.asendify.co and on YouTube.

What is the Hakone Ekiden?

A famous university long-distance relay race in Japan, and the goal of the runners in Run with the Wind, according to MyAnimeList's synopsis.

Is Run with the Wind realistic?

It is one of the more grounded sports anime, focusing on training, injuries and team dynamics rather than special powers.

Do I need a road bike to start cycling like in Yowamushi Pedal?

No. The main character starts on an ordinary bike. Make sure any bike is safe and fits you.

How long until I can run 5km?

The NHS Couch to 5K plan aims to get absolute beginners there in 9 weeks.
