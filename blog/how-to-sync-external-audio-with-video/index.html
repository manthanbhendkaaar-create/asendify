How do you sync a separate mic recording with your video?

By the Asendify editorial team, led by Manthan Bhendkar · 5 min read · Published 21 November 2026 · Last updated 21 November 2026

Disclosure: Asendify publishes this page and makes some of the options mentioned here; ours are named as ours. Every option is described from its own official page, and prices and features can change, so check before you buy.

To sync audio from a separate mic with your camera footage, record a clear sync sound at the start of each take (a clap works), keep the camera's own audio as a guide, then let your editor match the waveforms automatically: Merge Clips or Synchronize in Premiere Pro, Synchronize Clips in Final Cut Pro, or Auto Sync Audio based on waveform in DaVinci Resolve. Then mute or remove the camera audio so only the better recording plays.

Key takeaways

  • Waveform syncing compares the camera's scratch audio with the separate recording, so always record audio on the camera too.
  • Adobe says audio alignment works best with a distinct shared sound such as a clapper, and can fail with heavy wind noise.
  • Final Cut Pro looks for sync points such as markers, timecode, file creation date and audio content, and "the original clips are not affected."
  • In Resolve, choose Auto Sync Audio and Waveform, then decide whether to keep the camera's embedded audio.
  • After syncing, disable the camera audio so only the cleaner mic recording is heard.

Why separate audio needs syncing

Many YouTubers record voice on a lavalier, a portable recorder or a USB mic for better sound than the camera's built-in mic. That leaves two files per take: video with rough camera audio, and good audio with no video. Your editor needs to line them up exactly, or lips and words drift apart. Doing it by eye is slow; every major editor can do it automatically by matching the sound waves.

Premiere Pro

Adobe's tutorial on syncing audio by waveform gives two main methods:

Merge Clips (in the Project panel)

  1. Select the video clip and the audio clip.
  2. Right-click and choose Merge Clips.
  3. Name the clip, choose the audio alignment option and optionally remove the camera's audio.
  4. Click OK. Premiere creates a linked clip without writing a new file.

Synchronize (in the timeline)

  1. Put the clips on separate tracks.
  2. Select only the audio parts (Alt/Option-click), then right-click and choose Synchronize > Audio.

If you select video too, the Audio option disappears. Adobe also shows a multi-camera route: Create Multi-Camera Source Sequence, syncing by audio. If syncing fails, undo and align manually using the clap.

Final Cut Pro

Apple's guide to syncing audio and video:

  1. Select the clips in the browser.
  2. Choose Clip > Synchronize Clips (Option-Command-G).
  3. Name the synchronized clip and choose an event.
  4. Tick Use audio for synchronization.
  5. Optionally tick Disable audio components on AV clips to mute the camera's audio.
  6. Click OK.

Final Cut analyses markers, timecode, file creation dates and audio content; if it finds no sync points, it lines clips up at their start.

DaVinci Resolve

A Moody College of Communication guide (University of Texas wiki, 2024) sets out:

  1. Put matching camera and sound clips in a bin.
  2. Note each clip's audio tracks in Clip Attributes > Audio, because track numbers change after syncing.
  3. Select the matched clips, right-click and choose Auto Sync Audio, then Waveform.
  4. Decide whether to keep the camera's embedded audio, then click Sync.
  5. Check Clip Attributes to confirm; the guide warns "Nothing much will seem to have happened" on screen.

Why do I need camera audio if I have a separate mic?

The editor matches the camera's rough audio with the mic recording to line them up.

Do I need a clapperboard?

No. A sharp hand clap on camera works for most YouTube shoots.

Why won't Premiere sync my clips?

Select only the audio parts when using Synchronize, make sure there's a clear shared sound, and check for wind noise.

Does syncing change my original files?

In Final Cut, the original clips aren't affected; Premiere's Merge Clips creates a linked clip without writing a new file.

How do I stop the echo after syncing?

Mute or remove the camera's audio so only the mic recording plays.
