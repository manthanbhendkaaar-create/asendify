      How the score works

      Titles · 40%

      Curiosity, clarity, length and whether a stranger would click.

      Thumbnails · 40%

      Readable at phone size, one clear idea, contrast and text.

      Consistency · 20%

      How often and how regularly you've uploaded lately.

      Everything it checks, by name

      What it reads
      • Your public channel page: channel name, handle, subscriber count and profile picture
      • Your latest 15 public uploads from YouTube's public feed: title, upload date, view count and whether it's a Short
      • The thumbnails of your 8 most recent uploads
      Titles (40% of the score)
      • Curiosity
      • Clarity
      • Specificity
      • Length
      • Clickability: would a stranger click?
      Thumbnails (40%)
      • Readability on a phone
      • Contrast
      • Focal point
      • Amount of text
      • Consistency across your uploads
      • Emotion
      Consistency (20%, worked out from your upload dates)
      • Uploads in the last 30 days (about 11 a month gets full marks)
      • Days since your last upload (points come off after 14 quiet days)
      • Average gap between uploads
      • Longest gap between uploads (a gap over 14 days and 4 times your usual gap costs points)
      What you get back
      • Overall score out of 100, plus separate title, thumbnail and consistency scores
      • A one-line verdict
      • 5 one-line roasts
      • 3 fixes you can do this week
      • Your best and worst title, and a rewrite of the worst one
      • A share link for your roast
      What it doesn't check
      • Spoken hooks, audio or editing inside the video
      • Your retention graph or any private analytics
      • Private or unlisted videos
      • Your looks, voice or identity: it roasts packaging choices only

      A channel needs at least 3 public uploads to be roasted.

      Questions

      What does it check?

      Your latest public uploads: titles, thumbnails and how regularly you post.

      Does it judge hooks or retention?

      No. YouTube doesn't let outside tools read a video's audio or retention graph, so the roast only covers what's public.

      Will it make fun of how I look?

      No. It roasts packaging choices only, never anyone's face, body, voice or identity.

      Is it really free?

      Yes. No sign-up, no card. 3 roasts per person per day. A channel roasted in the last 24 hours shows the same result.
