About This Service

Talking head video editing for YouTube

Asendify edits talking head videos so to-camera content stays dynamic: tight cuts, well-timed B-roll and graphics, and structure that keeps viewers listening.

We build each edit around your delivery, removing dead air and repetition while keeping the energy and personality that make your audience connect with you.

Who it's for

Educators, coaches, commentators and business creators whose videos are built around them speaking to camera.

How we work

Every channel we take on runs through the same four-stage retention system:

  1. Retention diagnostic. We audit your existing uploads frame by frame against the audience-retention curve to find exactly where viewers drop off.
  2. Editing system overhaul. Pacing, cut density and visual language are rebuilt around what the data says keeps your viewers watching.
  3. Hook and structure. We re-engineer the opening seconds and the mid-video structure, then test and iterate against real watch time.
  4. Consistency and compounding. A steady publishing cadence, plus thumbnail and title testing, lets the algorithm compound the gains over time.

We only take on five clients at a time, and each one gets a dedicated editor. Nothing is templated or outsourced.
