What's inside

Four tools, one private page, saved as you type

Everything inside, by name: target and timeline, buying outright vs spreading the cost, what your budget really buys, the income plan, editable running costs and about 40 cars. Everything recalculates the moment you change a number; replace any estimate with a real quote.

Target and timeline

Months to the car at your saving rate and return, a savings chart against the goal, and the exact monthly amount for any deadline. The goal includes a first-year running-cost reserve, so you are not broke the day after you buy.

Buying outright vs spreading the cost

Buying outright and the common ways of spreading the cost, side by side: up front, per month, and the true cost over the years you keep the car, counting depreciation, interest, the money cash would have earned, and what the car is still worth.

What your budget really buys

Every car in the list a used example fits your budget, ranked by what it costs you per year to own, not by sticker price. Depreciation, insurance, servicing, tyres, tax and fuel at your mileage.

Income plan

Fill in your take-home pay, skills, hours and situation. The planner writes 3 to 5 realistic ways to earn the monthly gap using what you already know, with first steps for this week, conservative ranges, honest risks and a 90-day plan.

Running costs you can edit

Typical insurance, servicing, tyres, road tax and fuel or electricity for each car, in your currency. Type in your own quotes and every tab updates.

About 40 cars, or your own

From an Alpine A110 to a Bugatti Chiron, with typical UK new and used prices and depreciation. Planning for a car we do not list? Enter its price and the planner does the rest.

FAQ

Questions people ask

What is the Supercar Planner?

A private web page, built for one car you choose, that works out when you can afford it at your saving rate, how much you need to save a month to hit a deadline, the real cost of buying outright versus spreading the cost over the years you keep it, which cars your budget really buys when running costs are included, and an AI-written plan of realistic ways to earn the extra income that closes the gap. It saves your numbers so you can come back any time.

How much does it cost?

£435 one-time (£300 + 45% service fee). No subscription. Card checkout (Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Apple Pay, Google Pay) or PayPal.

Which cars are in the planner?

About 40 sports cars, supercars, super-SUVs, electric performance cars and hypercars from Alpine, Porsche, BMW, Toyota, Ford, Lotus, Chevrolet, Jaguar, Nissan, Honda, Audi, McLaren, Bentley, Maserati, Mercedes-AMG, Aston Martin, Lamborghini, Ferrari and Bugatti, each with typical UK new and used prices, depreciation and running costs. You can also enter any other car with its price.

Are the prices and costs real?

They are UK planning estimates for a typical example of each car (October 2026), not quotes. Every number can be replaced with your own quote, and the planner recalculates. It is not financial advice.

Does it work outside the UK?

Yes. Pick your currency (GBP, USD, EUR, AED, INR) and the planner converts the car figures at a rate you can edit. The cost maths and the income plan work for any country; local tax, insurance and payment terms are yours to check.

What is the income plan?

After you fill in your take-home income, skills, hours and situation, the planner writes a plan with 3 to 5 realistic extra-income routes based on what you already know how to do, with first steps, conservative monthly ranges, risks and a 90-day plan. Honest ranges, no promises, no get-rich schemes. You can rewrite it up to 3 times per planner.

Is there a guarantee?

Double money-back guarantee: follow the planner for 60 days (your numbers filled in, the income plan's first steps done). If you are not measurably closer to the car (more saved, a higher saving rate or more income), we refund twice what you paid. Claim within 90 days of purchase, one claim per person.
