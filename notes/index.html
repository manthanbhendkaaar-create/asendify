  • Filters, sorts and group by: set separately for each view

  • Linked databases: show a database inside another page

  • Comments: comment on pages and mark comments as resolved

  • Read-only public link: anyone with the link can read the page and the pages inside it; turn it off any time
  • View-only guests: invite up to 50 people by email to read a page, free
  • Export everything: Markdown + CSV (zip) or JSON, at any time
  • Trash with 30-day restore: deleted pages wait 30 days before they are gone for good

Who it's for, and who it's not for

Asendify Notes is for
  • Freelancers, founders and consultants who want one private place for notes, docs, tasks and client work.
  • People who like the Notion way of working (pages, blocks, databases) and want it in a simpler, private workspace.
  • Anyone who shares finished pages with clients or family as read-only links or view-only guests.
  • People who want to keep work, clients and personal life in separate workspaces.
It's not for
  • Teams who need several people editing the same workspace: there is one owner per account, and guests can only read.
  • Anyone looking for a free plan or a free trial: there isn't one.
  • People who need installable desktop or mobile apps or offline use today: it runs in the browser, and apps are on the roadmap.
  • Teams that need integrations or an API to connect other tools: Asendify Notes doesn't offer them.

Questions

Is Asendify Notes like Notion?

It works in a similar way: pages inside pages, a block editor with slash commands, and databases you can view as a table or a board. It is a separate product made by Asendify, not affiliated with Notion.

Does it renew automatically?

Only if you choose. Paying by card, you can tick auto-renew: we then charge £1,450 to the same card on the same date each year, and email you 14 and 3 days before. Turn it off any time in Settings and nothing more is charged. PayPal payments are one year at a time.

Why £1,450 a year?

It's £1,000 plus a 45% service fee, shown in full before you pay. You get one private workspace with unlimited pages. Nothing is added at checkout.

Is there a free trial?

No. Asendify Notes is paid from the start.

Can I get my notes out?

Yes, any time: Markdown for a page, JSON for everything. If your year ends you can still export for 30 days.

Can I share a page?

Yes, with a read-only link. People can read the page and the pages inside it, not edit. You can turn it off whenever you like.

What happens when my year ends?

With auto-renew on, a new year starts on that date. Otherwise editing stops until you renew. Your notes are kept, and you can export them for 30 days.

Are there apps for Windows, Mac or Android?

Asendify Notes runs in any modern browser on all of them today. Installable apps are on the roadmap but not available yet.

How much does Asendify Notes cost?

£1,450 a year: £1,000 plus a 45% service fee of £450, shown in full before you pay. There is no free plan and no free trial. If you pay by card you can choose auto-renew (£1,450 on the same date each year, turn it off any time); we email you 14 and 3 days before any renewal.

Do I need a password?

No. You sign in with a one-time link sent to your email.

Can other people edit my pages?

No. Each account has one owner who edits. You can invite up to 50 guests by email to read a page (free, view only) or make a read-only public link.

Can I move my notes over from Notion?

Notion can export pages as Markdown and databases as CSV files. Asendify Notes imports both: a Markdown file becomes a page and a CSV file becomes a database.
