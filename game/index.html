    About Asendify Daily

    A free 2-minute daily challenge: 10 quick calls on money, business, YouTube, people and habits. Same 10 for everyone, a new game every day.

    Every feature, by name

    • A new game every day: a numbered daily game (#1, #2, …) that changes at midnight in your time zone
    • 10 quick calls: about 2 minutes from start to score
    • Same 10 for everyone: everyone gets the same questions on the same day, so scores compare fairly
    • 6 kinds of question: Red flag or green flag?, Smart move or mistake?, Would viewers keep watching or leave?, Good deal or money pit?, Gets a reply or ignored?, Myth or fact?
    • 23 topics: YouTube retention, AI for business, Sellable business, Property, Dubai money myths, Influencer advice, Online business money, Problem solving, Critical thinking, Habits & focus, People red flags, Staying safe, Breakups, Confidence, Life story writing, Mindset, Trading basics, Glow-up habits, Caffeine, Cold email, Roofing websites, Agency automation, Video editing
    • A 146-question bank: questions don't repeat until most of the bank has been used
    • Mixed topics: the same topic doesn't come up twice in a row when it can be avoided
    • Tap to answer: two big buttons under every card
    • Swipe to answer: swipe the card left or right on a touch screen
    • Arrow keys: ← and → to answer, → or Enter for the next question
    • Instant feedback: see straight away whether your call was right and what the right answer is
    • Plain-English explanation: a short reason behind every answer
    • Go deeper: each answer links to the Asendify book or product on that topic
    • Progress bar: a green or red mark for each question you've answered
    • Pick up where you left off: leave mid-game and continue today's game from the same question
    • Score out of 10: with a one-line verdict on your day
    • Daily streak: grows each day you finish the daily game
    • Best streak: your longest run so far
    • Next-game countdown: a live timer to the next daily game
    • Share your result grid: a 🟩🟥 grid with your score and a link, shared or copied in one tap
    • Challenge a friend: send a link with your score and see if they can beat it
    • Head-to-head result: when a friend's challenge is open, see who won, or a tie, and send a rematch
    • Weakest topic: the end screen names your weakest topic and where to learn more
    • Review today's answers: every question again with the right answer and the explanation
    • Practice rounds: unlimited rounds of 10 random questions that don't touch your streak
    • Daily answers page: every finished day explained at game.asendify.co/answers/
    • No sign-up: your streak and progress are saved in this browser only
    • Reduced motion: card animations switch off if your device asks for less motion
    • Screen reader support: each question and result is announced
    • Free: no payment, no account

    Who it's for, and who it's not for

    Asendify Daily is for
    • Anyone who likes a quick daily brain game they can finish in about 2 minutes.
    • People who want to sharpen their instincts on money, business, YouTube, people and habits, one short explanation at a time.
    • Friends, families and teams who like comparing scores and keeping a streak going.
    It's not for
    • Anyone looking for in-depth lessons: each answer is one short explanation, and the linked books go deeper.

    • Anyone who wants long play sessions on one topic: the daily game is 10 mixed questions a day.

    Frequently asked questions

    Is Asendify Daily free?

    Yes. It's free to play every day, with no sign-up and no account.

    How does it work?

    You get 10 quick scenarios on money, business, YouTube, people and habits. For each one you make a call, such as red flag or green flag, or myth or fact, then see the right answer and a short explanation. It takes about 2 minutes.

    How do I answer?

    Tap one of the two buttons, swipe the card left or right, or use your ← → arrow keys.

    When is there a new game?

    Every day at midnight in your time zone. The end screen counts down to the next game.

    Does everyone get the same questions?

    Yes. Everyone gets the same 10 questions on the same day, so you can compare scores and challenge friends.

    How does the streak work?

    Finish the daily game on consecutive days to grow your streak. Best shows your longest streak. Practice rounds don't count towards it.

    How do I challenge a friend?

    After the daily game, tap Challenge a friend to send a link with your score. When they finish the same day's game, they see who won and can send a rematch.

    Can I play more than once a day?

    The daily game is once a day, but you can play as many practice rounds of 10 random questions as you like.

    Where can I see past answers?

    On the answers page, game.asendify.co/answers/, where every finished day is explained, newest first.

    Do I need an account?

    No. Your streak and today's progress are saved in this browser only, so they don't carry over to another device or browser.

    Is this advice?

    No. Asendify Daily is just for fun and learning. It isn't financial, legal or medical advice.
