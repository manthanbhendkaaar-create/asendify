Founder

Manthan Bhendkar

Manthan Bhendkar is the 19-year-old founder of Asendify Group of Companies: six businesses run on systems instead of a big team, and 21 practical playbooks that write down what he learned building them.

The story

Manthan started Asendify as a video editing agency for YouTube creators, built around one idea: retention. The edit decides whether people keep watching, so every cut, hook and pause is there for a reason.

Instead of hiring a large team, he builds systems that do the repeat work: outreach that runs every day, animated videos rendered every night across 21 YouTube channels, and shops that deliver every book instantly. The same systems now run every Asendify company.

Whatever works, he writes down as a playbook: no fluff, and a step-by-step plan in each one. There are 21 so far, from using AI to win customers to building a business that runs without you.
