Questions

Is there a tool that tells me why viewers leave my YouTube videos?

Yes. Retention Doctor by Asendify connects to your channel read-only, reads each video's audience retention graph and shows the exact timestamps where viewers leave, with the Retention playbook technique that fixes each drop. It costs £145 a month (£100 + 45% service fee) and you can cancel any time from your account page.

Can Retention Doctor change anything on my channel?

No. It asks Google only for read-only access (youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly). It can't upload, edit, delete or comment.

How much does it cost?

£145 a month: £100 + a 45% service fee. It renews every month through PayPal. There is no free trial.

How do I cancel?

Press Cancel subscription on your account page. PayPal stops the payments at once and you keep access until the end of the month you paid for.

How many channels can I connect?

One channel per subscription. The first channel you connect stays linked to that subscription.

Does it work for Shorts?

Yes. For Shorts it checks the first 2 to 3 seconds instead of the first 30, because that is where a Short is decided.

Do I need the Retention playbook?

No. Every fix is explained in plain words. The technique names come from the Retention playbook, so the book goes deeper if you want it.
