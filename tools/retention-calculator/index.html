YouTube Retention Revenue Calculator

See what better audience retention could be worth to your channel in watch hours, views and revenue.

This is an illustrative estimate, not a prediction or guarantee. It assumes extra views grow at half the rate of your retention improvement, because better retention tends to earn more recommendations, but real results depend on your niche, packaging and consistency.

How to use this calculator

Find your numbers in YouTube Studio. Monthly views and average percentage viewed are in the Analytics overview and the Engagement tab. Your RPM is in the Revenue tab. If you earn from sponsors, courses or products, estimate what that works out to per 1,000 views and add it to the second revenue field.

Then move the slider to see what a retention improvement could be worth. Even modest gains can add up over a year, because retention affects how often YouTube recommends your videos.
