What coaching is

Coaching is three months of personal guidance on growing your YouTube channel through better retention. You work directly with us, 1:1 or in a small group of up to a few creators, and we diagnose your channel, plan the changes, and review your videos as you make them.

It's the middle ground between learning on your own and hiring an agency: you keep control of your content and your editing, and we make sure every decision is backed by your retention data.

Who it's for

  • Creators who are posting consistently but whose views and watch time have stalled.
  • Channels that want an expert's eyes on every upload without handing over editing.
  • Finance, education and business creators whose content is dense and needs to be made easy to follow.
  • Firms and founders building a YouTube channel for credibility and deal flow.

How the three months work

  1. Month 1: Diagnose and rebuild. A full retention diagnostic of your recent videos, then a new plan for hooks, structure and pacing.
  2. Month 2: Apply and review. You publish with the new system and we review each video, pointing out exactly where viewers leave and why.
  3. Month 3: Compound. Lock in a repeatable process, refine titles and thumbnails, and set targets for the next quarter.

What you get

  • Regular coaching calls, 1:1 or small group
  • Written feedback on your videos between calls
  • A retention diagnostic of your channel at the start and end
  • Our checklists, templates and the book, Secrets to become a celebrity on Youtube

See how the same system worked for Imperial (200K to 500K subscribers in 8 months) and MegaloMedia (50K to 243K in 7 months).
