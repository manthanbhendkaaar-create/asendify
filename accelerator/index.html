What the Accelerator is

The Retention Editing Accelerator is a six-week cohort for YouTubers and video editors who want to keep viewers watching longer. Instead of reading about retention on your own, you work through our system step by step, apply it to your own videos every week, and get direct feedback on what to change.

It's built on the same four-stage system we use with our agency clients: a retention diagnostic, an editing system overhaul, a hook and structure redesign, and consistency so the improvements compound. It's the system behind results like Imperial, a finance channel that grew from 200K to 500K subscribers in 8 months.

Who it's for

  • Creators who edit their own videos, or work closely with an editor, and see viewers dropping off early.
  • Video editors who want to offer retention-focused editing and charge more for it.
  • Channels between roughly 1K and 500K subscribers that have good ideas but inconsistent watch time.

If you'd rather have us handle everything, the done-for-you agency service is a better fit.

The six weeks

  1. Week 1: Retention diagnostic. Learn to read your retention graph second by second and find the exact moments viewers leave in your last five videos.
  2. Week 2: Hooks and the first 30 seconds. Rebuild your openings: payoff first, clear stakes, open loops. You re-cut one of your own intros.
  3. Week 3: Structure and open loops. Plan videos in sections that keep pulling viewers forward, with chapters that support rather than interrupt.
  4. Week 4: Pacing and pattern interrupts. Cut dead air, add B-roll, on-screen text and visual resets so attention never drops in the middle.
  5. Week 5: Visualising dense ideas. Turn numbers, charts and complex explanations into visuals viewers can follow.
  6. Week 6: Consistency and compounding. Build a repeatable editing checklist and upload rhythm so every video improves on the last.
