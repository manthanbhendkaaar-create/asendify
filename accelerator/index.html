What the Accelerator is

The Retention Editing Accelerator is a self-paced online course for YouTubers and video editors who want to keep viewers watching longer. You work through our system step by step in 6 modules and 24 lessons, and apply each lesson to your own videos with checklists, templates, worked examples and an assignment.

It's built on the same four-stage system we use with our agency clients: a retention diagnostic, an editing system overhaul, a hook and structure redesign, and consistency so the improvements compound.

Who it's for

  • Creators who edit their own videos, or work closely with an editor, and see viewers dropping off early.
  • Video editors who want to offer retention-focused editing and charge more for it.
  • Channels between roughly 1K and 500K subscribers that have good ideas but inconsistent watch time.

If you'd rather have us handle everything, the done-for-you agency service is a better fit.

The six modules

  1. Module 1: Retention diagnostic. Learn to read your retention graph second by second and find the exact moments viewers leave in your last five videos.
  2. Module 2: The first 30 seconds. Rebuild your openings: payoff first, clear stakes, open loops. You re-cut one of your own intros.
  3. Module 3: Structure and open loops. Plan videos in sections that keep pulling viewers forward, with chapters that support rather than interrupt.
  4. Module 4: Pacing and pattern interrupts. Cut dead air, add B-roll, on-screen text and visual resets so attention never drops in the middle.
  5. Module 5: Editing system and checklists. Build a repeatable editing system and checklist so every video gets the same retention passes.
  6. Module 6: Compounding results. Set an upload rhythm and a way to review each video, so every video improves on the last.

What's inside: the techniques by name
