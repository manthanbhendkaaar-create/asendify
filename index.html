Questions

Frequently asked questions

What does Asendify do?

Asendify is a YouTube growth agency for creators serious about turning views into revenue. We edit for retention: every video gets a dedicated editor who knows your channel inside out, cutting frame by frame until every second earns its place.

How many clients do you take on?

Only 5 at a time. That's not a limitation, it's the whole point: fewer clients, more obsession.

Can I join right now?

Both partnership tiers are full right now. Join the waitlist for free and we'll contact you, in order, when a place opens. To be offered a place first, you can reserve with a £600 refundable deposit after joining.

What's the difference between the Elite Club and the Growth Plan?

The Elite Club adds dedicated editor(s) assigned to your channel, unlimited revisions, fast turnaround built around your upload schedule and a private community of 5 high-net-worth creators. The Growth Plan gives you retention-focused editing from the Asendify team with the same hook, pacing and structure system, for growing channels that aren't ready for the Elite Club yet.

How many videos do you edit?

Unlimited edited videos, Shorts or long-form, made one at a time (fair-use limit applies).

What kinds of video do you edit?

Long-form YouTube, Shorts, Reels and TikTok, podcasts, talking-head videos, trading and finance, gaming, vlogs, AI video content, comic-style explainers, and 2D and 3D animation.

Is the editing templated or outsourced?

No. Nothing here is templated, automated or outsourced. Every video gets a dedicated editor who knows your channel inside out.

An add-on that upgrades a standard edit (+40% cost), engineered using cognitive retention principles to flatten viewer drop-off.
